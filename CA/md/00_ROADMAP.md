# CA Roadmap — Direct Entry, Full-Time, Fastest Path

> Your profile: **BTech (≥60%) + MBA Finance** → qualifies for **Direct Entry**. You **skip CA Foundation** and start at **CA Intermediate**. Plan follows the **New Scheme of Education & Training (2024)**.

---

## The one thing to internalise
The **2-year articleship (practical training) is the hard floor.** Exams are not the bottleneck — training is. Even on a flawless run, qualifying takes **~3 years**. Speed comes from *clearing exams on the first attempt* so nothing delays the start of articleship.

---

## Route (8 steps)

| # | Step | Gate / rule |
|---|------|-------------|
| 1 | Register for **CA Intermediate** (both groups) on ICAI SSP portal | Direct Entry via BTech ≥60% |
| 2 | Serve **8-month study period** from registration date | Cannot sit Inter exam before this |
| 3 | Complete **ICITSS** (ITT + Orientation, ~4 weeks) | Do it during the study period |
| 4 | **Clear BOTH Groups of Intermediate** (6 papers) | New scheme: articleship starts only after this |
| 5 | Begin **2-year Articleship** | Reduced from 3 years |
| 6 | Clear **4 Self-Paced Modules** (SET A, B, C, D) | Need **>50%** in each; do during articleship |
| 7 | Complete **Advanced ICITSS** (Adv. IT + MCS) | After training, before Final exam |
| 8 | Sit **CA Final** (6 papers) | Allowed in **last 6 months** of articleship |

## Fees — rough overview (verify live on SSP)
Direct Entry students pay a combined registration that typically bundles Intermediate registration + student activity + articleship registration. Budget indicatively **₹30,000–₹35,000** for Intermediate-stage registration, plus separate fees for **ICITSS (~₹6,500–₹7,000)**, **exam fees per group**, **Final registration (~₹22,000)**, **Advanced ICITSS (~₹7,500)**, and **self-paced module assessment fees**. *These are indicative — confirm current amounts on the portal.*
