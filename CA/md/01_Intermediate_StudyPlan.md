# CA Intermediate — Full-Time Study Plan (both groups, one attempt)

**Assumptions:** full-time, ~8 hrs/day, 6 days/week; self-study on ICAI BoS material; ~8-month runway aligning with the study-period rule. Target: clear **both groups together** on the first attempt.

## The 6 papers

| Grp | Paper | Nature | Your MBA edge? |
|-----|-------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | **P1 Advanced Accounting** | Practical (problem-solving) | Partial |
| 1 | **P2 Corporate & Other Laws** | Theory / memory | No |

## Hour budget (per week, first-pass phase)
Weight harder/heavier papers more. Rough weekly split of ~48 hrs:

- Advanced Accounting — 10 hrs
- Taxation (IT + GST) — 10 hrs
- Corporate & Other Laws — 8 hrs
- Cost & Management — 6 hrs
- Audit & Ethics — 7 hrs
- FM + SM — 5 hrs (lighter for you — leverage MBA background)
- **+ daily:** 1 hr revision of *yesterday's* topics, 30 min MCQ practice

## Three phases over ~34 weeks

### Phase 1 — First pass / concept building (Weeks 1–20)
Learn every chapter once, working problems as you go. Pair a **practical** paper with a **theory** paper each day so you don't burn out.

**Suggested chapter order:**
- **Accounting:** Company accounts → Financial statements → AS (Accounting Standards, chunk them) → Special transactions (branch, departmental, partnership, ESOP, buyback).
- **Taxation — Income Tax first (5 heads → deductions → TDS → returns), then GST** (supply → ITC → registration → returns). Keep a running "amendments" note for the applicable Finance Act.
- **Law:** Companies Act 2013 (bulk) → then Other Laws (GCA, FEMA basics, Interpretation).
- **Cost:** Costing methods → Marginal/standard costing → Budgeting → Cost sheets.
- **Audit:** Nature & basic concepts → SAs (Standards on Auditing) → Company audit → Ethics.
- **FM+SM:** FM (cost of capital, capital budgeting, working capital, leverage) — fast for you → SM (theory, do near exam).

**Weekly rhythm (Phase 1):**
- Mon–Sat: 2 papers/day (one practical + one theory), ~8 hrs
- End of each week: 2-hr self-quiz on the week's chapters + update your amendment/formula sheets
- Sunday: light — MCQs, backlog, rest

### Phase 2 — Consolidation + module-wise revision (Weeks 21–29)
- Second read of all subjects, **faster** (you're revising, not learning).
- Solve **past exam papers + ICAI Suggested Answers**, chapter by chapter.
- Build one-page **summary/formula/section sheets** for every subject (these become your final-week revision).
- Start attempting **RTPs (Revision Test Papers)** for the applicable exam session.

### Phase 3 — Mocks & final revision (Weeks 30–34)
- Attempt every **MTP (Mock Test Paper)** ICAI releases for your session, **full 3-hr timed, hand-written**, then self-evaluate against Suggested Answers.
- Target **2 full mocks/week**, rotating groups.
- Final loop: revise only from your summary sheets + weak-area log.
- Last 10 days: no new topics — pure revision, MCQ drills, and presentation practice.

---

## Practice cadence (non-negotiables)
- **RTP** — do 100%, every subject, for your exam session (ICAI hints at what's important).
- **MTP** — attempt all series, timed.
- **Past papers** — last 5–6 attempts, with Suggested Answers.
- **MCQs** — daily; track accuracy; be careful (negative marking).
- **Amendments** — Tax & Law change every session; always study the *edition applicable to your attempt*.

---

## Free resources (self-study)
- **ICAI BoS Study Material** (module-wise PDFs/books) — primary source, exam is set from this.
- **ICAI BoS Knowledge Portal** — free recorded/live lectures per subject.
- **RTP / MTP / Suggested Answers / Past Papers** — all free under Students → BoS / Examination on icai.org.
- Keep a single **"weak areas" log** — every question you got wrong goes here; revise it weekly.

---

## Presentation tips (scoring, not just knowing)
- Practical papers: show **working notes**, correct format, and rounding.
- Law/Audit: quote **section numbers / SA numbers** where you can, then explain.
- Time discipline: ~1.8 min per mark. Attempt the full paper — partial answers still score.
