# CA Final — Study Plan

The last level. **6 papers, 2 groups**, 30% MCQ + 70% descriptive. You can appear in the **last 6 months of articleship**, and a **6-month study period after training** applies. Register for Final early in articleship (registration is valid 10 years).

## The 6 papers

| Grp | Paper |
|-----|-------|
| 1 | **P1 Financial Reporting** (Ind AS heavy) |
| 1 | **P2 Advanced Financial Management** (your MBA strength) |
| 1 | **P3 Advanced Auditing, Assurance & Professional Ethics** |
| 2 | **P4 Direct Tax & International Taxation** |
| 2 | **P5 Indirect Tax Laws** (GST + Customs) |
| 2 | **P6 Integrated Business Solutions** (multidisciplinary, **open-book**, case-study based) |

## Prerequisites to *sit* Final (all must be done)
- [ ] Both Intermediate groups cleared
- [ ] Articleship: at least in the last 6 months (Final attempt window)
- [ ] **All 4 self-paced modules passed** (>50% each) — see `02_SelfPaced_Modules.md`
- [ ] **Advanced ICITSS** completed (Adv. IT + Management & Communication Skills) — *after* practical training, *before* the Final exam

## Timing strategy (fastest path)
- Because articleship is full-time work, **build Final prep gradually across the 2 years**, not just at the end:
  - **Year 1 of articleship:** SET A & B modules + start **Financial Reporting** and **AFM** (concept-heavy, benefit from early exposure; AFM overlaps your MBA finance).
  - **Year 2:** taxes (DT + IDT — most amendment-sensitive, so study the edition applicable to your attempt), Audit, and practice for **Integrated Business Solutions** (case studies — practice writing, since it's open-book but time-pressured).
- **Advanced ICITSS:** schedule it so it finishes *before* your Final attempt but doesn't collide with your peak revision — ideally in a lighter stretch of year 2.
- **6-month study leave:** you're generally entitled to study leave near the end of articleship — use it for full timed revision + mocks.

## Study method (same discipline as Inter, higher depth)
- **ICAI BoS material + RTP + MTP + past papers + Suggested Answers** — all free, all essential.
- **Financial Reporting:** master Ind AS with practice problems; maintain a standards summary.
- **AFM:** leverage your finance base — focus on presentation and coverage of newer topics.
- **DT & IDT:** amendment-driven — keep a live amendments sheet for your exam edition; heavy practice.
- **Integrated Business Solutions:** practice full case studies under time; know how to *navigate* the open-book, not read it fresh in the exam.
- **Mocks:** attempt all ICAI MTPs full-length and timed in the final 6–8 weeks.

## After clearing both groups
- Complete any remaining requirement, then **apply for ICAI membership** → you're a **Chartered Accountant**. 🎓
