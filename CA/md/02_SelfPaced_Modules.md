# Self-Paced Online Modules (SET A, B, C, D)

A **new-scheme requirement** sitting between Intermediate and Final. You complete them **online, at your own pace, during articleship**, and must score **>50% in each** to be eligible for CA Final. They're open-book online assessments — not the same pressure as Inter/Final, but don't ignore them, because they *gate* your Final attempt.

## The four sets

| Set | Subject | Choice? |
|-----|---------|---------|
| **SET A** | Corporate & Economic Laws | Mandatory (fixed) |
| **SET B** | Strategic Cost & Performance Management | Mandatory (fixed) |
| **SET C** | One elective from **10** subjects | **You choose 1** |
| **SET D** | One from **4** subjects (multidisciplinary / soft skills / economics) | **You choose 1** |

## Recommended choices for your finance background
- **SET C** — pick an elective aligned with finance/where you're strong, e.g. a **Risk Management / International Taxation / Financial Markets**-type option (exact list is on the ICAI portal at enrolment). Choose the one that (a) overlaps most with a CA Final paper and (b) you find genuinely interesting — it compounds into Final prep.
- **SET D** — pick the option closest to **Economics / multidisciplinary business** given your MBA Finance base; it's the lightest lift for you.

> The exact SET C/D subject menus can be refreshed by ICAI — read the live list on the portal before locking your choice.

## When to slot them
- These are **not** on your critical path for *starting* articleship — Intermediate clearance is.
- But they **are** on the critical path for *sitting Final*. Don't leave them to the last month.
- **Plan:** knock out **SET A and SET B in the first year of articleship** (they overlap heavily with Final's law and costing papers — double benefit). Do **SET C and D in the second year**, comfortably before you register/appear for Final.

## Study approach
- Use ICAI's provided module material for each SET (available once you register for the module).
- Because assessment is online and open-book, focus on **understanding + fast lookup**, not rote memory.
- Treat SET A & B as a **head start on CA Final** (Corporate & Economic Laws ↔ Final law paper; Strategic Cost ↔ Final costing/AFM concepts).
