# CA Intermediate — Direct Entry Registration Checklist (ICAI SSP)

Your first concrete action. Registration is done online on the **ICAI Self-Service Portal (SSP): https://eservices.icai.org**

> ⚠️ **Verify-before-you-rely items** (these change; confirm on the live portal): exact **fees**, the **precise study-period rule** (months required before you can sit the exam), and the **next exam attempt** you're eligible for per the official exam calendar.

---

## Step 1 — Confirm eligibility
- [ ] BTech final marksheet shows **≥60% aggregate** (this is your Direct Entry basis as an "other" graduate).
- [ ] (MBA Finance also supports eligibility; keep its marksheet handy too.)
- If you were a *final-year* student you could provisionally register — but you've **passed out**, so you register as a full graduate.

## Step 2 — Create SSP account
- [ ] Go to https://eservices.icai.org → **Student → Register** → create login.
- [ ] Verify via OTP (email + mobile).

## Step 3 — Fill the Intermediate (Direct Entry) form
- [ ] Select **Intermediate** course, **Direct Entry** route.
- [ ] Register for **both groups**.
- [ ] Enter education details (BTech + MBA).

## Step 4 — Upload documents (keep scans ready)
- [ ] Recent **passport photo** + **signature** (as per size specs on portal)
- [ ] **10th / 12th** certificates (proof of name & DOB)
- [ ] **Graduation marksheets + degree** (proof of ≥60%)
- [ ] **MBA marksheets/degree** (supporting)
- [ ] **Category certificate** (if applicable)
- [ ] Aadhaar / ID proof

## Step 5 — Pay fees
- [ ] Pay the Intermediate registration fee (Direct Entry). *Indicative ₹30,000–₹35,000 combined — confirm live.*
- [ ] Save the payment receipt + registration number.

## Step 6 — Note your dates (put these in `05_Tracker.md`)
- [ ] **Registration date** = start of the 8-month study period clock.
- [ ] Compute **earliest eligible exam attempt** and confirm it against the **official exam calendar** (Students → Examination on icai.org).

## Step 7 — Book ICITSS
- [ ] Enrol for **ICITSS** = **ITT (Information Technology)** + **Orientation Course** (~4 weeks total).
- [ ] Complete it during your 8-month study period (it's a prerequisite before articleship). *Indicative fee ~₹6,500–₹7,000.*

## Later (don't do now, just know they exist)
- [ ] **Exam form** for Intermediate — submit in the window ICAI announces for your attempt (separate exam fee per group).
- [ ] **Articleship registration** — after clearing both Inter groups + ICITSS.
- [ ] **Advanced ICITSS** — during/after training, before Final.
- [ ] **CA Final registration** — early in articleship (valid 10 years).
