# Taxation (Income Tax & GST) — High-Yield / Weightage Map

> **Paper structure (CA Intermediate, Paper 3):** 100 marks, 3 hours.
> **Section A — Income Tax Law: 50 marks. Section B — GST: 50 marks.**
> Assessment is **30% objective (30-mark case-scenario MCQ part) + 70% descriptive (70-mark subjective part)**. Question 1 of the descriptive part is normally **compulsory** and is almost always a **comprehensive computation** (Total Income in Sec A, GST liability/ITC in Sec B).
>
> **Honesty note:** The marks below are *indicative averages* across recent attempts, RTPs, MTPs and suggested answers. Actual weightage shifts every attempt — ICAI deliberately rotates emphasis. Treat this as a study-priority guide, **not** a guarantee. Always cross-check against the **latest RTP / MTP / two most recent suggested answers** for your attempt and the applicable **Finance Act / assessment year** and **GST amendments**.

## The 80/20 — Highest-yield chapters (study first & deepest)

These alone typically carry the **majority of marks** in each section. Master computation mechanics cold.

**Income Tax (target ~35 of 50 marks):**
1. **Salaries (03)** — perquisite valuation is a perennial.
2. **PGBP (05)** — biggest single Sec A chapter; depreciation + disallowances.
3. **Capital Gains (06)** — exemptions + computation.
4. **Deductions Ch VI-A (09)** — small effort, appears in *every* computation.
5. **Advance Tax / TDS-TCS (10)** — MCQ goldmine + interest computation.
6. **Residential Status (02)** — short, gates the whole return, easy marks.
7. **Computation of Total Income (12)** — the compulsory Q1 that stitches the above together.

**GST (target ~30 of 50 marks):**
1. **Input Tax Credit (19)** — the single highest-yield GST chapter.
2. **Supply (14)** + **Charge/RCM & Composition (15)** — foundational, always tested.
3. **Value of Supply (18)** — computation-heavy.
4. **Exemptions (16)** and **Registration (20)** — recurring, computation + theory.

> Nail these ~11 chapters and you comfortably clear the 40% subject-wise and 50% aggregate passing bar with room to spare.

## Safe to skim if genuinely short on time

Low-yield relative to effort — don't ignore (MCQs draw from them), but study last and lightly:

- **11 Return Filing & Assessment** — memorise due dates, ITR forms, 139 variants for MCQs; low descriptive weight.
- **13 GST Concept & Framework** — constitutional background/GST Council; mostly 1–2 MCQs.
- **21 Tax Invoice / Credit-Debit Notes** — learn contents & time limits for MCQs.
- **23 GST Returns** — know return names, due dates, late fee; rarely a big descriptive question.
- **22 Payment of Tax** — learn only the **ITC set-off order** and interest rate; the rest is MCQ trivia.

> These five collectively are still worth ~12–15 marks (mostly via the MCQ part), so *skim, don't skip* — the return is high per minute for pure recall.

---

## One-line paper strategy

**Both sections are 50 marks with a compulsory Q1 computation each (Total Income in Sec A, GST liability/ITC in Sec B) — the paper is ~60% practical/computational and ~40% theory/provision-recall, so bank the two compulsory computations first, farm the 30-mark case-scenario MCQ part (fast, no negative marking) for guaranteed marks, and treat pure-theory chapters as MCQ fuel rather than descriptive answers.**
