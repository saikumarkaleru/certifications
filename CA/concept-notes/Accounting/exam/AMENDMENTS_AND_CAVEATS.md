# Advanced Accounting — Amendment & Caveat Checklist

> **Read this before you trust any number in these notes.**
>
> These notes are built to teach you the *structure and the logic* — the "why" behind
> every treatment, presentation, and calculation. They are deliberately **not** a source
> of truth for the *exact current figures, rates, and limits* that ICAI can change from
> one attempt to the next.
>
> **Golden rule:** For your specific attempt, the *method* here is durable, but every
> **rate, limit, threshold, date, and statutory reference** MUST be re-confirmed against
> the ICAI Study Material edition and the **RTP / MTP statutory update** applicable to
> **your exam attempt** (month + year). When in doubt, ICAI's own material for your
> attempt wins over any note, coaching PDF, or older edition.

---

## 0. How to use this checklist (do this first, every attempt)

1. **Find your applicable edition.** ICAI announces which Study Material edition and which
   amendments apply "for [Month] [Year] examination and onwards." Note it down.
2. **Download the RTP for your attempt.** The Revision Test Paper carries a
   **"Applicability of Standards / Statutory Update"** section listing exactly what is in
   and what is out. This is the single most authoritative "what changed" document.
3. **Cross-check the MTP** (Mock Test Paper) — it reflects the same cut-off as the RTP.
4. **Confirm the cut-off date.** Amendments/notifications issued *up to a stated date* are
   examinable; anything after is not. Get that date from the RTP.
5. Only then, plug the confirmed numbers into the logic these notes give you.

**Where to confirm (authoritative order):**
`ICAI BoS Knowledge Portal → Study Material (current edition) → RTP for your attempt →
MTP for your attempt → ICAI announcements/errata`. Coaching material is a convenience, not
an authority.

## Part A — Advanced Accounting: what actually moves

Advanced Accounting is **standards-driven, not rate-driven**, so it is *relatively stable*
compared to Taxation or Law. But "stable" is not "frozen." Flag and re-verify these:

### A1. Applicable Accounting Standards (AS) — scope & wording
- **What it is:** The list of AS in the syllabus and the *current text* of each standard.
- **Why it changes:** ICAI revises AS, issues amendments, and occasionally changes which
  standards are in the Intermediate scope vs. deferred/excluded. Disclosure requirements
  and wording get updated.
- **Confirm at:** RTP "Applicability of Accounting Standards" list + current Study Material.
  Verify the *exact* standards examinable and any carve-outs for your attempt.

### A2. Schedule III presentation (Division I) & disclosure formats
- **What it is:** Format of Balance Sheet / Statement of P&L and note disclosures used in
  company accounts, buy-back, and financial-statement problems.
- **Why it changes:** MCA amends Schedule III (e.g., additional disclosure requirements —
  ageing schedules, ratios, promoter shareholding, etc.). Presentation questions must match
  the *current* format.
- **Confirm at:** Current Study Material chapter on Company Financial Statements + any RTP
  statutory update referencing Schedule III amendments.

### A3. Company law-linked accounting limits & rules
- **What it is:** Numbers that come *from the Companies Act* but are applied in accounting —
  e.g., **buy-back limits/conditions**, **CSR** applicability thresholds and spend %,
  **managerial remuneration** limits, **dividend** rules, **transfer to reserves**.
- **Why it changes:** These ride on Companies Act 2013 amendments and MCA notifications
  (this is the *high-churn* interface between Accounting and Law).
- **Confirm at:** Current Study Material + RTP statutory update. If a figure looks like a
  percentage cap or a rupee threshold, treat it as **amendment-sensitive**.

### A4. Amalgamation, internal reconstruction, LLP/partnership rules
- **What it is:** Treatment methods (pooling vs. purchase logic, etc.) and any statutory
  hooks (e.g., LLP Act provisions, partnership rules).
- **Why it changes:** Method logic is stable; *statutory references and any limits* can be
  updated by amendment.
- **Confirm at:** Study Material + RTP. The **mechanics** in these notes stay valid; verify
  citations and any limits.

### A5. Consolidated Financial Statements & other chapters
- **What it is:** Consolidation procedure (AS-21/23/27 as applicable in scope), branch
  accounts, insurance/banking company accounts, ESOP, etc.
- **Why it changes:** Mostly stable in *method*; scope inclusion and disclosure detail can
  shift edition to edition.
- **Confirm at:** RTP applicability list — confirm the chapter is in scope and at the
  current disclosure level for your attempt.

> **Bottom line for Accounting:** the *procedure* is the durable asset. Re-verify the
> **standards list, Schedule III format, and every Companies-Act-derived limit** (buy-back,
> CSR, remuneration, dividend, reserves) against your attempt's RTP.

## Part B — Cross-paper HIGH-churn items (verify ruthlessly)

These do **not** belong to Advanced Accounting, but you will meet them in other Inter/Final
papers. They change *every attempt* and are the #1 source of wrong answers from stale notes.
**Never memorize a number from an old note for these — always pull the current figure.**

### B1. Taxation — the highest-churn paper of all
For **each** of the following, the value can change at *every* Budget / Finance Act and via
CBDT/CBIC notifications. Confirm each against the RTP statutory update for your attempt and
the applicable **Finance Act** / **Assessment Year**:

- **Income-tax slab rates** — old regime vs. **new regime (default u/s 115BAC)**; which
  AY applies to your attempt.
- **Surcharge** rates and thresholds (and any cap under the new regime).
- **Rebate u/s 87A** — eligibility income limit and rebate amount (differs between regimes;
  revised repeatedly).
- **Standard deduction** and salaried/pension-related figures.
- **Deduction limits** — 80C, 80D, 80CCD, 80G, etc. (some unavailable under the new regime).
- **TDS / TCS rates and threshold limits** — section-wise; frequently re-notified.
- **GST rates** and the **registration threshold** limits (goods vs. services; special-
  category states); composition scheme limits.
- **Latest Finance Act amendments** and **GST Council / notification changes** up to the
  RTP cut-off date.
- **Depreciation rates, presumptive limits (44AD/44ADA/44AE), audit thresholds.**

**Confirm at:** Taxation RTP "Statutory Update" (states the applicable AY and Finance Act) +
current Taxation Study Material. This section is *deliberately non-exhaustive* — if it is a
rate, a limit, or a threshold in tax, assume it moved.

### B2. Law (Corporate & Other Laws) — recent Companies Act churn
- **What it is:** Companies Act 2013 provisions and their amendments, plus MCA
  notifications, rules, and (for Inter) the applicable allied laws.
- **Why it changes:** Frequent **Companies (Amendment) Acts**, rule amendments, and MCA
  notifications; decriminalisation, thresholds, and procedural changes are common.
- **Confirm at:** Law RTP "Statutory Update / Recent Amendments" section + current Study
  Material. Pay special attention to anything with a **monetary threshold, day-count, or
  applicability limit** (e.g., small company definition, CSR, deposits, audit rotation).

### B3. Anything rate/limit/threshold-based, anywhere
- **Rule of thumb:** If a figure is a **rate (%), a rupee limit, a threshold, a day/period,
  or a date**, treat it as amendment-sensitive and re-confirm — regardless of which paper.

## Part C — The caveat, stated plainly

- These notes optimise for **understanding and retention of method**, not for being a rate
  card. That is by design: methods survive many attempts; numbers do not.
- **Any number you see here may be out of date for your attempt.** Do not reproduce a rate,
  limit, or threshold in the exam on the strength of these notes alone.
- The **authoritative, attempt-specific source** is always: **ICAI Study Material (current
  edition) + the RTP/MTP statutory update for your exam month and year.**
- If these notes and the current ICAI RTP disagree on a figure, **ICAI is right and this
  note is stale** — and please update the note.
