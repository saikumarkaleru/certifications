# CA Intermediate — Week-by-Week Dated Calendar

**Anchor assumptions** (change these and the dates shift, but the *structure* holds):
- Register on ICAI SSP by **mid-July 2026**
- Study starts **Monday 13 July 2026**
- Target attempt: **CA Intermediate, both groups, early May 2027**
- ~42 weeks · full-time · 6 days/week (~48 hrs/wk) · Sundays = light/rest

> ⚠️ Confirm the **actual May 2027 exam dates** on ICAI's calendar and adjust the last weeks. If you register later, slide every date forward by the same offset.

---

## Daily template (Mon–Sat)

| Slot | Time | Track | Focus |
|------|------|-------|-------|
| Morning | ~4 hrs | **Track A — Practical** | problem-solving subject of the week |
| Afternoon | ~3 hrs | **Track B — Theory** | reading/memory subject of the week |
| Evening | ~1 hr | Revision + MCQ | revise *yesterday*, 20–30 MCQs, update summary/formula sheet |

**Sunday:** 2–3 hrs light — clear backlog, weekly self-quiz, rest.

Two subjects run **in parallel** the whole first pass (one practical + one theory) so you never spend a full day on pure theory.

## Rules that make this work
- **First attempt, both groups** — any slip delays articleship, which delays everything.
- **RTP + MTP are mandatory**, not optional — ICAI signals importance through them.
- **Amendments**: study the Tax & Law editions **applicable to the May 2027 attempt**.
- **Log daily** in `05_Tracker.md`; every wrong answer → weak-areas log → revised weekly.
- Falling behind? Compress the **second read**, never skip **mocks**.
