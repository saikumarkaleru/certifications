Use lectures for tough conceptual topics (Accounting standards, Costing, Law); read the module for everything else.

## Practice material (the backbone) — from Examination section

**Path:** icai.org → Students → Examination → *Intermediate*

| Resource | Use it for |
|----------|-----------|
| **RTP** (Revision Test Papers) | Per-attempt; do 100% — strong signal on important areas |
| **MTP** (Mock Test Papers) | Full-length timed practice; multiple series per attempt |
| **Past Question Papers** | Last 5–6 attempts |
| **Suggested Answers** | ICAI's model answers — learn presentation & marking |
| **MCQ / case-scenario booklets** | 30% of each paper is MCQ (with negative marking) |

Download the **RTP & MTP for the May 2027 attempt** as soon as ICAI releases them (usually a couple of months before the exam).

---

## Amendments & updates (critical for Tax & Law)
- **Path:** BoS Knowledge Portal → *Academic Updates / Supplementary Study Material*
- Tax (IT + GST) and Company Law change every session. Always study the **Statutory Update / Supplementary** issued for **May 2027** alongside the main book.
