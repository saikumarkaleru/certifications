# Chapter 02 — Residential Status & Scope of Total Income

## Snapshot

- Residential status is the **first substantive gate** in the computation pipeline: person [Ch.1] → previous year [Ch.1] → **residential status [Sec 6]** → scope of total income [Sec 5] → five heads → deductions/set-off → rates.
- It decides the **SCOPE** (which income basket is taxable), **NOT the rate**.
- Based on **physical presence (day-count)**, not citizenship, domicile, or nationality.
- Tested **afresh every previous year** — no "once resident, always resident".
- Three individual labels: **NR**, **ROR**, **RNOR**. RNOR reached by **four routes**.
- Governing sections: **6** (who you are), **5** (what is caught), **7 & 9** (deeming — no escape).
- Illustrations use **PY 2025-26**. Verify ₹15,00,000 threshold, 120-day figure, POEM turnover threshold, and day-count convention against current ICAI material.

## Core concepts

- **Economic allegiance / nexus** drives residence. Two nexuses: **source nexus** (income arose here → catches everyone) and **residence nexus** (person lives here → extra reach onto global income, residents only).
- **Sec 5 design:** everyone pays on India-source income; **residents additionally** pay on foreign income.
- **Residence ≠ domicile ≠ nationality/citizenship.** An Indian citizen/domiciliary can be NR for a given PY.
- **Accrue/arise ≠ receive** — separate events. *Accrual* = right to receive arises; *receipt* = money comes to hand. Sec 5 catches income if **either** event is (or is deemed) in India.
- **"Received" = first receipt.** Salary first received in India then remitted abroad = received in India. **Remittance to India of income already received abroad is NOT "received in India".** Past-year foreign income brought into India later = **not taxable for anyone**.
- **RNOR = soft-landing** transitional status for returning NRIs: shields **passive** foreign income but not foreign income from a business controlled from India.
- Statuses with no ROR/RNOR split: **firm/AOP and company** (only Resident or NR). RNOR exists **only for individuals & HUFs**.

## Key provisions / rules

### Statutory map

| Section | Deals with |
|---|---|
| **Sec 6** | How residential status is determined (individual, HUF, firm, company) |
| **Sec 5** | Scope of total income for each status |
| **Sec 7** | Income deemed to be *received* in India |
| **Sec 9** | Income deemed to *accrue or arise* in India |

Hook: *"6 = WHO, 5 = WHAT, 7 & 9 = no escape."*

### Individual — Step 1: Basic conditions [Sec 6(1)] — Resident if satisfies at least ONE

| Limb | Condition |
|---|---|
| **(a)** | In India **≥ 182 days** during the PY |
| **(b)** | In India **≥ 60 days** in PY **AND ≥ 365 days** in the **4 immediately preceding years** (BOTH limbs cumulative) |

Fails both → **Non-Resident (NR)**. Always name **which limb** was used.

### Relaxations to condition (b) — 60 replaced by 182 [Explanation 1 to Sec 6(1)]

| Case | Rule |
|---|---|
| Indian **citizen leaving India during PY for employment abroad** (or crew of Indian ship) | 60 → **182** (only limb (a) can make resident) |
| Indian **citizen or PIO** outside India **coming on a visit** to India during PY | 60 → **182** |
| **Visitor** (citizen/PIO) whose **total income other than foreign-source > ₹15,00,000** | 60 → **120** (not 182) [Expl. 1(b) proviso] |

- **"For employment"** read widely — includes leaving to start/carry on a business/profession/vocation abroad. **Excludes** leaving for holiday, medical treatment, or study.
- Employment relaxation applies **only in the year of leaving**; later years use the visitor relaxation.
- **PIO:** he, or either parent, or any grandparent, was **born in undivided India** (pre-Partition India, incl. present Pakistan/Bangladesh).

### Deemed resident [Sec 6(1A)] — ALL four preconditions must hold

1. Person is an **Indian citizen** (PIO **not** covered).
2. **Total income other than foreign-source income > ₹15,00,000.**
3. **Not liable to tax in any other country** by reason of domicile/residence ("liable to tax" = the *right* to tax exists, not that tax was paid).
4. **Fallback only** — applies only if NR under ordinary 6(1). Test 6(1) first.

Such a deemed resident is always classified **RNOR**.

### Step 2: Additional conditions [Sec 6(6)] — ROR needs BOTH; fail either → RNOR

| Condition | Test | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| **(i)** | Resident in **≥ 2 of the 10** immediately preceding PYs | resident-**years** |
| **(ii)** | In India **≥ 730 days in the 7** immediately preceding PYs | actual **days** |

Windows differ: **10 years** for (i), **7 years** for (ii). Do not swap.

**Four routes into RNOR:** (1) fail additional (i); (2) fail additional (ii); (3) 120-day high-income visitor who becomes resident; (4) Sec 6(1A) deemed resident.

### Non-individual persons

| Person | Resident if… | ROR/RNOR split? |
|---|---|:--:|
| **HUF [6(2)]** | control & management **wholly or partly in India**; ROR/RNOR by testing **karta** on 6(6) additional conditions | Yes |
| **Firm / AOP [6(2),6(4)]** | control & management **wholly or partly in India** | No |
| **Company [6(3)]** | **Indian company (always resident)** OR **POEM in India** | No |

- NR only if control & management is **wholly outside** India (low bar to be caught, high bar to escape).
- "Control and management" = the **head-and-brain** (where policy decisions taken, *De Beers* principle), not day-to-day operations.
- **POEM** = place where key management & commercial decisions for the business as a whole are **in substance** made (substance-over-form). Company POEM not triggered merely by some Indian shareholders/directors/support office. De-minimis turnover relief (verify figure).

### Scope of total income [Sec 5]

| Type of income | ROR | RNOR | NR |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Received / deemed received **in India** | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |
| Accrues / deemed to accrue **in India** | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |
| Foreign income (accrues **and** received outside India) from **business controlled from / profession set up in India** | ✔ | ✔ | ✘ |
| Any **other (passive) foreign** income | ✔ | ✘ | ✘ |

**Two-question reduction:** (1) India-connected? → taxable for all, stop. (2) If foreign: from a business controlled from India/profession set up in India? Yes → ROR & RNOR; No (passive) → ROR only.

### Sec 9 — income deemed to accrue or arise in India

| Sec 9(1) | Item |
|---|---|
| **(i)** | Income from **business connection**, property/asset/source in India, or transfer of a **capital asset situate in India** (incl. **indirect transfer** of shares deriving substantial value from Indian assets). Only income **attributable to Indian operations** is caught. |
| **(ii)** | **Salary** for services **rendered in India** (even if paid abroad) |
| **(iii)** | **Salary** paid by **Govt of India** to an **Indian citizen** for services **outside India** |
| **(iv)/(v)** | **Dividend** by Indian company; **Interest** by Govt / resident / NR (funds used for business in India) |
| **(vi)/(vii)** | **Royalty** & **FTS** by Govt / resident / NR (used for business in India) |

Source rule for interest/royalty/FTS: Indian-source if **payer is resident**, *except* where used for a business/source **outside** India; a non-resident payer creates Indian-source income if the borrowing/IP/service is used for a business **in** India. Test = **where the money/IP/service is put to work**.

**Sec 7 — deemed received:** excess employer contribution to RPF, interest credited to RPF beyond notified rate, transferred balance. Deemed received in India → taxable for **all three statuses**.

## Worked mini-example

**Ms. Chandni** — Indian citizen, NR for 20 years in USA, returned permanently **1 April 2025**, present entire **PY 2025-26 (365 days)**.

**Step 1 (basic):** 365 ≥ 182 → **RESIDENT**.
**Step 2 (additional):** (i) resident in ≥2 of last 10 PYs? NR all 10 years → **fails** → **RNOR**.

| # | Income | Nature | Taxable (RNOR)? | ₹ |
|---|---|---|:--:|---:|
| 1 | Consultancy salary, services + received in India | India-source | ✔ | 18,00,000 |
| 2 | Bengaluru rent, received in India | India-source | ✔ | 3,00,000 |
| 3 | US bank interest (accrues + received USA) | Passive foreign | ✘ | — |
| 4 | Business received in USA but **controlled from India** | India-controlled foreign business | ✔ | 6,00,000 |
| 5 | US dividend, received in USA | Passive foreign | ✘ | — |
| | **Total taxable in India** | | | **27,00,000** |

Same facts: **ROR** = ₹32,00,000 (all 5); **NR** = ₹21,00,000 (items 1,2 only). ₹5,00,000 swing from status alone.

## Exam traps & must-remember

1. **Citizenship ≠ residence.** Foreign citizen present 182+ days = resident; Indian citizen abroad can be NR.
2. **"Received" vs "remitted".** Foreign income remitted to India stays foreign; past-year foreign income brought in later = never taxable.
3. **Forgetting the relaxation.** 60-day limb inoperative (→182, or 120 if visitor's non-foreign income > ₹15L) for citizen leaving for employment / citizen-PIO visiting.
4. **RNOR business exception** — foreign income from business **controlled from India** IS taxable for RNOR. Deciding word = location of **control**, not operations/receipt.
5. **Day counting** — both **arrival and departure days count** as in-India days. Count only days **within the PY** (1 Apr–31 Mar) when a stay straddles 31 March. Leap-year PY: 182 still governs (366 days available; don't adjust threshold).
6. **"2 of 10" counts RESIDENT-years, not days.** (i) uses 10-year window; (ii) uses 7-year/730-day. Never swap.
7. **HUF/firm/company use CONTROL, not days.** Indian company is **always resident** regardless of POEM.
8. **RNOR exists only for individuals & HUFs.** Firm/AOP/company = Resident or NR only.
9. **Sec 6(1A)** — Indian **citizens only** (not PIO), non-foreign income > ₹15L, not liable to tax anywhere → **RNOR**; fallback used only if NR under 6(1).
10. **Govt salary to citizen abroad** [9(1)(iii)] taxable even for NR (Indian source). Non-Govt employer paying NR for services abroad = foreign income, not taxable.
11. **120-day rule vs 6(1A)** — 120-day = high-income **visitor** (citizen OR PIO) who **actually crosses 120 days**; 6(1A) = high-income **citizen** deemed resident regardless of days, only when tax-resident nowhere.
12. **"Business connection" ≠ whole global profit** — only India-attributable part deemed to arise here.
13. **HUF two layers:** Layer 1 = family's **control** (Resident/NR); Layer 2 = **karta's** personal additional conditions (ROR/RNOR).
14. **Don't assume a returning NRI is auto-RNOR** — may be ROR immediately if prior visits satisfy 2/10 and 730/7. Always check the numbers.
15. Determine status **first, completely**, then apply scope; quote the section beside each conclusion; state the reason for each income item.

## One-line recall

- **Basic:** ≥182 days in PY **OR** (≥60 in PY **AND** ≥365 in prior 4 yrs); relaxations lift 60→182 (or →120 if visitor non-foreign income > ₹15L).
- **Additional (ROR needs both):** resident in ≥2 of last 10 PYs **AND** ≥730 days in last 7 PYs; fail either → RNOR.
- **Scope:** India income → taxed for all; India-controlled foreign business → ROR + RNOR; passive foreign → ROR only.
- **Sec 6(1A):** Indian citizen + non-foreign income > ₹15,00,000 + not liable to tax anywhere → deemed RNOR (fallback).
- **Non-individuals:** HUF/firm/AOP resident if control wholly/partly in India; company resident if Indian company OR POEM in India; RNOR split only for individuals & HUFs.
- Hooks: *"6=WHO, 5=WHAT, 7&9=no escape"* · *"182 half-year; 60+365 a habit; 2/10 & 730/7 history for ROR."*
