# Chapter 08 — Clubbing of Income & Set-off/Carry-forward of Losses

## Snapshot
- **Clubbing (s.60–65):** substance over form. Keep the real economic benefit but shift paper income to a controlled relative/entity → income taxed in the **real owner's** hands. Decides *whose* income it is. Works **sideways** (person to person).
- **Set-off & carry-forward (s.70–80):** a loss is negative income; relief is **rationed** — intra-head (s.70) → inter-head (s.71) → carry-forward (s.72–74A). Decides *how a loss travels*. Works **downward and forward** (head to head, year to year).
- Clubbing tracks **retained control, not blood relationship**. It is surgical, not total — genuine full-value transfers/adult-relative gifts escape.
- Relief gets progressively harder the further the loss travels (carry-forward is most fenced + needs timely return).

## Core concepts
- **Income-splitting problem:** progressive slabs tempt re-labelling income to empty-slab relatives (gift-and-earn; divert the income source). Clubbing plugs only the artificial channels.
- **Accretion / "income on income":** first-generation income (from the transferred asset) is clubbed; **income earned on the clubbed income is NOT clubbed** (already taxed once → offspring is transferee's).
- **Four dials behind set-off fences:** ability-to-pay (allow c/f), anti-abuse (fence certain losses), finality (time limits), and **rate integrity** (LTCL locked to LTCG; lottery reducible by nothing).
- **Order is fixed** (intra → inter → c/f) because relief becomes more aggressive/avoidance-like the further a loss reaches.

## Key provisions / rules

### Clubbing (s.60–65)

| Section | Trigger | Clubbed with | Key exception / note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **60** | Transfer of **income** without transfer of **asset** (revocable or not) | Transferor | **No relationship needed** — even to friend/charity. "Tree stays → fruit stays." |
| **61** | **Revocable** transfer of asset | Transferor | **s.62:** not clubbed if irrevocable during transferee's lifetime / specified period + no benefit + no reassumption. Clubbing starts the **year power to revoke arises** (s.62(2)). "Any provision to re-transfer/re-assume power" = revocable, even contingent/future. |
| **64(1)(ii)** | **Remuneration to spouse** from concern where individual has **substantial interest** | Higher-earning spouse (total income excl. this remuneration) | Substantial interest = **≥20%** shares/voting (co.) or **≥20%** profit share (other), self + relatives. **Skill exception:** both (a) technical/professional qualification AND (b) income solely attributable to it → not clubbed. |
| **64(1)(iv)** | **Asset to spouse** for inadequate consideration | Transferor | Not clubbed if: adequate consideration / agreement to live apart / relationship absent at transfer **and** accrual. **Partial consideration → club proportionate gift %.** s.27(i): house → transferor deemed owner (HP income direct). **Gain on later sale also clubbed.** |
| **64(1)(vi)** | Asset to **son's wife (DIL)** for inadequate consideration | Transferor (father/mother-in-law) | Same proportionate + timing rules. |
| **64(1)(vii)/(viii)** | Transfer to person/AOP **for benefit of** spouse / son's wife | Transferor | Benefit may be **immediate OR deferred** (accumulating trust still caught). |
| **64(1A)** | **All income of minor child** (incl. step/adopted/married daughter) | Higher-earning parent (before this clubbing); if marriage doesn't subsist → parent who **maintains** child. Sticky in later years unless AO orders. | Exceptions: minor's **manual work / skill/talent** income; minor with **80U disability**. **s.10(32):** exempt **₹1,500 per child** (or actual if less), per child. Layering trap: interest on skill-income IS clubbed. |
| **64(2)** | Self-property **converted to HUF** for inadequate consideration | Individual (transferor) | On partition, **spouse's share** still clubbed (via s.64(1)(iv)). |
| **65** | Recovery route | Transferee **jointly & severally liable** (to extent of asset) for tax on clubbed income, if AO serves notice. |

### Set-off — intra-head fences (s.70)
- **Speculation loss** → only speculation profit (s.73).
- **LTCL** → only **LTCG**. **STCL** → **STCG or LTCG**.
- **Race-horse** owning/maintaining loss → same activity only (s.74A).
- **Specified business (35AD)** loss → other specified-business profit only (s.73A).
- **No set-off vs casual income** (lottery/cards/gambling/betting — s.58(4)/115BB): taxed **gross**, no deduction, no set-off.
- **Loss from exempt source cannot be set off** at all (phantom loss).
- Otherwise a source's loss freely cancels another source in same head (Business A vs B; House 1 vs House 2).

### Set-off — inter-head fences (s.71)
- **Business loss ≠ Salary** (can hit HP/CG/OS).
- **Capital-gains loss:** stays **within Capital Gains only**.
- **Speculation / race-horse / 35AD:** never leave their silo.
- **House-property loss:** inter-head cap **₹2,00,000/yr** (s.71(3A)); excess c/f.
- Nothing may be set off against **casual/lottery income** (absolute).
- **Directionality trap:** fences are on the **loss**, not the target head.

### Carry-forward (s.72–74A, 71B)

| Loss type | Sec | C/f against | Years | Timely return (s.80)? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| House-property loss | 71B | HP income only | **8** | No (late OK) |
| Non-speculation business loss | 72 | Business income (spec + non-spec) | **8** | **Yes** |
| Speculation loss | 73 | Speculation profit | **4** | **Yes** |
| Specified business (35AD) | 73A | Specified-business profit | **No limit** | **Yes** |
| Race-horse loss | 74A | Same activity | **4** | **Yes** |
| Long-term capital loss | 74 | LTCG only | **8** | **Yes** |
| Short-term capital loss | 74 | STCG or LTCG | **8** | **Yes** |
| Unabsorbed depreciation | 32(2) | Any income **except salary** | **No limit** | No |

- **s.72 b/f loss:** can later hit **any business income incl. speculation profit**; but b/f *speculation* loss → speculation profit only. Business **need not continue** to carry forward.
- **s.80:** timely s.139(1) return needed to c/f — except **HP loss** & **unabsorbed depreciation**. Current-year set-off always allowed even with belated return.
- **s.79 (closely-held company):** c/f loss denied unless **≥51% voting power** beneficially held by same persons on last day of loss year and set-off year (anti "loss-shopping"; carve-outs — verify AY).
- **Future-year order:** current-year depreciation → **b/f business loss (s.72)** → **unabsorbed depreciation** last ("use what expires first").

## Worked mini-example
**Set-off & c/f (Mr. E):** Salary 9,00,000; HP1 loss 3,50,000; HP2 profit 40,000; Business X loss 2,00,000; Business Y profit 1,20,000; Speculation loss 60,000; STCG 80,000; LTCL 1,00,000; OS interest 50,000.
1. **Intra-head:** HP net loss = −3,50,000 + 40,000 = **3,10,000**. Business net loss = −2,00,000 + 1,20,000 = **80,000**. Speculation loss 60,000 → c/f (4 yrs). LTCL 1,00,000 can't touch STCG → c/f (8 yrs); STCG 80,000 stays.
2. **Inter-head:** HP loss capped **₹2,00,000** vs other heads → 1,10,000 c/f (8 yrs, s.71B). Business loss 80,000 (≠salary) vs STCG 80,000 → both nil.
3. **GTI:** Salary 9,00,000 − 2,00,000 = 7,00,000; OS 50,000; STCG 0. **GTI = ₹7,50,000.**
4. **C/f:** HP 1,10,000 (8y); Speculation 60,000 (4y); LTCL 1,00,000 (8y).
- *Tweak:* if HP loss were 1,80,000 → under cap, fully set off, nothing c/f. If STCG were 50,000 → business loss absorbs 50,000, remaining **30,000 c/f under s.72** (salary fence forces it).

**Accretion (Mr. A):** gifts ₹8,00,000 to homemaker wife → FD interest **₹56,000 clubbed** with A (s.64(1)(iv)); reinvested ₹56,000 earns **₹4,000 = wife's own** (accretion, not clubbed).

**Minor + s.10(32) (Mrs. B higher earner):** Son bond interest 40,000 − 1,500 = **38,500 clubbed**; daughter interest 1,000 − min(1,500,1,000)=1,000 → **0**; son's ₹25,000 singing prize (own talent) → **not clubbed**. Total clubbed = **₹38,500**.

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **Accretion:** only original-asset income clubbed; second-generation income free. Watch 3-generation problems.
2. **Relationship-timing (spouse):** must exist at **transfer AND accrual** — transfer before marriage → not clubbed.
3. **Skill exception** defeats clubbing even if 20% interest met (spouse) / bonds exist (minor).
4. **Cross-parent stickiness:** once clubbed with one parent, stays even if incomes reverse.
5. **s.10(32):** per child, capped at actual income (₹1,000 income → ₹1,000, not ₹1,500).
6. **Business loss NEVER cancels salary** — and **unabsorbed depreciation also can't hit salary**.
7. **LTCL is LT-only; STCL flexible (STCG/LTCG).** Don't reverse.
8. **HP ₹2,00,000 inter-head cap** — test against cap *before* writing "carry forward"; excess c/f vs future HP income only.
9. **Speculation & race-horse = 4 years**, never leave silo.
10. **s.80:** late return loses c/f — except HP loss & unabsorbed depreciation; current-year set-off still allowed late.
11. **Casual/lottery income:** no set-off, no expenditure, taxed gross (s.115BB).
12. **Future-year order:** current dep → b/f loss (s.72) → unabsorbed dep.
13. **Partial consideration:** inadequate ≠ nil — club only proportionate gift portion.
14. **Clubbing follows asset into capital gain**, not just recurring income.
15. **s.60 needs no relationship.**
16. **Loss from exempt source = phantom**, cannot be set off.
17. **b/f business loss can hit speculation profit; b/f speculation loss cannot hit non-spec.** Direction matters.
18. **s.79:** >49% beneficial shareholding change kills c/f in closely-held company.
- *Regime note:* under default **s.115BAC**, treatment of HP loss set-off / certain c/f differs — verify current-AY interaction with s.71/71B.

## One-line recall
- **Clubbing = whose income** (sideways); **set-off = how a loss travels** (down + forward).
- Club first-generation income only; accretion stays with transferee; clubbing follows asset into capital gain.
- Minor → higher-earning parent minus **₹1,500** (s.10(32)); skill/80U income exempt from clubbing.
- Order: **s.70 intra → s.71 inter → carry forward**; loss fences sit on the loss, not the target.
- Business loss ≠ salary; LTCL ↔ LTCG only; HP cap **₹2,00,000**; casual income = no set-off.
- Time limits: **8 yrs** normal; **4** speculation/race-horse; **none** for 35AD & depreciation; c/f needs timely return (s.80) except HP loss & depreciation.
