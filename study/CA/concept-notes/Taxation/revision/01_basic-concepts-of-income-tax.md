# Chapter 01 — Basic Concepts of Income Tax

> Illustrative rates/limits drawn from recent Finance Acts. Verify exact figures and applicable AY against current ICAI material. Sitting used: PY 2025-26 → AY 2026-27.

## Snapshot

- Income tax = progressive, annual, **direct** charge on **total income** of a **person**, earned in the **previous year (PY)**, taxed in the **assessment year (AY)** at the year's rates.
- Legal layers: **Income-tax Act, 1961** (skeleton — definitions, charge, machinery) · **Finance Act** (annual rates) · **Rules, 1962** (procedure) · CBDT circulars (bind dept only) · notifications · case law.
- Pipeline: Person → residential status → income under 5 heads → aggregate + set-off = **Gross Total Income (GTI)** → less Chapter VI-A = **Total Income (TI)** → slab/special rate → +surcharge → −marginal relief → −rebate 87A → +cess → less TDS/advance tax = **Tax Payable**.

## Core concepts

- **Person [Sec 2(31)] — 7 categories:** Individual, HUF, Company, Firm (incl. LLP), AOP/BOI, Local authority, AJP. AJP is the catch-all so nothing escapes. AOP = any persons (companies can be members); **BOI = individuals only**; two companies joining = **AOP, never BOI**. **LLP taxed as a firm**, not a company. HUF taxed separately from members. Applies "whether or not formed to derive income."
- **Assessee [Sec 2(7)] — 3 sub-types:** (1) Ordinary (tax/interest/penalty payable or under proceeding — includes refund-claimant and loss-return filer); (2) Deemed/Representative (legal heir, guardian of minor, agent of NR, trustee); (3) Assessee-in-default (failed to deduct/deposit TDS). Every assessee is a person; not every person is an assessee.
- **Income [Sec 2(24)] — inclusive** (not exhaustive), so future cleverness is netted. Includes business profits, dividends, voluntary contributions to trust, perquisites/profits in lieu of salary, capital gains u/s 45, lottery/betting winnings, Keyman insurance, gifts u/s 56(2)(x). Legal or illegal source irrelevant; cash or kind; regular or one-time; received OR accrued.
- **Capital vs revenue receipt:** capital receipt taxable ONLY if a charging section (e.g. Sec 45) brings it in; revenue receipt taxable UNLESS specifically exempted.
- **Diversion vs application:** income diverted by **overriding title** *before* it reaches you (charge by will/decree/statute) = not your income. Income that reaches you then spent on an obligation = application = fully taxable (*CIT v. Sitaldas Tirathdas*).
- **Not income / exempt income / taxable income:** exempt (Sec 10, e.g. agricultural income) IS income but freed from tax; "not income" never enters computation.
- **GTI [Sec 80B(5)]** = sum of 5 heads after set-off, before Chapter VI-A. **TI [Sec 2(45)]** = GTI − Chapter VI-A. Chapter VI-A deductions can never exceed GTI (cannot create/increase a loss).

**Sec 4 (Charge):** 4(1) — tax charged for AY on PY's total income of every person at FA rates, "subject to provisions of this Act" (lets deeming/override/exemption sections work). 4(2) — TDS / advance tax (pay-as-you-earn). Sec 4 is the general charging section; Secs 15/22/28/45/56 are head-level charging sections.

**PY = AY same-year taxation exceptions** — memory hook "Ships Leaving Are Transferring & Discontinuing":

| Sec | Situation |
|-----|-----------|
| 172 | Non-resident shipping (self-contained; % of freight deemed income) |
| 174 | Persons leaving India, likely permanently |
| 174A | AOP/BOI/AJP formed for a particular event, likely to dissolve |
| 175 | Person likely to transfer assets to avoid tax |
| 176 | Discontinued business |

**First PY of a new business/source:** date of set-up → following 31 March (shorter than 12 months permitted only for first PY).

**Slabs — Old regime, resident individual < 60 (illustrative):**

| Slab | Rate |
|------|------|
| Up to ₹2,50,000 | Nil |
| ₹2,50,001–₹5,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹5,00,001–₹10,00,000 | 20% |
| Above ₹10,00,000 | 30% |

Senior (60–80): exemption ₹3,00,000; super-senior (80+): ₹5,00,000 — **old regime only**.

**Slabs — New regime u/s 115BAC (illustrative):**

| Slab | Rate |
|------|------|
| Up to ₹4,00,000 | Nil |
| ₹4,00,001–₹8,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹8,00,001–₹12,00,000 | 10% |
| ₹12,00,001–₹16,00,000 | 15% |
| ₹16,00,001–₹20,00,000 | 20% |
| ₹20,00,001–₹24,00,000 | 25% |
| Above ₹24,00,000 | 30% |

**Slabs vs flat:** Individual/HUF/AOP-BOI/AJP → slabs + basic exemption. Firm/LLP (flat 30%, no exemption), Company (domestic/foreign vary; 115BAA 22%, 115BAB 15% new mfg), Local authority (30%), Co-op society → flat rate, no basic exemption.

**Rebate 87A:** resident individual only (not HUF, not firm, not NR). Lower of actual tax on non-special-rate income or statutory cap. Illustrative: old — TI ₹5,00,000 / cap ₹12,500; new — higher TI ceiling ₹12,00,000 with marginal relief on the 87A edge. Not on 115BB winnings.

**Surcharge (on tax, individual, illustrative):**

| TI exceeds | Rate |
|------------|------|
| ₹50 lakh | 10% |
| ₹1 crore | 15% |
| ₹2 crore | 25% |
| ₹5 crore | 37% (capped lower, e.g. 25%, under new regime) |

Surcharge on tax relating to dividends and 111A/112/112A capital gains **capped at 15%** regardless of TI — split the tax and cap the gains portion.

**Marginal relief:** at every surcharge threshold, extra tax (incl. surcharge) ≤ extra income above the threshold; benchmark against the **immediately-lower** threshold just crossed.

**Health & Education Cess:** 4% on (income tax + surcharge), after marginal relief and rebate, always last.

**Regime opt-out:** No business income → choose old/new every year in the return. Business/professional income → Form 10-IEA by due date u/s 139(1) to opt out; may switch back to new **once only** (one-way ratchet).

**Rounding:** TI rounded to nearest ₹10 [288A]; final tax to nearest ₹10 [288B]; once each, not intermediate steps.

## Worked mini-example

**Mr. C, resident individual, 45, PY 2025-26, TI ₹51,00,000 (all normal income, old regime). Surcharge 10% above ₹50 lakh; cess 4%.**

Tax on ₹51,00,000: Nil + 12,500 + 1,00,000 + (30% × 41,00,000 = 12,30,000) = **₹13,42,500**
Surcharge 10% × 13,42,500 = ₹1,34,250 → Tax + SC = **₹14,76,750**

Marginal relief test — tax on exactly ₹50,00,000 = 12,500 + 1,00,000 + (30% × 40,00,000) = **₹13,12,500** (no surcharge).
- Extra income = ₹1,00,000; extra tax = 14,76,750 − 13,12,500 = ₹1,64,250 > ₹1,00,000 → relief applies.
- Relief = 1,64,250 − 1,00,000 = ₹64,250 → surcharge after relief = 1,34,250 − 64,250 = ₹70,000.

Tax + surcharge = 13,42,500 + 70,000 = 14,12,500 → cess 4% = 56,500 → **Tax payable = ₹14,69,000.**
Check: 14,12,500 − 13,12,500 = exactly ₹1,00,000 = the extra income. ✔

## Exam traps & must-remember

1. **Higher slab taxes only the slice** in that slab, never the whole income.
2. **87A never reduces tax on 115BB winnings** or (generally) special-rate capital gains.
3. **Cess = on (tax + surcharge)**, computed LAST — after marginal relief and rebate.
4. **Marginal relief only reduces** surcharge; benchmark against the immediately-lower threshold.
5. **Default regime is NEW (115BAC).** If question is silent, use new. Business opt-out gets one switch back.
6. **Person leaving India permanently → Sec 174** same-year assessment (not next-year).
7. **Diversion (before it reaches you, overriding title) = not income; application = taxable.**
8. **Illegal income is taxable** (smuggling, bribes).
9. **HUF/AOP taxed as separate persons**, not the karta/members.
10. **Senior/super-senior higher exemption = OLD regime only** (new regime exemption uniform).
11. **Refund-claimant / loss-return filer IS an assessee.**
12. **Two companies joining = AOP (never BOI); LLP taxed as a firm.**
13. **Basic-exemption adjustment against special-rate gains = resident only, never against 115BB winnings.** NR cannot shelter LTCG with unused exemption.
14. **First PY can be shorter than 12 months, never longer.**
15. **Firms/companies: no slabs, no basic exemption** — applying individual slabs is a structural error.
16. **Capital receipt taxable only if a charging section catches it; revenue receipt taxable unless exempted.**
17. **Special-rate income shown/taxed separately** from slab income; round TI and tax once each.

## One-line recall

- **Persons (7):** Individual, HUF, Company, Firm/LLP, AOP/BOI, Local authority, AJP — humans → families → companies → firms → groups → govt bodies → everything else.
- **Heads (5):** **S**he **H**as **B**ig **C**apital **O**utflows — Salaries(15-17), House Property(22-27), PGBP(28-44), Capital Gains(45-55), Other Sources(56-59, residuary).
- **Same-year tax:** Ships(172) Leaving(174) Associations-for-event(174A) Transferring(175) Discontinuing(176).
- **Stacking:** Slab/special rate → +Surcharge (15% cap on gains/dividends) → −Marginal relief → −87A rebate → +4% Cess → −TDS/advance tax = Tax payable.
- **Sec 4:** FA rates × AY × PY's total income × every person, "subject to the provisions of this Act."
- **Regime:** New (115BAC, default, low rates/few deductions) vs Old (high rates/many deductions) → compute both, choose lower.
