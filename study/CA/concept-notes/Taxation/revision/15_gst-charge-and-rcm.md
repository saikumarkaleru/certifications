# Charge of GST (Levy & Reverse Charge) — Revision Note

## Snapshot
Three answers: **charging section** (Sec 9 CGST / Sec 5 IGST) = the authority (Art. 265: no tax without law); **RCM** = collect from recipient where supplier is untraceable; **composition** = keep small dealers in net without crushing them. Tax never changes — only WHO pays and HOW MUCH paperwork. Read every problem by 3 dials: **territory** (intra/inter), **collector** (FC/RCM/ECO), **regime** (normal/composition).

## Core concepts
**Levy ≠ collection.** Levy (liability) arises the instant a taxable supply happens; collection is the machinery. One levy; three collection routes (forward charge / reverse charge / Sec 9(5) ECO). Calling RCM "a separate tax" is wrong.

## Key provisions / rules

### Sec 9 CGST (mirror = Sec 5 IGST)
| Sub-sec | Content |
|---|---|
| 9(1) | Levy CGST on intra-State supplies, except alcoholic liquor for human consumption, on value u/s 15, rate ≤ **20%**, paid by taxable person (**forward charge**, default) |
| 9(1) proviso / 9(2) | 5 petroleum products levied from date notified (inside GST, not yet notified) |
| 9(3) | RCM on notified goods/services → recipient pays |
| 9(4) | RCM on purchase from unregistered by notified classes (chiefly real-estate promoter: 80% procurement, cement) |
| 9(5) | ECO deemed supplier on notified services |
Rates (0/5/12/18/28 + cess) are NOTIFIED, not in Act. IGST ceiling **40%**; IGST on import of goods collected under Customs Tariff Act. Industrial/denatured alcohol NOT auto-excluded from GST (only alcohol *for human consumption* is out).

### Forward charge — GTA option
GTA default = 9(3) RCM @ 5% (recipient pays, no ITC to GTA on that route). GTA may OPT forward charge @ **12% with full ITC** (Annexure V, per FY) → then recipient does NOT pay RCM.

### RCM (Sec 2(98), 9(3)/(4)/(5)) — key rules
1. Recipient pays in **CASH** (electronic cash ledger); cannot use ITC to PAY RCM; claim ITC after (if used for business). Blocked if only exempt outputs → real cost.
2. **Compulsory registration — Sec 24(iii)**, no threshold.
3. **Self-invoice — Sec 31(3)(f)** when supplier unregistered (+ payment voucher).
4. Composition dealer pays RCM at NORMAL rate, no ITC.
5. TOS different under RCM (goods: earliest of receipt/payment/invoice+30; services: payment/invoice+60).

### Sec 9(3) notified services (Notification 13/2017 — illustrative)
GTA (unless 12% FC) → registered recipient/body corporate/firm/etc.; **legal services** by advocate → any **business entity**; arbitral tribunal → business entity; **sponsorship** → body corporate/firm; Govt services → business entity; **director** (non-employee capacity) → company; insurance agent → insurance co.; recovery agent → bank/NBFC; renting of motor vehicle (fuel incl., not 12%) → body corporate; import of service → Indian recipient.
- Legal/Govt-service RCM: recipient must be **business entity**; below-threshold carve-out (verify).
- Director: salary (employee) = Sch III, outside GST; only sitting fees/commission (independent capacity) → RCM.
Notified goods (9(3)): cashew (unshelled), tobacco leaves, silk yarn, raw cotton — by agriculturist → any registered person; lottery.

### Sec 9(5) ECO deemed supplier vs Sec 52 TCS
- **9(5):** 4 notified services — cab aggregators (Ola/Uber), restaurant via platform (Zomato/Swiggy, not specified premises), unregistered-hotel accommodation, house-keeping — ECO pays WHOLE GST as supplier; actual supplier reports nothing.
- **Sec 52 TCS:** all OTHER e-commerce — seller pays GST; ECO only collects TCS (currently 0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST = **1%**, verify) on net value.
Acid test: is it one of the 4 Sec 9(5) services? Yes → ECO pays; No → seller pays + ECO does TCS.

### Composition (Sec 10)
| Category | CGST+SGST | Total | Base |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manufacturer (not notified goods) | 0.5+0.5 | 1% | Total State turnover |
| Trader | 0.5+0.5 | 1% | **Taxable** State turnover |
| Restaurant (no alcohol) | 2.5+2.5 | 5% | Total State turnover |
| Sec 10(2A) services/mixed | 3+3 | 6% | Turnover; ceiling **₹50 L** |
- **10(1) eligibility:** preceding-FY **aggregate turnover ≤ ₹1.5 cr** (₹75 L special States). Aggregate turnover INCLUDES exempt supplies (all-India, PAN).
- 10(1) permitted extra services: higher of **₹5 L or 10% of turnover**.
- **Two measuring sticks:** eligibility = aggregate turnover (incl. exempt); tax base = State turnover (taxable-only for trader).
- **Conditions (10(2)):** no inter-State OUTWARD supply (inward OK); no supply via TCS e-commerce operator; not dealing in non-leviable goods; not notified excluded manufacturer (ice-cream, pan masala, tobacco, aerated water); not CTP/NRTP; all same-PAN regns opt together; **no ITC, no tax collection, Bill of Supply, quarterly CMP-08, annual GSTR-4, pay RCM at normal rate**.
- **Exit (10(3)/(5)):** option lapses day turnover crosses ceiling — **prospective**; file CMP-04; claim opening-stock ITC via ITC-01. Wrong opt-in → normal tax + penalty (Sec 73/74).

## Worked mini-example
Vega (registered) month: legal fees ₹2,00,000 → RCM 18% = ₹36,000 (cash); GTA freight ₹50,000 (not 12% FC) → RCM 5% = ₹2,500 (cash); stationery ₹40,000 from registered dealer → FC. **RCM cash = ₹38,500** (then claim as ITC). If GTA opted 12%: GTA charges ₹6,000 FC, Vega RCM drops to ₹36,000.

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. RCM CANNOT be paid using ITC — cash only; claim ITC after.
2. RCM recipient must register regardless of turnover (24(iii)).
3. Alcohol (human consumption) permanently out; 5 petro inside but not notified; industrial alcohol not auto-out.
4. Composition dealer: Bill of Supply, no tax collected, "composition taxable person" declaration.
5. Trader 1% on TAXABLE turnover; manufacturer/restaurant on TOTAL turnover.
6. Ceilings: ₹1.5 cr goods / ₹50 L services / ₹75 L special; extra-services limit ₹5 L or 10%.
7. One inter-State OUTWARD supply kills composition (inward purchase OK).
8. Composition dealer still pays RCM at normal rate.
9. GTA RCM only if NOT opted 12% forward.
10. 9(5) ECO deemed supplier ≠ Sec 52 TCS.
11. Composition eligibility (incl. exempt) vs tax base (taxable-only trader) — exempt counted for eligibility, excluded from trader base.
12. Director salary = Sch III (out); sitting fees = 9(3) RCM.
13. Levy ≠ collection; RCM/9(5) not separate taxes.
14. Composition transition = prospective from breach day.

## One-line recall
- Charge: Sec 9 CGST (≤20%), Sec 5 IGST (≤40% + imports); rates notified.
- RCM: recipient pays cash, self-invoice if unregistered supplier, compulsory registration (24(iii)); GTA/legal/director-fees/sponsorship/import etc.
- GTA: RCM 5% no-ITC, or opt FC 12% full-ITC.
- 9(5): ECO pays as supplier (cab/restaurant/hotel/housekeeping); else Sec 52 TCS 1%.
- Composition: ≤₹1.5cr goods (1%), restaurant 5%, services ≤₹50L (6%); no ITC/no collection/Bill of Supply/CMP-08.
- Two sticks: eligibility = aggregate incl. exempt; base = State turnover (trader taxable-only).
