# Chapter 06 — Capital Gains

> All rates, CII values, holding-period slabs, ceilings, and the very availability of indexation change by Finance Act. Verify every number and the applicable AY against current ICAI material.

## Snapshot
- Charge (Sec 45(1)): profits from **transfer** of a **capital asset** are taxed in the **year of transfer** (realisation, not accretion).
- Four gates — must ALL hold: (a) capital asset, (b) transferred, (c) in the previous year, (d) a gain arises. Break one → no capital gain.
- Rationale for a separate head: bunching, inflation (phantom gain), realisation-only, and clean boundary vs PGBP/IFOS.
- Spine: **relief scales with holding period** — short-term = ordinary treatment; long-term = indexation + concessional rate + reinvestment deferral.
- Substance over form both ways: expand charge on disguised cash-outs (2(47), 50C, 50CA); spare disguised non-events (Sec 47).

## Core concepts
- **Capital asset — Sec 2(14):** "property of any kind" (incl. rights: tenancy, route permit, goodwill, booking rights). Excludes (**SPRAG**): Stock-in-trade, Personal effects, Rural agricultural land, Agri (rural), Gold bonds/schemes. Jewellery, art, drawings, paintings, sculptures are NOT personal effects (taxable). Personal-effects test = actual personal use, movable only; a house is immovable → always a capital asset. Agri land **outside India** always a capital asset.
- **Transfer — Sec 2(47):** sale, exchange, relinquishment, extinguishment of rights, compulsory acquisition, conversion into stock-in-trade, allowing possession under part-performance (Sec 53A TPA). Exchange = each side a transfer, FVC = market value of asset received. Part-performance: possession date (not registration) is the transfer date.
- **Sec 47 (NOT transfer):** HUF partition; gift/will/irrevocable trust; holding↔100% subsidiary; amalgamation/demerger to Indian company; bond/debenture→shares of same company; firm/proprietorship→company, company→LLP (all "subject to conditions"). Breach → **Sec 47A** claws gain back onto transferor.
- **Holding period (Sec 2(42A)/2(29A)):** acquisition→transfer; "**more than**" threshold (acquisition date in, transfer date out). Bonus/rights shares: period from **date of allotment**. Gift/inheritance/partition: include **previous owner's period**.
- **Step-into-shoes (Sec 49(1)):** cost = cost to last previous owner who actually paid (trace back through gratuitous links); holding period aggregates the chain. Indexation from previous owner's year (case law *Manjula Shah*; ICAI position — verify).

## Key provisions / rules

**Sec 45 timing sub-charges**

| Sub-sec | Situation | Timing / rule |
|---|---|---|
| 45(1A) | Insurance receipt on damage/destruction | Year money/asset received; FVC = value received |
| 45(2) | Conversion into stock-in-trade | Year converted stock sold; FVC = FMV on conversion date |
| 45(3) | Asset introduced into firm/AOP | FVC = amount recorded in firm's books |
| 45(4) | Reconstitution of firm (post-2021) | Taxed in firm's hands (verify mechanics) |
| 45(5) | Compulsory acquisition + enhanced comp. | Initial comp. taxed year first received; enhanced comp. year of receipt, cost of enhancement = nil |

**Holding-period ladder (LTCA if held MORE than)**

| Asset | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Listed equity / equity units / business-trust units | 12 months |
| Unlisted shares, immovable property (land/building) | 24 months |
| Any other asset (gold, debt, etc.) | 36 months |

**Sec 48 computation**
- STCG = FVC − transfer expenditure − COA − cost of improvement.
- LTCG = FVC − transfer expenditure − **Indexed** COA − **Indexed** cost of improvement.
- Indexed COA = COA × (CII of year of transfer ÷ CII of year of acquisition, or 2001-02 if earlier). Improvement indexed from year incurred. **CII base year 2001-02 = 100.**
- FVC overrides: **Sec 50C** (immovable sold below stamp value beyond tolerance band → stamp value); **Sec 50CA** (unquoted shares → FMV).
- **STT is NOT a deductible transfer expense.**
- Non-residents: no indexation on Indian company shares/debentures bought in forex → **1st proviso Sec 48** forex-fluctuation method (mutually exclusive with indexation).

**Sec 55 — cost rules**

| Asset | Cost of acquisition |
|---|---|
| Bonus shares allotted on/after 1-4-2001 | Nil |
| Bonus shares allotted before 1-4-2001 | FMV as on 1-4-2001 |
| Rights shares subscribed | Amount paid to company |
| Right entitlement (renounced) | Nil to renouncer; buyer = amount to renouncer + to company |
| Self-generated goodwill/tenancy/route permit/brand | Nil (verify current goodwill law) |
| Asset acquired before 1-4-2001 | Higher of actual cost or FMV on 1-4-2001 (option) |

**Special rates**

| Section | Applies to | Rate (verify) |
|---|---|---|
| 111A | STCG on STT-paid listed equity/equity units | Flat concessional (historically 15%) |
| 112A | LTCG on STT-paid listed equity, above annual exemption (historically ₹1 lakh); **no indexation** | Flat concessional; grandfathering FMV 31-Jan-2018 |
| 112 | LTCG on all other assets | Historically 20% with indexation / newer flat rate without |

- 112A needs STT on acquisition AND transfer (exceptions: IPO/bonus/rights). Annual ₹1 lakh exempt slice is per-year, per-assessee; only excess taxed.
- **Chapter VI-A (80C etc.) NOT allowed against 111A/112/112A** income.
- **Basic-exemption shortfall** (residents only) set off against these gains; ₹1 lakh 112A relief applied first (verify ordering).
- Surcharge on 111A/112A capped (historically 15%); 87A rebate availability — verify.

**Sec 51 / 56(2)(ix):** advance forfeited on/after 1-4-2014 → taxed as **IFOS**, no cost reduction; forfeited before → reduce COA when asset sold. Buyer-side under-pricing → **Sec 56(2)(x)**.

**Reinvestment exemptions**

| Sec | Sell (source) | Buy | Who | Exemption = lower of |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 54 | LTCG on residential house | Residential house (India) | Indiv/HUF | Gain OR cost of new house |
| 54F | LTCG on any LTCA except house | Residential house | Indiv/HUF (not own >1 other house) | **Proportionate**: gain × (investment ÷ net sale consideration) |
| 54EC | LTCG on **land/building only** | Specified bonds (NHAI/REC), 5-yr lock | Any | Gain OR invested, **cap ₹50 lakh** |
| 54B | Gain (ST/LT) on agri land used 2 yrs | Other agri land | Indiv/HUF | Gain OR cost |
| 54D | Compulsory acq. of industrial land/building | Land/building to re-establish | Any | Gain OR cost |
| 54G/54GA | Shifting undertaking from urban / to SEZ | New assets at new location | Any | Gain OR cost |

- **54EC:** invest within **6 months**; ₹50 lakh cap is **aggregate across year of transfer + next year** (kills the straddle).
- **Sec 54:** one house only; once-in-a-lifetime **two-house** option if gain ≤ ₹2 crore (verify).
- **54/54F new-asset cost cap = ₹10 crore** (verify).
- **CGAS:** if not reinvested by **return due date**, park unutilised gain in CGAS a/c (use within: buy 2 yrs / construct 3 yrs). Applies to 54, 54B, 54D, 54F, 54G/54GA — **NOT 54EC**. Unutilised balance at window-end → taxable in that year.
- **Clawback:** new house sold within 3 yrs (54/54F) or 54EC bonds transferred/loaned within 5 yrs → exemption withdrawn. Sec 54: reduce new-house COA by exempted gain. Sec 54F: earlier-exempt LTCG revived as LTCG of sale year.

**Set-off (Chapter VI):** STCL → against STCG & LTCG; LTCL → **against LTCG only**; capital losses not set off against other heads; carry forward **8 years** (return filed by Sec 139(1) due date). TDS: 194-IA (1% on immovable ≥ ₹50 lakh), 195 (non-resident). Sec 234C relief on gain arising after instalment date.

## Worked mini-example
LTCG on a **gifted house + Sec 54** (illustrative CII: 2005-06 = 117, 2023-24 = 348). Mr C gets a house as gift (father bought 10-Aug-2005 for ₹12,00,000); sells 15-Jan-2024 for ₹90,00,000 (stamp value ₹92,00,000; gap 2.2% within band → FVC = ₹90,00,000); selling expenses ₹90,000; buys new house for ₹35,00,000.
- Holding period: count **father's** period (Aug-2005) → ~18 yrs → **long-term** (not from gift date).
- COA (Sec 49(1)) = father's ₹12,00,000. Indexed = 12,00,000 × 348 ÷ 117 = ₹35,69,231.
- LTCG before exemption = 90,00,000 − 90,000 − 35,69,231 = **₹53,40,769**.
- Sec 54 exemption = lower of gain ₹53,40,769 or investment ₹35,00,000 = **₹35,00,000**.
- **Taxable LTCG (Sec 112) = ₹18,40,769.** (Investing ≥ whole gain → nil.)

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. Jewellery/art/bullion (incl. puja silver) are NOT personal effects → taxable.
2. Gifted/inherited asset: use previous owner's **holding period AND cost**; trace chain to last purchaser.
3. Rural agri land = outside tax; urban = taxable; use population/**aerial**-distance test; agri land outside India always taxable.
4. Sec 50C: substitute stamp value only if gap **exceeds** tolerance band; land/building only.
5. **54F proportionate; 54 not.** 54F denominator = **net sale consideration**; needs "not own >1 other house."
6. 54EC: ₹50 lakh cap aggregated over 2 years; land/building only; not shares/gold.
7. 80C etc. not deductible against 111A/112/112A.
8. CGAS deadline = **return due date**, not window-end; not for 54EC.
9. Indexation only for long-term (where law allows); never index a Sec 50 STCG.
10. Clawback on early sale of new asset (54: reduce COA; 54F: revive old LTCG).
11. Conversion into stock-in-trade: gain taxed when converted stock sold, FMV on conversion date as FVC; excess over FMV = business income.
12. STT not deductible.
13. Bonus shares (post-1-4-2001): nil cost, holding period from allotment.
14. "More than", not "at least", 12/24/36 months.
15. Sec 45(5): initial comp. year first received; enhanced comp. year of receipt, cost of enhancement nil.
16. **Sec 50 does not always fire:** if block survives and sale value < block WDV → no capital gain, block keeps depreciating.

## One-line recall
- Charge only on **transfer** (Sec 45); define asset (2(14)) and transfer (2(47)) precisely — Sec 47 spares non-events.
- Split short vs long via **12/24/36** ladder; long gains get **111A/112/112A** concessional rates.
- **Indexation** (Sec 48 + CII, base 2001-02 = 100) strips inflation from long-term cost.
- **Reinvest** (54/54F/54EC + 54B/54D/54G family) to defer; guarded by **CGAS parking** and **clawback**.
- STCL vs STCG & LTCG; **LTCL only vs LTCG**; carry forward 8 years if return filed in time.
- Verify every rate, CII, threshold, ceiling, and AY before relying on a number.
