# Chapter 09 — Deductions under Chapter VI-A (Revision Note)

> Illustrative anchors for **AY 2025-26 (PY 2024-25)**. Verify all limits, %, sunset/sanction windows and slab rates in current ICAI material — Finance Acts move these frequently.

## Snapshot

- **Core equation:** Gross Total Income (GTI) − Chapter VI-A deductions = Total Income. Total Income rounded to nearest ₹10 [Sec 288A].
- Deductions reward **application** of income (approved spending/investing), not earning. They act on the **aggregate (GTI)** after all set-offs, never on a single head.
- **Sec 80A(2) ceiling:** total VI-A deductions ≤ GTI. Can zero out Total Income, never below. Excess is lost — not carried forward, not refunded.
- **Two templates:** *Payment* deductions (80C–80GGC, reward money paid out) vs *Income* deductions (80-IA–80U, reward favoured income; **can never exceed that income** in GTI).
- **New regime [115BAC(1A)]** disallows almost all VI-A (bargain = lower rates, no nudges). Survivors: **80CCD(2), 80CCH, 80JJAA**.

## Core concepts

- **GTI [Sec 80B(5)]** = income under 5 heads after intra/inter-head set-off (Chapter VI). Then VI-A applied → Total Income → tax.
- **Five-test checklist for any payment deduction:** (1) Amount paid, (2) Ceiling (own cap → 80CCE umbrella → 80A(2) GTI ceiling), (3) Eligible person (permitted relative — list differs per section), (4) Mode (cash vs non-cash mandatory), (5) Claim (regime; and 80AC timely return for profit-linked).
- **No double deduction [Sec 80A(3),(4)]:** firm and partner cannot both claim; one Part-C (80-IA family) deduction per profit.
- **No deduction against special-rate income [Sec 80A(4)]:** LTCG 112/112A, STCG 111A, winnings 115BB and 115-series are ring-fenced (already given a rate concession). % -of-income bases use **Adjusted GTI / Adjusted Total Income** which strip these out.
- **Adjusted GTI** (for 80G limit) = GTI − LTCG − STCG(111A) − other VI-A deductions − specified incomes. **Adjusted Total Income** (for 80GG) is the analogous base.
- **Five policy families:** (1) Savings/Investment, (2) Health/Disability/Illness, (3) Education/Housing, (4) Donations/Political, (5) Interest/Rent.

## Key provisions / rules

### Structural rules [Sec 80A]

| Rule | Section | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Deduct from GTI only | 80A(1) | Never from a single head |
| Total ≤ GTI | 80A(2) | Cannot create loss/refund |
| No double deduction | 80A(3),(4) | Same amount not twice (firm & partner) |
| Claimed in return | 80A(5)/80AC | Profit-linked (80-IA family) lost if return late [Sec 139(1) due date] |

### Family 1 — Savings & Investment

| Section | Deduction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 80C | ₹1,50,000 | LIC premium, EPF/PPF, 5-yr FD, NSC, ELSS, housing-loan **principal**, tuition (2 kids, India), Sukanya, superannuation |
| 80CCC | within umbrella | Pension/annuity premium; pension **taxable** on receipt (Other Sources) |
| 80CCD(1) | within umbrella | Own NPS: **10% of salary** (salaried) / **20% of GTI** (self-emp) |
| **80CCE umbrella** | **₹1,50,000** | Caps 80C + 80CCC + 80CCD(1) **together** |
| 80CCD(1B) | **+₹50,000** | Extra NPS, **over** umbrella. Same rupee not in both 1 and 1B. Max own-side = ₹2,00,000 |
| 80CCD(2) | Outside caps | Employer NPS. **Survives new regime.** Added to salary then deducted |

- **"Salary" for 80CCD** = basic + DA (to extent DA enters retirement benefits), not gross.
- **80CCD(2) ceiling is regime-dependent:** Old regime = **10% salary** (private) / **14%** (Central/State Govt only). **14% for all (incl. private) only under new regime 115BAC(1A)** (raised from 10% w.e.f. AY 2025-26).
- **LIC premium vs sum assured:** only premium up to **10%** of sum assured qualifies (policies on/after 1-4-2012); **15%** if insured has disability (80U) or disease (80DDB). "Children" for LIC includes married/independent children (relationship, not dependency).
- **Tuition fees:** max **2 children**, India, tuition only (no donation/development/coaching).
- **Housing-loan principal reversed** if house sold within **5 years** of end of year of possession (added back to income). Interest goes to **Sec 24(b)**, not 80C.
- **NSC accrued interest** (except final year) reinvested → separately qualifies for 80C.
- *Hook:* "1.5 shared, 0.5 solo, employer free."

### Family 2 — Health / Disability / Illness

**80D — medical insurance premium:**

| Covered | Base | If senior (60+) |
|---|---|---|
| Self, spouse, dependent children | ₹25,000 | ₹50,000 (if self/spouse senior) |
| Parents (dependent or not) | ₹25,000 | ₹50,000 (if parents senior) |
| Preventive check-up | within above, max ₹5,000 | — |

- **Max 80D = ₹1,00,000** (self+family senior ₹50k + parents senior ₹50k).
- Two **separate** buckets (not one combined ₹25,000). Premium must be **non-cash**; only **preventive check-up allowed in cash**, and it sits **within** (never on top).
- Single-premium multi-year policy → **apportioned** (total ÷ years), each year capped by annual ceiling.
- **Parents bucket has no dependency test**, but **parents-in-law excluded**.
- **Senior (60+) with no insurance** → actual **medical expenditure up to ₹50,000** (cash permitted). Non-seniors cannot use this route.

**Disability/illness sections:**

| Feature | 80DD | 80DDB | 80U |
|---|---|---|---|
| For whose condition | Dependant's disability | Self/dependant specified disease (Rule 11DD) | Own disability |
| Amount | Flat ₹75,000 / ₹1,25,000 (severe ≥80%) | Actual or ₹40,000 (₹1,00,000 senior), lower | Flat ₹75,000 / ₹1,25,000 |
| Actual spend based? | No (flat) | Yes | No (flat) |
| Reduced by reimbursement? | No | **Yes** | No |
| Certificate | Form 10-IA | Specialist prescription | Form 10-IA |

- 80DD "dependant" = spouse/children/parents/siblings wholly or mainly dependent. Cannot claim **both 80DD and 80U for same person**.
- *Hook:* 80D = doctor's cover, 80DD = dependant's disability, 80DDB = big disease, 80U = yourself.

### Family 3 — Education / Housing

| Section | Deduction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 80E | Full **interest**, **no cap** | Education loan (self/spouse/children/ward). **8 years** from repayment start (or until interest fully paid, earlier). Bank/notified institution/approved charity only. Higher education = any course after Class 12 (incl. vocational). Interest only, not principal |
| 80EE | ₹50,000 | First home, sanction FY 2016-17. Stacks on Sec 24(b) ₹2 lakh |
| 80EEA | up to ₹1,50,000 | Sanction FY 2019-20 to 31-3-2022, stamp value ≤ ₹45 lakh, no other house. Stacks on 24(b). *Verify sunset* |
| 80EEB | ₹1,50,000 | EV-loan interest, sanction 1-4-2019 to 31-3-2023. *Closed after window* |

- EE/EEA: exhaust Sec 24(b) ₹2 lakh **first**, then claim balance; same interest rupee not double-counted.

### Family 4 — Donations / Political

**80G — four buckets:**

| Category | % | Qualifying limit? |
|---|---|---|
| PM National Relief Fund, National Defence Fund, PM CARES | 100% | No limit |
| PM Drought Relief Fund, certain notified funds | 50% | No limit |
| Govt/local for family planning, Indian Olympic Assn | 100% | **Yes — 10% of Adjusted GTI** |
| Other approved charities, any govt for charity | 50% | **Yes — 10% of Adjusted GTI** |

- **80G 4-step algorithm:** (1) Bucket each donation; (2) process **no-limit** donations fully first (never compete for cap); (3) pool with-limit donations, cap pool at **10% of Adjusted GTI**; (4) within cap apply priority **100%-with-limit first, then 50%-with-limit**, then apply the %.
- Cash donation **> ₹2,000 disallowed**. **In-kind (goods) donations disallowed** — must be money.
- **80GGA** — scientific research/rural development, for taxpayers with **no business income** (business uses Sec 35). Full deduction; cash > ₹2,000 disallowed.
- **80GGB (Indian companies) / 80GGC (any other person)** — political parties/electoral trusts. **100%, non-cash only — zero cash tolerance** (the ₹2,000 tolerance does NOT apply).
- *Hook:* G = charity, GGA = research, GGB = companies to politics, GGC = citizens to politics.

### Family 5 — Interest / Rent

| Section | Deduction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 80TTA | ₹10,000 | Savings interest only (bank/co-op/PO), **not FD**. Individual/HUF **< 60**. Type B — can't exceed actual interest in GTI |
| 80TTB | ₹50,000 | **Resident seniors (60+)**, covers savings **+ FD +** PO/co-op. NRI senior excluded |
| 80GG | Least of 3 | Rent, **no HRA**, no house owned (self/spouse/minor child/HUF) at workplace/residence |

- **80TTA and 80TTB mutually exclusive** — senior uses 80TTB, not 80TTA.
- **80GG least of:** (i) ₹5,000/month (₹60,000/yr); (ii) 25% of Adjusted Total Income; (iii) rent − 10% of Adjusted Total Income.

### Other specials

| Section | Deduction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 80JJAA | **30% of additional employee cost × 3 AYs** | New-job creation. **Survives new regime.** Needs 80AC timely return. Emoluments ≤ ₹25,000/mo, min days, banking channel |
| 80QQB / 80RRB | **₹3,00,000 each** | Author royalty / patent royalty. Type B — needs royalty in income. Authors: lump-sum has 15%-of-value restriction |
| 80CCH | Agnipath Scheme contribution | **Survives new regime** |

### New regime [Sec 115BAC(1A)]

| Deduction | Allowed in new regime? |
|---|---|
| 80C, 80D, 80E, 80G, 80TTA/TTB, 80GG, 80DD, 80U, 80DDB… | **No** |
| 80CCD(2) employer NPS | **Yes** |
| 80CCH Agnipath | **Yes** |
| 80JJAA new-employee cost | **Yes** |

- New regime is **default**; opt out for old regime by return-filing due date. **Business/professional income = one-way switch** (Form 10-IEA, *verify procedure*); **salaried without business = choose afresh every year**.
- **Standard deduction [Sec 16(ia)] is NOT VI-A and IS allowed in new regime** — don't add it back.

## Worked mini-example

Mr. Iyer (40, resident, old regime), PY 2024-25:
Salary ₹8,00,000; House Property ₹1,20,000; LTCG 112A ₹2,00,000; SB interest ₹14,000 → **GTI = ₹11,34,000**.
Payments: PPF ₹1,20,000, LIC ₹50,000, NPS own ₹40,000, health insurance (cheque) ₹22,000, education-loan interest ₹90,000, PM National Relief Fund ₹30,000, local trust ₹50,000 (all cheque).

| Section | Working | Deduction |
|---|---|---|
| 80C | PPF 1,20,000 + LIC 50,000 = 1,70,000, capped 80CCE | ₹1,50,000 |
| 80CCD(1B) | NPS 40,000 (exclusive window) | ₹40,000 |
| 80D | 22,000 < 25,000, cheque | ₹22,000 |
| 80E | interest, no cap | ₹90,000 |
| 80TTA | SB 14,000 capped at 10,000 | ₹10,000 |
| 80G | see below | ₹55,000 |

**80G:** PM National Relief Fund = 100%, no limit → ₹30,000.
Adjusted GTI = 11,34,000 − 2,00,000 (LTCG) − (1,90,000 + 22,000 + 90,000 + 10,000) = **₹6,22,000**.
Qualifying limit = 10% × 6,22,000 = ₹62,200. Trust ₹50,000 ≤ cap → 50% × 50,000 = ₹25,000.
Total 80G = 30,000 + 25,000 = **₹55,000**.

**Total VI-A = ₹3,67,000** (≤ GTI, 80A(2) OK). **Total Income = 11,34,000 − 3,67,000 = ₹7,67,000.**
LTCG ₹2,00,000 stays inside Total Income, taxed at special 112A rate; VI-A cannot offset it (hence stripped from Adjusted GTI).

*80A(2) biting (Mr. Sethi, GTI ₹2,20,000):* raw deductions ₹2,90,000 exceed GTI → **restricted to ₹2,20,000**, Total Income = **NIL**. Excess ₹70,000 lost (not carried forward).

## Exam traps & must-remember

1. **₹1.5 lakh "each" illusion:** 80C/80CCC/80CCD(1) **share** one ₹1.5 lakh (80CCE). Only 80CCD(1B) ₹50k is genuinely extra.
2. **Route NPS to 80CCD(1B)** when 80C already ≥ ₹1.5 lakh.
3. **80D single-bucket error:** self-family and parents are two separate limits; senior = ₹50,000 not ₹25,000; preventive ≤ ₹5,000 is within, not on top; parents-in-law excluded.
4. **80TTA vs 80TTB:** senior gets 80TTB (₹50,000, incl. FD), never both; FD interest never qualifies for 80TTA; Type B ≤ actual interest.
5. **80G on plain GTI** instead of **Adjusted GTI** — classic error.
6. **80G priority-order:** 100%-with-limit claims cap first, then 50%-with-limit.
7. **Cash-donation trap:** 80G cash > ₹2,000 = zero; 80GGB/GGC any cash = zero; in-kind = zero.
8. **80E:** interest only, no cap, 8-year limit; bank/notified institution not relative/employer.
9. **No deduction against special-rate income** (LTCG/STCG-111A/lottery).
10. **80A(2) ceiling:** deductions cut to GTI; no negative Total Income, no carry-forward.
11. **New-regime blindness:** only 80CCD(2), 80CCH, 80JJAA survive; but standard deduction (Sec 16) stays.
12. **Rounding:** Total Income to nearest ₹10 (Sec 288A), after VI-A.
13. **80DD + 80U** both for same person — not allowed.
14. **LIC premium > 10% (or 15%) of sum assured** — excess not eligible under 80C.
15. **80C reversal on house sale within 5 years** — principal added back.
16. **80CCD(2) double-count:** added to salary as inclusion, then deducted.
17. **80DDB reimbursement:** reduce claim by insurance/employer reimbursement.
18. **80AC timely-return:** profit-linked (80-IA/IB/IE, 80JJAA) wholly lost if return after Sec 139(1) due date; ordinary 80C-type not affected.

## One-line recall

- **GTI − VI-A = Total Income**; deductions ≤ GTI [80A(2)]; round to ₹10 [288A].
- **Family 1:** ₹1.5 lakh umbrella (80CCE) + ₹50k NPS solo (80CCD-1B) + employer NPS free (80CCD-2, survives new regime).
- **Family 2:** 80D two buckets (₹25k/₹50k senior, max ₹1L); 80DD/80U flat ₹75k/₹1.25L; 80DDB actual or ₹40k/₹1L less reimbursement.
- **Family 4:** 80G = 100%/50%, some capped at 10% Adjusted GTI (no-limit first, then 100%-with-limit, then 50%); cash > ₹2,000 & in-kind disallowed; political (80GGB/GGC) non-cash only.
- **Family 5:** 80TTA ₹10k (savings, <60) vs 80TTB ₹50k (resident senior, incl FD); 80GG rent least-of-three.
- **New regime** blocks nearly all VI-A → survivors 80CCD(2), 80CCH, 80JJAA; always compute tax both ways.
