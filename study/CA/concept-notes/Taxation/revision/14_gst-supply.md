# Supply under GST — Revision Note

## Snapshot
GST replaces manufacture/sale/service with ONE taxable event: **supply** (Sec 7, CGST Act) read with Schedules I, II, III and Sec 8 (bundles). No supply → no GST, however much money moves. Golden line: "No supply, no GST."

## Core concepts
**Upstream filter (before Sec 7):** everything is *goods* [2(52): every movable property, incl. actionable claims, growing crops/severable things; **excl. money & securities**] OR *services* [2(102): anything else; incl. use/conversion of money for a separate consideration]. Money & securities = NEITHER → trading is outside GST, but the fee/commission/conversion charge IS a taxable service.

## Key provisions / rules

### Sec 7(1) — the four gates
| Clause | Captures | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 7(1)(a) | Forms: sale, transfer, barter, exchange, licence, rental, lease, disposal | **C**onsideration **AND** course/furtherance of **B**usiness ("C+B") |
| 7(1)(b) | Import of services | For consideration; business NOT needed |
| 7(1)(c) + Sch I | Schedule I activities | WITHOUT consideration (anti-avoidance) |

Four-quadrant shortcut: price+business → 7(1)(a); price, no business → only 7(1)(b); no price, business → only Sch I; no price, no business → never a supply.

**Sec 7(1A) + Sch II** — once a supply, classify goods vs services (post-2018: Sch II is CLASSIFIER only, not a gate).
**Sec 7(2) + Sch III** — non-obstante clause; overrides 7(1). Test Sch III FIRST. **Sec 7(3)** — Govt may notify goods↔services.

### Key definitions
- **Consideration [2(31)]:** money/act/forbearance, by recipient or any other person. Govt subsidy EXCLUDED; deposit NOT consideration until supplier appropriates it (tax on appropriation, not receipt).
- **Business [2(17)]:** very wide; incl. one-off adventures; whether or not for pecuniary benefit, volume, frequency. Test attaches to transaction's purpose, not person's occupation.
- **Distinct persons [25(4)/(5)]:** same PAN, different GSTIN (or 2 regns in 1 State) → separate persons (makes branch transfers taxable).
- **Related persons [Expl. to Sec 15]:** control/holding ≥25%/family/employer-employee/partners/sole-agent-distributor.

### Schedule I — supply WITHOUT consideration (4 entries)
| # | Deemed supply | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Permanent transfer/disposal of business assets where **ITC availed** | No ITC availed → NOT covered |
| 2 | Supply between **related/distinct persons** in course of business | Except employer→employee gift ≤ ₹50,000/FY |
| 3 | Principal ↔ agent (agent handles goods under own invoice) | Pure commission agent (arranges only) NOT covered |
| 4 | Import of services from related person / own foreign establishment, in business | Mirrors 7(1)(b) for free related case |

### Schedule II — goods vs services
Title/ownership passes → **goods** (incl. hire-purchase). Right-to-use/activity/forbearance → **services**. Declared **services**: works contract [2(119)], restaurant supply, lease/tenancy/licence of immovable property, renting immovable property, job work, temporary transfer of IPR, IT software development, agreeing to refrain/tolerate/do an act. Construction for sale = service (except entire consideration received AFTER completion certificate → then out).

### Schedule III — NOT a supply ("ELF-CAG")
**E**mployment (employee→employer), **L**and & completed buildings (sale), **F**uneral/burial/mortuary, **C**ourt/Tribunal + constitutional/MP/MLA functions, **A**ctionable claims (except lottery, betting, gambling + notified online gaming), **G**oods outside India / high-sea sales / in-bond sales before home consumption. Actionable claim chain: 2(52) puts in → Sch III takes ordinary ones out → lottery/betting/gambling stays in.

### Sec 8 — bundles (precondition: single price + ≥2 different supplies)
| Type | Definition | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Composite [2(30)] | Naturally bundled, one **principal supply** [2(90)] | Rate of PRINCIPAL supply [8(a)] — "Composite = Core" |
| Mixed [2(74)] | Single price, NOT naturally bundled | HIGHEST rate [8(b)] — "Mixed = Max" |
Separately priced → not Sec 8 (each at own rate). One type only → plain single supply. Natural-bundle indicators: recipient perception, industry practice, main-element reason, can't be supplied separately.

## Worked mini-example
Gift hamper ₹2,000: snacks 12% + chocolate 18% + aerated drink 28%, single price → **mixed** → 28% → GST ₹560. Laptop + carry-bag single ₹50,000 → **composite** (laptop principal) → 18% → ₹9,000. Same laptop+bag separately priced (₹47,000+₹3,000) → not Sec 8 → 8,460+840 = ₹9,300.

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. "No money → no GST" wrong: check Schedule I (branch/related/ITC-availed disposal/free import from parent).
2. Import of service for personal use IS a supply (7(1)(b) drops business requirement).
3. Post-2018 Sch II only classifies goods/services; cross 7(1) gate first.
4. Composite vs mixed: ask "sold separately in normal course?"
5. Sale of land/completed building → Sch III (out); under-construction flat (before completion cert / part payment) → service, taxed.
6. Employee salary + contractual perquisites → Sch III; gift > ₹50,000 → Sch I supply.
7. Actionable claims out EXCEPT lottery/betting/gambling (+ notified gaming).
8. Distinct persons apply even within same State (2 regns).
9. Govt subsidy excluded from consideration; non-govt subsidy included.
10. Works contract & restaurant = declared services by Sch II — don't run composite/mixed test.
11. Securities/money trade outside GST; only brokerage/commission/conversion fee taxable.
12. Deposit taxed only on appropriation, not receipt.
13. Distinct persons (regn, Sec 25) ≠ related persons (relationship, Sec 15).
14. High-sea/in-bond sale not taxed twice; IGST once at import stage.

## One-line recall
- Supply [7] = (a) forms for consideration + business (C+B); (b) import of service for consideration (no business); (c) Sch I without consideration.
- Sch II = classifier (7(1A)); Sch III = NOT supply "ELF-CAG" (7(2)).
- Schedule I = 4 no-consideration supplies (ITC-availed disposal; related/distinct; principal↔agent; import from foreign establishment).
- Bundles: Composite → principal rate 8(a); Mixed → highest rate 8(b); separately priced → out of Sec 8.
- Goods/services exhaustive & mutually exclusive; money & securities are neither (fee is a service).
