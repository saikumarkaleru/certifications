# Advance Tax, TDS & TCS

> Figures reflect AY 2025-26. 194-series rates/thresholds change every Finance Act — verify against current ICAI material.

## Snapshot
Three mechanisms to collect tax *as/where income arises* instead of a delayed lump sum. Advance Tax = taxpayer prepays own estimate (Sec 207–211, 234B, 234C). TDS = payer deducts before paying (Sec 192–196). TCS = seller collects extra from buyer (Sec 206C). All prepaid tax shows in Form 26AS/AIS; final settlement via self-assessment tax (Sec 140A). Deductor needs **TAN** (Sec 203A); exception — 194IA/194IB buyers use PAN + challan-cum-statement.

## Core concepts
- "Pay-as-you-earn" (advance tax) and "pay-where-you-earn" (TDS/TCS by third party).
- Deduct vs Deposit = two separate defaults, two separate interest rates (1% vs 1.5%).
- TDS is on **gross** at a crude rate (deductor can't know net); taxpayer reclaims excess via return or Sec 197 certificate.
- Rate roughly tracks expected effective tax: contractor 1–2% (turnover), professional 10% (pure income), salary = slab, lottery 30% (max flat).

## Key provisions / rules

### Advance Tax
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Sec 208 threshold | Payable if estimated tax liability **≥ ₹10,000** (after reducing TDS/TCS deductible) |
| Sec 207(2) exemption | **Resident** individual **60+** with **NO PGBP income** → fully exempt. (Non-resident senior NOT exempt; senior with business NOT exempt) |
| Sec 209 base | Tax on estimated TI + surcharge + 4% cess − rebate 87A − TDS/TCS deductible = advance tax |
| Sec 211 instalments (all) | **15% / 45% / 75% / 100%** by **15 Jun / 15 Sep / 15 Dec / 15 Mar** |
| Presumptive 44AD/44ADA | **100% by 15 March** (single instalment) — only for 44AD/44ADA income; other heads follow normal schedule |
| Payment up to 31 March | Still counts as advance tax (reduces 234B base) though late for 234C |

**Sec 234B** (default in payment): trigger — advance tax paid **< 90%** of assessed tax. Rate **1% p.m.** (part = full). Period: **1 April of AY** to date of determination/payment. Base: shortfall (assessed tax − advance tax paid), rounded down to ₹100. Assessed tax = tax on TI − TDS/TCS − relief 89/90/90A/91 − MAT/AMT credit 115JAA/115JD.

**Sec 234C** (deferment/timing): trigger — instalment short of required cumulative %. Rate **1% p.m.**, period **3 months** (Jun/Sep/Dec) / **1 month** (Mar). Safe harbour: **12%** by Jun, **36%** by Sep escapes 234C (but once failed, shortfall measured against full 15%/45%). Carve-out: no 234C on **capital gains / casual (lottery) / dividend (not 2(22)(e)) / first-time business income** if tax paid in instalment(s) after income arose (or by 31 Mar). 44AD/44ADA: bites only if 100% not paid by 15 Mar.

Both 234B and 234C can apply together (different failures).

- Timing (non-salary): **credit OR payment, whichever earlier** (suspense a/c = credit). Salary: payment only, average rate.
- Sec 192(2B): employee reports other income/TDS + only **loss from house property** to employer. Two employers → Form 12B.
- Sec 197 lower/nil certificate (Form 13). Sec 197A self-declaration: **Form 15H** (senior), **Form 15G** (others).
- Sec 206AA (no PAN): higher of normal rate **or 20%**. Sec 206AB (non-filer): higher of **2× normal / 5%**. Both apply → higher result.
- **Deposit:** April–Feb by **7th of next month**; **March by 30 April**; 194IA/IB/M/S = challan-cum-statement within **30 days** of month-end.
- Certificates: Form 16 (salary, by 15 Jun), 16A (non-salary quarterly, 15 days of return due date), 16B/C/D/E (194IA/IB/M/S). Returns: 24Q/26Q/27Q (Q1 31Jul, Q2 31Oct, Q3 31Jan, Q4 31May).

**TDS default:** Sec 201(1A) interest — **1% p.m.** (not deducted) / **1.5% p.m.** (deducted not deposited). Sec 40(a)(ia): **30% expense disallowed** (100% for non-resident 40(a)(i)); reversed when TDS paid. 234E fee ₹200/day (capped at TDS); 271H penalty ₹10k–₹1L (nil if paid + filed within 1 yr); 271C penalty = tax not deducted; 276B prosecution (3 mo–7 yr). Relief: Sec 201 proviso — not assessee-in-default if resident payee filed return + included income + paid tax (CA cert **Form 26A**); but 201(1A) interest still runs to payee's filing date.

### TCS (Sec 206C) — collect on debit/receipt whichever earlier
Liquor 1%, timber/forest 2.5%, scrap 1%, tendu leaves 5%, minerals 1%, parking/toll/quarry 2%, motor vehicle > ₹10L 1%. **206C(1H)** sale of goods 0.1% (seller T/O > 10cr, buyer receipts > 50L). **206C(1G)** LRS/tour package above 7L: 5% (20% tour/higher LRS). Deposit within **7 days** of month-end; return **27EQ**, certificate **27D**; interest 1% p.m. (206C(7)); penalty 271CA.

**194Q vs 206C(1H):** buyer's TDS (194Q) **prevails**; seller collects TCS only if buyer not liable. Test buyer first.

## Worked mini-example
Assessed tax (net of TDS) = ₹4,00,000; **no** advance tax paid; entire amount as self-assessment on 15 July.
- **234C**: shortfalls 60k/1,80k/3,00k/4,00k → 60,000×1%×3=1,800 + 1,80,000×1%×3=5,400 + 3,00,000×1%×3=9,000 + 4,00,000×1%×1=4,000 = **₹20,200**.
- **234B**: paid 0% (<90%) → on ₹4,00,000, 1 Apr–15 Jul = 4 months → 4,00,000×1%×4 = **₹16,000**.
- Total interest = **₹36,200**. Both apply (234C = missed instalments; 234B = unpaid past 31 Mar).

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. Senior exemption needs **BOTH** resident AND no business income; non-resident senior never exempt.
2. 234C safe harbour 12%/36% only decides *whether* interest triggers; shortfall then measured vs full 15%/45%.
3. 234B is **90%** cutoff — paying 89% triggers it on entire shortfall.
4. 194C: 1% ind/HUF, 2% others; thresholds 30k single OR 1L aggregate. 194I: 2% P&M, 10% others.
5. "Credit OR payment whichever earlier" — salary is exception (payment only).
6. Deduct vs deposit interest: **1% vs 1.5%**.
7. No PAN → higher of normal or **20%** (206AA).
8. March TDS deposit = **30 April** (not 7th); 194IA/IB/M/S = 30-day window.
9. TCS **added** to buyer's price; TDS **deducted** from recipient's payment.
10. Form 15H = senior, 15G = others.
11. "Assessed tax" for 234B is **net of TDS**.
12. 194Q beats 206C(1H); test buyer's liability first.
13. Any advance tax up to **31 March** counts for 234B even if late for 234C.

## One-line recall
- Advance tax ≥ ₹10,000; 15-45-75-100 on 15 Jun/Sep/Dec/Mar; presumptive 100% by 15 Mar.
- 234B: <90% assessed tax → 1% p.m. from 1 April AY. 234C: short instalment → 1% p.m. ×3/×1, safe harbour 12%/36%.
- 201(1A): 1% not deducted / 1.5% not deposited; 40(a)(ia) disallows 30%.
- No PAN → 20% (206AA); non-filer → 2×/5% (206AB).
- TCS deposit 7 days, return 27EQ; TDS deposit 7th/30-Apr, returns 24Q/26Q/27Q.
- 194Q (buyer TDS) prevails over 206C(1H) (seller TCS).
