# Exemptions from GST — Revision Note

## Snapshot
A consumption tax is regressive; exemption is the release valve for essentials, merit goods (health/education) and small suppliers. Exemption is **supply-based** (removes a supply regardless of who); distinguish from **threshold** (Sec 22, person-based) and **composition** (Sec 10, person-based, flat rate). Golden line: **Exempt = tax dies AND credit dies; Zero-rated = tax dies BUT credit lives.**

## Core concepts
Exemption breaks the ITC chain → mid-chain exempt supplier absorbs blocked input tax as cost → next taxable runner taxes an inflated price → **cascading sneaks back**; a mid-chain exemption can RAISE total tax. Therefore exemptions kept **short and near the final/consumer stage**.

### Four "no-tax" devices — different ITC consequences
| Device | Is it "supply"? | "Exempt supply" 2(47)? | ITC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Schedule III (salary, sale of land) | **No** | No | No (not a supply) |
| Non-taxable 2(78) (alcohol, petro) | Yes | Yes (included) | Blocked |
| Exempt / nil (Sec 11 / schedule) | Yes | Yes | Blocked |
| Zero-rated (export/SEZ, Sec 16 IGST) | Yes | **No** | **Preserved/refundable** |

## Key provisions / rules

### Power to exempt — Sec 11 CGST / Sec 6 IGST
| Sub | Content |
|---|---|
| 11(1) | General exemption by **notification**; wholly/partly; absolute/conditional; needs **Council recommendation + public interest** |
| 11(2) | **Special order** (ad-hoc) under exceptional circumstances |
| 11(3) | **Explanation** within **1 year**, effect **retrospective** |
Notification prospective from stated date unless provided. Absolute exemption = compulsory (can't opt to pay).

### Definitions
- **Exempt supply — 2(47):** (a) nil-rated, OR (b) **wholly** exempt (Sec 11/6), AND includes non-taxable supply. "Wholly" is load-bearing: **partly** exempt (concessional rate) = taxable at lower rate → ITC fully AVAILABLE.
- **Non-taxable supply — 2(78):** GST not leviable at all (alcohol; current petro).
- **Aggregate turnover — 2(6):** taxable + exempt + exports + inter-State (PAN, all-India); **excludes** GST and inward RCM value.

### Key notifications (verify)
Goods rates 1/2017-CT(R); exempt goods 2/2017-CT(R); service rates 11/2017-CT(R); **exempt services 12/2017-CT(R)**.
Read a 12/2017 entry via 4-part test: **supplier / recipient / description / condition** — all must align; examiner breaks exactly one.

### Absolute vs conditional
- **Absolute** (no condition): mandatory; can't charge tax; wrong tax charged → recipient's ITC **ineligible** (dead credit). E.g. transmission/distribution of electricity, RBI services, public conveniences.
- **Conditional:** exempt only if condition met (miss → taxable). E.g. hotel tariff limit; GTA consignment value; charitable activities (Sec 12AA/12AB Income-tax + defined activities).

### The Big Four — ITC treatment
| Concept | Leviable? | ITC | In aggregate turnover? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nil-rated | Yes (rate nil) | Blocked | Yes |
| Exempt (Sec 11/6) | Yes | Blocked | Yes |
| Non-taxable (2(78)) | No | Blocked | Yes (is exempt supply) |
| Zero-rated (Sec 16 IGST) | Yes, @0 with credit | **Allowed/refundable** | Yes |
Zero-rating (exports/SEZ): LUT/bond without payment + refund of unutilised ITC, OR pay IGST + claim refund. Destination principle: "don't export our taxes." **Export of an exempt good → zero-rated** → ITC unlocks (domestic sale blocks it).

### Key exempt clusters (why → what)
- **Health care (merit/equity):** clinical establishment/practitioner/para-medics; ambulance; in-patient package (room+meds+food per doctor) = composite exempt. NOT exempt: cosmetic/plastic surgery, hair transplant, room rent above notified limit (commonly >₹5,000/day excl. ICU), food to attendants/visitors.
- **Education:** institution to students/faculty/staff (pre-school–higher secondary / recognised qualification / approved vocational); input services (transport, catering incl. mid-day meals, security, cleaning, admission/exam) **only up to higher secondary**; entrance exam by institution. NOT exempt: private coaching, placement, sale of uniforms/books by third parties.
- **Agriculture:** cultivation/harvesting/threshing/plant protection/farm labour/warehousing/commission agent; unbranded grains, fresh produce, milk. **"Agricultural produce" narrow** — warehousing/loading of **processed** produce (tea, coffee, jaggery, milled pulses, sugar) = TAXABLE.
- **Transport:** non-AC public passenger (stage/contract carriage, metro, vessel, metered auto/e-rickshaw); goods by road (except GTA/courier) & inland waterways; specified goods by GTA/rail; GTA value limits (e.g. ₹750 single consignee / ₹1,500 single carriage — verify); electricity; tolls. Taxable: AC contract carriage, radio taxi, app cabs.
- **Others:** charitable (12AA/12AB, defined activities only); religious ceremonies + precinct rent within limits; **interest on loans/deposits exempt but fees taxable**; residential dwelling as residence (RCM if registered recipient); Govt/RBI/SEBI/IRDA services; legal services (individual advocate to non-business / small business — else RCM); event admission below limit.

### Registration interplay
- Only exempt supplies → **Sec 23(1)(a): not liable to register**, however large.
- Mixed supplies → all turnover (incl. exempt) counts for threshold.
- **Sec 24 RCM liability overrides Sec 23 shelter** (verify current wording).
- Consequences: ITC blocked (17(2)); common ITC reversed (Rule 42/43); switch taxable⇄exempt → Sec 18(1) claim / 18(4) reversal; issue **bill of supply** (31(3)(c)); **no tax collected** (Sec 32).

## Worked mini-example
Aggregate turnover: taxable goods ₹28 L + exempt pulses ₹9 L + alcohol (non-taxable) ₹6 L = **₹43 L** > ₹40 L → **must register** (inward RCM ₹3 L excluded). Exempt + non-taxable push him over even though no GST owed. (If only exempt/non-taxable, Sec 23 exempts registration.)

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. "Exempt = good for everyone" false; blocks ITC, can raise total tax.
2. Nil-rated ≠ Zero-rated — nil blocks ITC; zero-rated refunds it.
3. Non-taxable is STILL an "exempt supply" (2(47)) → counts in turnover, blocks ITC.
4. Absolute exemption mandatory; wrong tax charged → recipient ITC dead.
5. Conditional exemption tested per unit/limit (hotel/GTA/charity/event).
6. Coaching ≠ education (no recognised qualification) → taxable.
7. Interest exempt, fees taxable — split the bill.
8. Bill of supply, no tax collected (Sec 32) on exempt.
9. Rule 42 reversal compulsory the moment ANY exempt output exists.
10. "Wholly" vs "partly" — partly exempt = taxable at lower rate → ITC allowed.
11. Schedule III ≠ exempt (not a supply); doesn't enter 2(47).
12. "Agricultural produce" narrow — processed produce taxable.
13. In-patient hospital package exempt; canteen food to visitors taxable.
14. Export of exempt good → zero-rated refund.
15. Sec 23 shelter lost if Sec 24/RCM liability arises.

## One-line recall
- Sec 11 CGST/6 IGST: 11(1) notification, 11(2) special order, 11(3) explanation retrospective (1 yr).
- Exempt supply 2(47) = nil + wholly exempt + non-taxable; all block ITC & count in turnover.
- Big Four: nil/exempt/non-taxable block ITC; only zero-rated (Sec 16 IGST) preserves/refunds.
- Absolute = mandatory (wrong tax = dead ITC); conditional = test limit; partly exempt = still taxable, ITC allowed.
- Clusters: health, education (up to higher secondary), agriculture (primary produce), transport (non-AC public), interest, charitable (12AA/AB).
- Only-exempt → no registration (Sec 23); RCM (Sec 24) overrides.
