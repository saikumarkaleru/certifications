# Input Tax Credit (ITC) — Secs 16–21, Rules 36–45

## Snapshot
ITC = GST paid on purchases (inputs, input services, capital goods) set off against GST on sales, so each business is taxed only on its own value-add (cures cascading). ITC is a conditional privilege, not a right — default is no credit; earn it by proof. High-yield: Sec 16/17/18 conditions, blocked credits, utilisation order, reversal formulae.

## Core concepts
- Three species of input tax [Sec 2(62)]: CGST/SGST/UTGST/IGST charged on supply to you + IGST on imports + RCM tax. Excludes composition tax and penalty.
- Inputs [2(59)] & input services [2(60)] → full instant credit. Capital goods [2(19), capitalised in books] → full upfront credit unless Rule 43 common-use applies; subject to depreciation bar (16(3)) and clawback on sale (18(6)).
- Input vs capital good = whether capitalised in books (not durability).
- "Intended to be used" → credit on receipt, not deferred to consumption (hence reversal machinery).

## Key provisions / rules

### Sec 16(1) gateway — R-I-B: Registered person + Input tax + Business/taxable use
Composition dealer & unregistered person get NO ITC.

### Sec 16(2) — four cumulative conditions (non-obstante; PRTR)
| # | Condition | Section |
|---|---|---|
| P | Possess valid tax invoice / debit note / bill of entry / ISD invoice | 16(2)(a) |
| — | Invoice furnished by supplier in GSTR-1 & appears in GSTR-2B | 16(2)(aa) + Rule 36(4) |
| R | Goods/services actually received (bill-to-ship-to deemed receipt; lots → credit on last lot) | 16(2)(b) |
| T | Tax actually paid to govt by supplier | 16(2)(c) |
| R | Recipient filed GSTR-3B | 16(2)(d) |

Plus 5th cross-cutting: pay supplier value + tax within 180 days [2nd proviso + Rule 37] else reverse ITC + interest; re-avail on payment (no fresh time bar). Proportionate on part-payment. NOT for RCM or Schedule I (no-consideration) supplies.

- Rule 37A: supplier filed GSTR-1 but not GSTR-3B (tax unpaid) by 30 Sep → recipient reverses by 30 Nov; re-avail when supplier pays.
- Sec 41: self-assessed credit reversed (+interest) if supplier hasn't paid; re-avail on payment.
- GSTR-2A (dynamic) vs GSTR-2B (static snapshot ~14th) — 16(2)(aa)/Rule 36(4) keyed to 2B. Provisional buffer (20%→10%→5%) now removed; strict to 2B.

### Sec 16(3) depreciation bar
No ITC on tax component if income-tax depreciation claimed on that tax. (Capitalise net of GST, claim ITC.)

### Sec 16(4) time limit
ITC on invoice/DN for a FY claimable up to earlier of 30 Nov following FY OR annual return date. Keyed to invoice's FY. Carve-outs (no bar): re-availment under Rule 37/37A/Sec 41; debit notes run on own FY; Sec 18(1) uses separate 1-year bar.

### Sec 17(1)/(2) apportionment
17(1) partly non-business → business portion only. 17(2) partly exempt → taxable portion only.
Exempt supply widened [17(3)]: includes RCM-outward, sale of land, sale of completed building, securities (value 1% of sale). EXCLUDES zero-rated/exports. Bank/NBFC [17(4)]: option flat 50% of eligible ITC, irrevocable for FY (not on intra-entity supplies between own registrations).

### Rules 42 & 43
Rule 42 (inputs/input services, monthly):
- C2 = T − (T1 non-business + T2 exempt + T3 blocked) − T4 (exclusive taxable)
- D1 = C2 × E/F (E=exempt T/O, F=total T/O); D2 = 5% of C2 (non-business)
- Eligible C3 = C2 − D1 − D2; add D1+D2 to output liability.
- Annual true-up [42(2)] on yearly E/F by 30 Nov; pay shortfall + interest, or reclaim excess.

Rule 43 (capital goods, spread over 60 months): Tm = A ÷ 60; Te = Tm × E/F reversed each month for 60 months. Exclusive exempt → nil; exclusive taxable → full. If exclusive later becomes common, A reduced 5% per quarter (or part) elapsed.

### Utilisation order — Sec 49, 49A, 49B, Rule 88A
1. IGST credit exhausted FIRST (49A): IGST → then CGST/SGST in any order & proportion (Rule 88A).
2. CGST credit → CGST then IGST. NEVER SGST.
3. SGST credit → SGST then IGST. NEVER CGST.
"IGST = universal donor; CGST & SGST never cross." Steer leftover IGST to head with bigger cash shortfall.
- Rule 86B: taxable turnover > ₹50 lakh/month → ≥1% of output tax liability in cash (exceptions: large income-tax paid, refund received, etc.).
- Rule 86A: officer blocks credit ledger on reason-to-believe fraud (temporary freeze, ≤1 year, not reversal).

### Reversal & interest
Reverse whenever taxable-business-use premise breaks. Interest [Sec 50(3), Rule 88B]: 18% p.a. only if ITC wrongly availed AND utilised (utilised = ledger balance falls below wrong amount); mere unused availment reversed → no interest.

### Sec 18 special-situation credit (Form ITC-01, within 30 days; 1-year invoice bar; CA/CMA cert if claim > ₹2 lakh)
| Sub-clause | Trigger | Credit on |
|---|---|---|
| 18(1)(a) | Becomes liable & registers ≤30 days | Inputs in stock only |
| 18(1)(b) | Voluntary registration | Inputs in stock only |
| 18(1)(c) | Composition → regular | Inputs in stock AND capital goods (CG −5%/qtr) |
| 18(1)(d) | Exempt → taxable | Inputs in stock AND capital goods |
| 18(4) | Regular → composition / output wholly exempt | REVERSE stock & CG |
| 18(3) | Sale/merger/transfer | Unutilised ITC transfers (Form ITC-02) |
| 18(6)+Rule 44 | Sale of used CG | Pay HIGHER of [ITC − 5%/qtr] or [tax on transaction value]; scrap moulds/dies/jigs/fixtures/refractory bricks → tax on value only |

Asymmetry: CG credit on transition-IN only for 18(1)(c)/(d), never (a)/(b). On way OUT, both stock + CG reversed.

### ISD (Sec 20) & Job-work (Sec 19)
ISD distributes common input-SERVICE credit to same-PAN branches by turnover (moving to mandatory; not goods/CG). Job-work: principal retains ITC if goods return in 1 yr (inputs)/3 yrs (CG); moulds/dies/jigs/fixtures/tools outside limit.

## Worked mini-example
Output: IGST 40,000, CGST 30,000, SGST 30,000. ITC: IGST 50,000, CGST 20,000, SGST 18,000.
- IGST credit 50,000: pays IGST 40,000, then 10,000 to CGST → CGST left 20,000.
- CGST credit 20,000 → CGST nil.
- SGST credit 18,000 vs SGST 30,000 → cash 12,000 (SGST).
Net cash = ₹12,000. CGST credit cannot rescue SGST shortfall.

Sale of used CG: ITC 1,80,000, used 3 yr 2 mo = 13 quarters. Residual = 1,80,000 × (100−65)% = 63,000; tax on value 4,00,000 × 18% = 72,000. Pay higher = ₹72,000.

## Exam traps & must-remember
- CGST never pays SGST (and vice-versa) — cash still due.
- IGST credit MUST be used before CGST/SGST (49A); leftover IGST split freely (88A) to save cash.
- Installation of P&M NOT blocked though building construction is; P&M excludes land/building/tower/outside pipeline.
- Motor-vehicle block only for passenger vehicles seating ≤13; trucks/buses>13/taxi/dealer/driving-school eligible.
- Depreciation bar hits tax component only, not asset base.
- 180-day rule: value+tax to supplier, proportionate on part-pay, not RCM/Schedule I.
- Sec 16(4) keyed to invoice's FY; debit-note credit runs on DN's own FY; re-availed credit no fresh bar.
- "Received" includes bill-to-ship-to; lots → last lot only.
- Exempt supply (17) includes RCM-out, land, completed building, securities; EXCLUDES exports — never park exports in exempt column of Rule 42.
- Blocked-credit exceptions are the exam: same-category inward=outward, obligatory-under-law.
- Goods lost/stolen/destroyed/gifted/free-samples → reverse 17(5)(h).
- CG transition-in credit only 18(1)(c)/(d).
- Interest on reversal only if availed AND utilised (18%, Rule 88B).
- Rule 86B forces ≥1% cash if monthly taxable T/O > ₹50 lakh even with ample ITC.
- GSTR-2B (frozen), not 2A, governs 16(2)(aa)/Rule 36(4).

## One-line recall
- Earn ITC via Sec 16 PRTR conditions within time; pay supplier ≤180 days or reverse.
- Sec 17: apportion for exempt/non-business (Rules 42/43); Sec 17(5) blocks end-consumption items.
- Rule 42: C2=T−(T1+T2+T3)−T4; D1=C2·E/F; D2=5%C2; annual true-up by 30 Nov.
- Utilise IGST-first & universal; CGST↔SGST wall absolute.
- Sale of used CG = higher of [ITC−5%/qtr] or [tax on value]; scrap moulds etc. → tax on value.
- Interest 18% only if wrong credit availed AND utilised.
