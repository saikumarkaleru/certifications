# Chapter 03 — Income under the Head "Salaries"

> Sections 15–17 of the Income-tax Act, 1961. Verify every rate/limit against current ICAI Study Material for your AY.

## Snapshot
- Head "Salaries" taxes the **total employment reward** flowing from an employer–employee (master–servant) relationship, in every form: cash, allowance, kind (perquisite), profits in lieu, deferred (retirement).
- Charge (Sec 15): **earlier of due or receipt**, taxed **once only** (proviso bars double tax).
- Definition (Sec 17(1)): **inclusive** ("includes") → nothing escapes by relabelling.
- Taxed near **gross receipts**, not profit → only Sec 16 deductions allowed.
- **Regime split (115BAC):** new regime = lower slabs but most exemptions/deductions withdrawn. **Fix the regime first.**

## Core concepts
- **Relationship, not label, is the trigger.** Same rupee sits in a different head depending on control/supervision.
- **Due-or-receipt (Sec 15):** blocks defer (taxed on due), advance (taxed on receipt), and double-count (proviso). "Due" = legally enforceable entitlement, not "morally earned."
- **Bonus** usually taxed on **receipt** (no due date till declared); **contractual commission** accrues on its fixed due date.
- **Sec 89(1):** re-spreads bunched arrears/advance to earlier years' rates — changes the *tax*, not the *head/year*. Often not available in new regime (verify).
- **Allowances:** "spare the cost, tax the surplus."
- **Retirement benefits:** exempt = **least of** (actual / statutory cap / formula) — two-jawed anti-abuse (never > actual, never > objective ceiling).
- **"Salary" means different bases** for HRA, RFA, gratuity, PF — read each.

## Key provisions / rules

### Boundary cases (correct head)
| Receipt | Head |
|---|---|
| Whole-time director remuneration | Salaries |
| Non-executive director sitting fee/commission | Other Sources |
| Partner's remuneration from firm | PGBP |
| MP / MLA salary & allowances | Other Sources |
| Family pension (widow/heir) | Other Sources (Sec 57 std deduction ⅓ or ₹15,000, lower) |

Employee's own pension = Salary; family pension = Other Sources.

### Allowances (Sec 10)
| Type | Rule |
|---|---|
| Fully taxable | DA, city compensatory, overtime, servant, warden, non-practising |
| **10(14)(i)** spend-to-save | Exempt = actual amount spent (capped at received); tour/travel, daily, conveyance, uniform, helper, research |
| **10(14)(ii)** flat caps | Children Education ₹100/child/month (max 2); Hostel ₹300/child/month (max 2); disabled transport; border/tribal |
| **HRA 10(13A)** | Least of three |
| Fully exempt | HC/SC judges, UN employees |

Trap: CEA *allowance* (₹100, cash) ≠ free-school *perquisite* (Rule 3, ₹1,000/month/child). Plain transport allowance now generally taxable (subsumed into std deduction), except higher exemption for blind/deaf-dumb/orthopaedically disabled.

### HRA (10(13A)) — exempt = LEAST of
1. Actual HRA received
2. Rent paid − 10% of salary
3. 50% of salary (metro) / 40% (non-metro)

Salary = Basic + DA (if enters retirement benefits) + commission as fixed % of turnover. **No rent → limb 2 = 0 → HRA fully taxable.** Metros = **Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai only** (Bengaluru/Hyderabad/Pune = 40%). Compute **period-wise** on any mid-year change. No HRA → **Sec 80GG** (least of ₹5,000/month, 25% of total income, or rent − 10% of total income); cannot claim both.

### Perquisites (Sec 17(2), Rule 3)
- **Specified employee** (Sec 17(2)(iii)) if ANY: (1) director; (2) >20% voting power; (3) monetary salary (all employers, after Sec 16, excl. non-monetary perks) **> ₹50,000**. Gates 1 & 2 have no floor.
- **Employer discharging employee's own liability (17(2)(iv))** — taxable to **ALL** (e.g., employee's income tax, own club bill, child's fee billed to employee). Facility employer contracts for = often specified-only.
- **Exempt perks:** telephone/mobile; employer PF/superannuation within limits; free refreshments; club/laptop use; medical within limits; goods at trade price; interest-free loan ≤ small ceiling or for specified medical treatment; LTC within 10(5).

| Perk | Rule 3 value |
|---|---|
| Rent-free/concessional accommodation | % of salary (govt vs non-govt, population slab) − rent recovered; if leased = **lower of** lease rent or %; furnished = **+10% p.a. of furniture cost** (or hire). Salary = Basic + DA(retirement) + bonus + commission + all taxable allowances + monetary payment; **excludes perks & employer PF** |
| Motor car | By owner / cost-bearer / engine cc (≤1.6L vs >1.6L). Wholly official + logbook = Nil |
| Interest-free/concessional loan | **SBI rate (1st day of FY) × max monthly outstanding − interest recovered.** Exempt if loan ≤ **₹20,000** or for specified-disease medical treatment |
| ESOP/sweat equity | **FMV on exercise − price paid** (FMV becomes cost for future CG) |

### Profits in lieu (Sec 17(3))
Terminal compensation, unrecognised PF (employer share + interest), keyman insurance, amounts before joining / after leaving. **Sec 10(10B)** retrenchment = least of (actual / ID Act amount / notified ceiling). **Sec 10(10C)** VRS exempt up to ceiling (commonly **₹5,00,000**); claiming it **bars Sec 89(1)** on same amount.

### Section 16 deductions
| Sec | Deduction |
|---|---|
| 16(ia) | Standard deduction — flat, one per aggregate salary (not per employer) |
| 16(ii) | Entertainment allowance — **govt only**: least of (actual / ₹5,000 / 20% of **basic**); added first, then deducted |
| 16(iii) | Professional tax — actually paid; employer-paid PT = added as perquisite then deducted (not a perfect wash) |

New regime: std deduction generally survives; 16(ii)/16(iii) generally not (verify).

### Retirement benefits
**Gratuity (10(10)):** Govt = fully exempt.
| | Act-covered | Non-Act |
|---|---|---|
| Formula limb | 15/26 × **last-drawn** salary × years (>6m rounds up) | ½ × **avg last 10 months** salary × completed years (ignore part-year) |
| "Salary" | Basic + **full DA** | Basic + DA-in-retirement + turnover commission |
Also least of: actual; statutory ceiling (**₹20,00,000**, shared across lifetime).

**Pension (10(10A)):** Uncommuted = fully taxable. Commuted: Govt = full exempt; others = **⅓** of full value (if gratuity also received) or **½** (if not). Gross up partial commutation (full value = amount ÷ % commuted) first.

**Leave encashment (10(10AA)):** During service = fully taxable. Govt retiree = fully exempt. Others = least of: actual / notified ceiling / 10-month avg salary / cash equiv (**max 30 days per completed year** × avg 10-month salary). Ceiling is lifetime aggregate.

**Provident Fund:**
| Fund | Employer contrib | Interest | Lump sum |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPF | Exempt | Exempt | Fully exempt |
| RPF | Exempt ≤ **12%** of salary (excess taxable) | Exempt ≤ notified rate | Exempt if **≥5 yrs** service |
| URPF | Not taxed yearly | Not taxed yearly | 4-bucket split |
| PPF | Self | Exempt | Fully exempt |

Modern aggregate caps: employer's RPF+NPS+superannuation > **₹7,50,000/yr** = taxable perquisite (17(2)(vii)); accretion on excess taxable (17(2)(viia)). Interest on employee's **own** RPF contribution > **₹2,50,000/yr** (₹5,00,000 if no employer contribution) = taxable. Employee's own RPF contribution → **Sec 80C**. URPF payout split: own contribution (nil) / interest on own (Other Sources) / employer share + its interest (Salary, profit in lieu).

## Worked mini-example
Ms A, Mumbai (metro). Basic ₹40,000/m, DA ₹10,000/m (enters retirement), HRA ₹18,000/m, rent ₹22,000/m.
- Salary for HRA = (40,000 + 10,000) × 12 = ₹6,00,000
- HRA received = ₹2,16,000; Rent = ₹2,64,000
- Limb 1 (actual) = ₹2,16,000
- Limb 2 (rent − 10% salary) = 2,64,000 − 60,000 = ₹2,04,000
- Limb 3 (50% metro) = ₹3,00,000
- Exempt = least = **₹2,04,000** → **Taxable HRA = 2,16,000 − 2,04,000 = ₹12,000**

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **HRA, no rent → exemption NIL** (limb 2 = 0). The classic trap.
2. **"Salary" is not one number** — HRA vs Act-gratuity (last-drawn + full DA) vs non-Act gratuity (10-month avg) vs RFA — all different bases.
3. **DA "enters retirement benefits" or not** — decides inclusion in HRA/PF/gratuity base. Problem always states it.
4. **Advance salary taxed on receipt; loan against salary not.** Advance already taxed ≠ re-taxed on due (proviso). Advance ≠ arrears.
5. **Uncommuted pension fully taxable**; only commuted gets ⅓/½. Gross up partial commutation.
6. **Gratuity part-year:** Act rounds >6 months up; non-Act ignores part-year.
7. **Employee's own PF = 80C, not a salary deduction.** Employer's excess (>12% or >₹7.5L) IS added.
8. **Entertainment allowance = govt only**, added first then capped (least of ₹5,000 / 20% basic / actual). Private = nil.
9. **Employer-paid professional tax:** add as perquisite, then deduct 16(iii); deduct only on payment.
10. **Specified employee** — car/servant perks taxable if director / >20% shareholder / monetary salary > ₹50,000.
11. **Regime mismatch** — don't apply old-regime HRA under new regime.
12. **Family pension = Other Sources (Sec 57)**, not Salary/Sec 16.
13. **"Tax-free" salary must be grossed up** — employer-borne tax is itself a perquisite.
14. **Period-wise HRA** on any mid-year change; don't annualise.
15. **RFA "salary" excludes perks & employer PF** but includes taxable allowances — a third distinct base.
16. **Foregone/surrendered salary still taxable** (unless surrendered to Central Govt under the specific Act).
17. **80CCD(2)** (employer NPS) survives under new regime; **89(1)** relief generally does not on certain items.

## One-line recall
- Sec 15: earlier of due or receipt, once only; Sec 89(1) softens rate, not year.
- HRA = least of (actual / rent − 10% salary / 50% metro–40% non-metro); no rent = nil.
- Metros for HRA = Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai only.
- Specified employee gate 3 = monetary salary > ₹50,000; gates 1 & 2 (director / >20%) have no floor.
- Gratuity ceiling ₹20,00,000; VRS ₹5,00,000; RPF employer exempt ≤12%; aggregate cap ₹7.5L; own-interest cap ₹2.5L.
- Commuted pension: ⅓ if gratuity received, ½ if not; uncommuted fully taxable.
