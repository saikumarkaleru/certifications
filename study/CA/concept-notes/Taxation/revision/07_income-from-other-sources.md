# Chapter 07 — Income from Other Sources (IFOS)

> Verify every rate, limit and threshold against the current ICAI module for your AY. Logic is durable; numbers drift.

## Snapshot

IFOS is the **residual, catch-all head** (Sec 14 splits income into 5 heads; IFOS is the 5th). If a receipt is **income (or deemed income)**, is **not exempt**, and fits **none of the other four heads** (Salaries, House Property, PGBP, Capital Gains), it is taxed here. Two kinds of "orphan" land here: (a) **genuine income with no natural head** (interest, dividend, family pension, sub-letting) and (b) **capital-type windfalls deemed to be income** (gifts, excess share premium). The more artificial the inclusion, the stingier the deductions.

## Core concepts

- **Sec 56(1)** — general residual charge: "income of every kind" not excluded and not under heads 1–4.
- **Sec 56(2)** — illustrative (not exhaustive) list of named inclusions; serves 3 purposes: (a) certainty for common items, (b) deem capital-type receipts to be income, (c) plug avoidance.
- **Precondition:** must be income or deemed income first. A pure capital receipt (not income by nature, not deemed) is NOT dragged into 56(1) — that is why gifts needed a separate deeming clause 56(2)(x).
- **Head of last resort:** reach IFOS only after ruling out the other 4 heads; the same item (interest, letting, royalty) becomes PGBP the moment it is part of a business.

**Three buckets of 56(2) clauses**

| Bucket | Purpose | Clauses | Deduction regime |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certainty | Name common real income | Dividend 56(2)(i); interest on securities 56(2)(id); letting 56(2)(ii)/(iii) | Normal Sec 57 costs |
| Deeming | Turn capital receipt into income | Gifts 56(2)(x); interest on compensation 56(2)(viii) | Restricted/proxy |
| Anti-abuse | Plug avoidance | Angel tax 56(2)(viib); deemed dividend 2(22)(e) | Little/none |

## Key provisions / rules

### Named 56(2) inclusions

| Clause | Item |
|---|---|
| 56(2)(i) | Dividends |
| 56(2)(id) | Interest on securities |
| 56(2)(ib) | Winnings — lotteries, crossword, races (incl. horse), card games, gambling, betting, TV game shows |
| 56(2)(x) | Money/property without or for inadequate consideration (gifts) |
| 56(2)(viib) | Share consideration above FMV (angel tax) |
| 56(2)(ii)/(iii) | Letting of machinery, plant, furniture; + building let inseparably with such assets |
| 56(2)(viii) | Interest on compensation / enhanced compensation |

### Default residual items (IFOS unless business)
Family pension, interest income (bank/FD), director's **sitting fees** (non-executive), income from **sub-letting**, casual income, royalty (if not business).

- **Sub-letting:** owner letting = House Property; **tenant** sub-letting (no ownership) = IFOS. Ownership is the switch.
- **Director:** whole-time/MD → employee → **Salary**; non-executive sitting fees → **IFOS**.

### Dividends — 56(2)(i)
DDT abolished w.e.f. FY 2020-21; now **classical taxation → taxable in shareholder's hands at slab rates**.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deduction (57) | Only **interest expense** to earn dividend, **capped at 20% of dividend**. No other expense (collection, PMS, demat). |
| TDS | On dividend above threshold (commonly ₹5,000/yr) — verify. |
| Timing | Final dividend: taxable when **declared** in general meeting. Interim: when **unconditionally made available** (payment). |

**Deemed dividend 2(22):**
- **2(22)(a):** distribution of accumulated profits releasing company assets.
- **2(22)(e):** loan/advance by a **closely-held** company to a shareholder with **≥10% voting power** (or a concern he has substantial interest in), deemed dividend **only up to accumulated profits**. Money-lending business carved out. Taxed in recipient shareholder's hands.

### Interest on securities — 56(2)(id)
IFOS unless securities held as **stock-in-trade** (then PGBP). **Gross up** when received net of TDS: **Gross = Net ÷ (1 − TDS rate)**. If given the coupon (e.g., 8% on ₹1,00,000 = ₹8,000), that IS gross — no grossing up. Fixed-due-date securities taxed on **due date** whether or not received.

### Casual income — 56(2)(ib) + 115BB

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Rate | Flat **30%** (+ surcharge + cess), NOT slab (Sec 115BB) |
| Deductions | **Nil** — Sec 58(4), no expense (ticket, travel) |
| Basic exemption / Ch VI-A | Not available |
| Set-off | No loss set off against it; its losses not set off either |
| TDS | 194B / 194BB above threshold (e.g., ₹10,000; recent law aggregates over year) |

- **Winnings in kind** (car, gold): payer releases prize only after tax on **FMV** is paid; if cash part < TDS, winner deposits the **shortfall**. Taxable income = **FMV** (do not net TDS off).
- **Owning & maintaining race horses** = quasi-business: **expenses allowed**, loss carried forward up to **4 years**, set off only against same activity. Distinguish from **betting on races** (pure 115BB, nothing allowed).
- **Online games:** own regime — flat rate on **net winnings**, own TDS. Verify section; do not conflate with 194B.

### Gifts — 56(2)(x)
Applies to **any person**. Three categories, each with its own ₹50,000 basket:

| Category | Without consideration | Inadequate consideration |
|---|---|---|
| **Money** | Aggregate **> ₹50,000** → **whole** sum taxable | — |
| **Immovable property** | SDV **> ₹50,000** → whole SDV | (SDV − consideration) **> higher of ₹50,000 or 10% of consideration** → taxable diff |
| **Movable property** | Aggregate FMV **> ₹50,000** → whole FMV | (FMV − consideration) **> ₹50,000** (flat, no 10% band) → taxable diff |

- **Three separate ₹50,000 baskets** — apply category-wise, never a grand total.
- Movable "property" = **defined list**: shares/securities, jewellery, bullion, art, drawings, paintings, sculptures, archaeological collections. **A car is NOT listed** → gifting a car is outside 56(2)(x).
- Immovable: SDV on **date of agreement** allowed (vs registration) if part consideration paid by non-cash mode on/before agreement date.

**Exemptions (not taxed if received):**
1. From a **relative** (narrowly defined — see below).
2. On occasion of the **individual's own marriage** (not child's/sibling's).
3. Under a **will / by inheritance**.
4. In **contemplation of death** of payer.
5. From local authority / specified fund/trust / registered charitable trust, etc.
- Employer receipts excluded from 56(2)(x) → taxed as **Salary**.

**"Relative" (individual), read from receiver outward:** spouse; brother/sister of self and of spouse; **brother/sister of either parent** (uncles/aunts both sides); any **lineal ascendant/descendant** of self or spouse; and the **spouse of each** of these.
- **Uncle → nephew = exempt; nephew → uncle = taxable** (nephew is not the uncle's relative). Test from receiver's viewpoint.
- **HUF recipient:** "relative" = any **member** of the HUF.

**Angel tax 56(2)(viib):** closely-held company issues shares to a resident **above FMV** → excess taxed as **company's** IFOS. Recognised start-ups (prescribed conditions) exempt.

**Cost on later sale (49(4)):** value charged under 56(2)(x) becomes the **cost of acquisition** for capital gains (no double tax). Inherited/relative-gifted (exempt) assets carry previous owner's **cost and holding period** (49(1)/2(42A)).

### Deductions — Section 57

| Clause | Allowed |
|---|---|
| 57(i)/(ia) | Dividend & interest-on-securities: collection charges + interest on borrowing (dividend interest **capped at 20%**) |
| 57(ii)/(iia) | Family pension: std deduction = **lower of 1/3 of pension or ₹15,000** |
| 57(iii) | Any other expense **wholly & exclusively to earn** income (**purpose, not result**) |
| 57(iv) | Interest on compensation/enhanced comp: flat **50%**, no other expense |
| — | Depreciation / current repairs on let-out machinery, plant, furniture, building |

- **57(iii) purpose test:** deductible even if no income actually arose (e.g., interest on share loan in a no-dividend year), subject to caps.
- Family pension: cap of ₹15,000 bites once pension > ₹45,000 (1/3 × 45,000 = 15,000).

### Disallowances — Section 58
- Personal expenses.
- Interest/salary payable **outside India without TDS** (58(1)(a)).
- Wealth tax / income tax.
- **58(1A):** 40A disallowances (excessive related-party 40A(2); cash payments over limit 40A(3)) apply to IFOS too.
- **58(4): nothing deductible against casual income** (exception: race-horse activity expenses).

### Method of accounting — Section 145
Regular **cash or mercantile** basis, applied consistently (145(1)). **Forced receipt basis:** dividend (declared/paid) and interest on compensation/enhanced compensation (**145B**) — because the right is contingent until an outside event (declaration / final court award).

## Worked mini-example

**Gift computation (56(2)(x)), individual Mr. C:**
- ₹40,000 cash from friend → money basket ₹40,000 **≤ ₹50,000 → nil**.
- ₹30,000 from father's brother (uncle) → **relative → exempt**.
- ₹1,00,000 employer festival bonus → **Salary, not IFOS**.
- ₹3,00,000 on **own marriage** → exempt.
- Land SDV ₹6,00,000 from **brother** → relative → exempt.
- Land bought ₹18,00,000, SDV ₹20,00,000: diff ₹2,00,000 vs higher of ₹50,000 or 10%×18,00,000 = ₹1,80,000 → 2,00,000 > 1,80,000 → **₹2,00,000 taxable**.
- Shares FMV ₹2,00,000 free from friend → movable, > ₹50,000 → **₹2,00,000 taxable**.
- Jewellery FMV ₹1,50,000 bought ₹1,10,000: diff ₹40,000 ≤ ₹50,000 (no 10% band) → **nil**.
- Painting inherited under will → exempt.
- Car FMV ₹5,00,000 from friend → **not in listed property → nil**.

**Total taxable IFOS from gifts = ₹2,00,000 + ₹2,00,000 = ₹4,00,000.**

*Tweaks:* SDV ₹19,50,000 → diff ₹1,50,000 < ₹1,80,000 → land nil. Friend cash ₹60,000 → whole ₹60,000 taxable (cliff, not step).

**Composite IFOS (Mrs. D):** Family pension ₹1,80,000 − std deduction (lower of 1/3 = ₹60,000 or ₹15,000) = ₹15,000 → **₹1,65,000**. Let-out machinery+building ₹3,00,000 − dep ₹40,000 − repairs ₹20,000 = **₹2,40,000**. Interest on enhanced compensation ₹2,00,000 (receipt basis) − 50% = **₹1,00,000**. **IFOS = ₹5,05,000.** If husband died in armed-forces operational service → family pension fully exempt u/s 10 → IFOS = **₹3,40,000**.

## Exam traps & must-remember

1. **₹50,000 is a threshold, not an exemption** — cross it on money gifts and the **whole** amount is taxable (₹51,000 → ₹51,000).
2. **Immovable vs movable test differs:** immovable = higher of ₹50,000 or **10%** of consideration; movable = flat ₹50,000, **no 10% band**.
3. **"Relative" is narrow and asymmetric** — read from receiver outward. Uncle→nephew exempt; nephew→uncle taxable. Cousin/friend not relatives.
4. **Own marriage only** — child's/sibling's marriage gift not exempt.
5. **Employer gifts = Salary**, expressly excluded from 56(2)(x).
6. **Car & unlisted movables outside 56(2)(x)** — only the defined list counts.
7. **Casual income:** gross it, flat 30%, deduct nothing, set off nothing, no basic exemption. TDS ≠ final tax when surcharge applies.
8. **Gross up** interest/winnings received net of TDS.
9. **Dividend interest cap 20%** — excess actually paid is lost; only interest allowed (not demat/PMS/collection).
10. **Family pension std deduction = LOWER of 1/3 or ₹15,000** (not higher).
11. **Interest on enhanced compensation:** receipt basis (145B) + flat 50% (57(iv)), no other expense.
12. **Three separate ₹50,000 baskets** — never merge into one total.
13. **Only interest deductible against dividend** (contrast interest income where collection charges ARE allowed).
14. **Deemed dividend 2(22)(e) capped at accumulated profits**, only if closely-held co. and borrower ≥10% voting power.
15. **Race-horse ownership** (quasi-business, expenses + 4-yr carry-forward) vs **betting** (pure 115BB, nothing).
16. **War-widow / gallantry family pension** may be **fully exempt** u/s 10 — do not mechanically apply ₹15,000 deduction.
17. **57(iii) purpose not result** — deductible even if no income arose.
18. **Online-gaming winnings** in own section on net winnings — do not force into 194B/115BB.
19. **Clubbing overlap:** husband gifts wife cash (exempt as relative) but interest she earns is **clubbed** in his hands as his IFOS (Sec 64).

## One-line recall

- IFOS = residual net (56(1)); catches only **income or deemed income** not covered by the other 4 heads.
- **Money gift > ₹50,000 → whole sum taxable**; three separate baskets; immovable gets a 10% cushion, movable none.
- Casual/lottery = **flat 30% (115BB)**, zero deductions, zero set-off, zero basic exemption; winnings in kind taxed on FMV.
- Dividend = slab, interest deduction **capped at 20%**; deemed dividend 2(22)(e) capped at accumulated profits.
- Pension → take the **LOWER** (1/3 or ₹15,000); compensation interest → take **HALF** (50%, receipt basis 145B).
- Sec 57 = costs wholly & exclusively to earn (purpose not result); Sec 58 = nothing against casual income + 40A discipline (58(1A)); Sec 49(4) bridges gifted-asset cost into capital gains.
