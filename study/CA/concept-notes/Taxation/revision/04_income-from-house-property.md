# Chapter 04 — Income from House Property

## Snapshot
Head taxes the **inherent earning capacity (Annual Value)** of buildings owned by the assessee and NOT used in his own business/profession — not merely cash rent received. Softened by NIL value for self-occupied homes and a flat 30% deduction. Charging Sec 22; computation Secs 23–27; only two deductions (Sec 24). Verify all rates/limits against current ICAI material for your AY.

## Core concepts
- **Charge = Annual Value** (notional), benchmarked against market rent — so a below-market "sweetheart" rent is still taxed on the higher Expected Rent.
- **B-O-N gate (Sec 22):** all three must hold — **B**uilding (or land appurtenant), **O**wner, **N**ot used for own business/profession whose profits are taxable. Fail one → income leaves this head.
- Rent from **bare land / open plot / parking / swimming pool** (no building) → **Income from Other Sources**. Building includes shops, offices, godowns, factory sheds, cinema halls, auditoriums.
- Ownership of the **building** triggers charge even if land belongs to another. Foreign property rent taxable for a **Resident & Ordinarily Resident** (ROR); non-resident only Indian-situated property.
- **Two regimes:** Self-Occupied (SOP, AV = NIL) vs Let-Out (LOP, AV = GAV). Extra houses beyond limit = **Deemed Let Out (DLO)**.
- Computation ladder: **GAV → (− municipal tax paid) → NAV → (− 30% of NAV) → (− interest 24b) → Income/Loss.**

## Key provisions / rules

### Sections at a glance
| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| 22 | Charging section — AV of owned buildings not used in own business |
| 23(1) | Compute GAV (ER vs AR, vacancy) |
| 23(2) | Self-occupied → AV NIL; 23(2)(b): home vacant due to employment elsewhere still NIL |
| 23(4) | More than two houses → extras Deemed Let Out |
| 23(5) | Stock-in-trade flats — AV NIL for a specified period from end of FY of completion certificate |
| 24(a) | Standard deduction 30% of NAV |
| 24(b) | Interest on borrowed capital (incl. pre-construction in 5 instalments) |
| 25A | Unrealised rent / arrears recovered — taxable, 30% deduction |
| 26 | Co-owners with definite shares taxed separately |
| 27 | Deemed owner (substance over paper title) |

### Limits & rules (verify for your AY)
| Item | Rule / Limit |
|---|---|
| Standard deduction 24(a) | 30% of NAV (flat, regardless of actual spend) |
| Interest 24(b) — let-out | No ceiling (full interest); loss possible |
| Interest 24(b) — SOP, acquisition/construction loan (completed in prescribed period) | Ceiling ₹2,00,000 (aggregate across ≤2 SOP houses) |
| Interest 24(b) — SOP, other cases (e.g. repair loan / conditions not met) | Ceiling ₹30,000 |
| Pre-construction interest | Accumulate from date of borrowing to 31 March before completion; deduct in 5 equal annual instalments from completion year |
| Municipal taxes | Deductible only if actually PAID by owner in the year (year of payment governs; tenant-paid not deductible) |
| No. of self-occupied houses | Up to 2 (individual/HUF); extras = DLO; assessee chooses which |
| HP loss set-off | Against other heads up to ₹2,00,000 per year |
| Carry forward of HP loss | 8 assessment years (vs HP income only thereafter) |
| Principal repayment | Sec 80C (Chapter VI-A), NOT Sec 24 |
| Unrealised rent exclusion | Only if Rule 4 conditions met |

### Deemed owners (Sec 27)
Transfer to **spouse** (not for adequate consideration / not under agreement to live apart) or to **minor child** (not a married daughter); holder of an **impartible estate**; member of **co-op society/company/AOP** allotted a building; buyer under **power of attorney / Sec 53A part-performance**; lessee holding a **lease ≥ 12 years** (excludes month-to-month or ≤1-year leases).
**Escapes deeming:** married daughter; transfer to spouse under agreement to live apart; transfer for adequate consideration.

### GAV logic (Sec 23)
- **ER (Expected Rent)** = higher of (Municipal Value, Fair Rent), **capped at Standard Rent** (if Rent Control applies).
- **AR** = (rent p.m. × months let) − unrealised rent (if Rule 4 met).
- **No vacancy** → GAV = higher of (ER, AR).
- **Vacancy that pulled AR below ER** → GAV = vacancy-adjusted actual rent (not taxed on genuinely empty months).
- MV/FR/SR given for full year are NOT pro-rated for vacancy; only actual rent is time-apportioned.
- Single house part-let/part-self-occupied over the year → treated as **let-out for whole year**; no SOP NIL benefit for residence months.

### Rule 4 conditions for excluding unrealised rent (all required)
1. Tenancy bona fide; 2. Defaulting tenant vacated / steps taken to make him vacate; 3. Tenant not occupying any other property of assessee; 4. All reasonable steps taken for legal recovery (or AO satisfied proceedings useless).

### Interest 24(b) finer points
Interest allowed on **accrual (payable)** basis. Interest on a fresh loan to repay the original acquisition/construction loan IS deductible. NOT deductible: "interest on interest" (penal), brokerage/processing commission, interest where borrowing diverted to non-property use.

### Pre-construction interest
Period = date of borrowing to 31 March immediately preceding year of completion/acquisition (or loan repayment date if earlier). Total ÷ 5 = equal instalments from the completion year onward. Completion-year's own interest is **current**, never pre-construction. For SOP, current + pre-con instalment together fit inside the ₹2,00,000 / ₹30,000 ceiling; for LOP both flow in fully.

### Special situations
| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Unrealised rent recovered later (25A) | Taxable in year of recovery, even if property sold; 30% deduction allowed |
| Arrears of rent (25A) | Taxable on receipt; 30% deduction |
| Co-owners with definite shares (26) | Each taxed on share; each gets own SOP benefit & ceiling; indefinite shares → assessed as AOP |
| Composite rent — separable | Building → HP; amenities/plant → Other Sources/Business |
| Composite rent — inseparable | Whole → Business/Other Sources; building never enters HP |
| Part let / part self-occupied (physically divided) | Compute each portion separately; apportion MV/FR/SR/taxes/interest by area |
| Builder's stock-in-trade flats (23(5)) | AV NIL for specified period from end of FY of completion certificate |

## Worked mini-example
Let-out house, whole year at ₹25,000/month. MV ₹2,40,000; FR ₹2,80,000; no Rent Control. Municipal taxes ₹30,000 paid by owner. Loan interest ₹1,50,000.

- ER = higher of (₹2,40,000, ₹2,80,000) = ₹2,80,000 (no SR cap).
- AR = ₹25,000 × 12 = ₹3,00,000; no vacancy.
- GAV = higher of (₹2,80,000, ₹3,00,000) = ₹3,00,000.

| Particulars | ₹ |
|---|---:|
| Gross Annual Value | 3,00,000 |
| Less: Municipal taxes paid | (30,000) |
| Net Annual Value | 2,70,000 |
| Less: Standard deduction @ 30% of NAV [24(a)] | (81,000) |
| Less: Interest [24(b)] | (1,50,000) |
| **Income from House Property** | **39,000** |

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **Municipal tax = "paid," interest = "payable."** Only tax actually paid by owner deducts (tenant-paid or merely-due gets nothing; arrears paid this year for an earlier year ARE deductible). Reversing the paid/payable basis is the commonest slip.
2. **Standard deduction is 30% of NAV** — not GAV, not rent. It swallows insurance, ground rent, collection charges, land revenue, repairs, painting — these are distractors, never deduct again.
3. **Bare land / open parking / pool rent → Other Sources**, not HP (no building).
4. **Vacancy vs unrealised rent differ:** unrealised rent reduces AR *before* the ER-vs-AR comparison (Rule 4); genuine vacancy pulls GAV down *after* it.
5. **SOP:** 30% deduction and municipal taxes give NIL benefit (NAV already nil). Interest ceiling (₹2,00,000 / ₹30,000) is **aggregate across up to two SOP houses**, and excess over ceiling is **lost, not carried forward**.
6. **Pre-construction interest** starts from the completion year (not borrowing year); cut-off is 31 March *before* completion; each instalment = 1/5 of the accumulated pre-con interest.
7. **80C (principal) ≠ 24(b) (interest).** Brokerage/processing fee is neither.
8. **DLO (3rd+ house)** taxed on Expected Rent though earning nothing; assessee chooses which up-to-two are SOP — shield the highest ERs.
9. **Married daughter / agreement to live apart / adequate consideration** each defeat Sec 27 deeming.
10. **Co-owners:** definite shares → assessed separately (two ceilings); indefinite → AOP (concessions lost).
11. **Unrealised/arrears recovered later** taxable on receipt with 30% deduction, even if property since sold.
12. **Foreign property of an ROR** is taxable here — don't drop the "London flat."
13. **Sec 115BAC (default/concessional regime):** SOP interest deduction and set-off of HP loss against other heads are curtailed — verify for your AY.

## One-line recall
- **B-O-N** — Building, Owner, Not-own-business (the three Sec 22 gates).
- **"24 = a + b = 30% + interest"** — the only two deductions; 30% is of NAV.
- **"Tax paid, interest payable"** — municipal tax on payment, interest on accrual.
- **"Pre-con = 5 slices from completion year."**
- **"NIL roof, capped interest, excess lost"** — the self-occupied regime (up to 2 houses, ₹2,00,000 / ₹30,000 ceilings).
- **"Higher for cheap rent, lower for genuine vacancy"** — the two GAV branches; HP loss set-off capped at ₹2,00,000, carried forward 8 years.
