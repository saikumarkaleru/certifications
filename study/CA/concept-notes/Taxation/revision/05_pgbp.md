# Chapter 05 — Profits & Gains of Business or Profession (PGBP)

> Rates/limits are anchors — confirm exact figures and the applicable Assessment Year in current ICAI material. The source gives no fixed AY.

## Snapshot
- Business income is a **net** concept: taxable profit = revenue − revenue expenses wholly & exclusively incurred, computed on the assessee's regular accounting method (Sec 145), subject to statutory overrides.
- Core exam skill: **start from Net Profit as per P&L → add back disallowed/wrongly-reduced items + Sec 41 deemed incomes → deduct income of other heads, exempt income, allowable-but-omitted expenses, and Sec 32 depreciation.**
- Direction test on every item: did it make book profit too low (add back) or too high (deduct)?
- Governing sections: 28–44DB. Rulebook: Sec 29 → compute per Sec 30–43D.

## Core concepts
- **Sec 28 (charging):** taxes anything arising from a business/profession — profits, contract-termination compensation, export incentives, benefit/perquisite (in cash or kind), Keyman insurance sums, partner's interest/salary/remuneration from firm (to extent allowed to firm), **non-compete fees**, Sec 35AD asset recoveries. Threshold questions: is there a business? revenue vs capital receipt? did income arise during business or after cessation (→ Sec 176(3A)/41)?
- **Speculation business [Expl 2 to Sec 28]:** transactions settled without delivery = distinct/separate business; **speculation loss set off only against speculation profit, c/f 4 years.** Recognised-exchange derivatives/hedging carved out.
- **Specific overrides general:** if an item is dealt with by Sec 30–36 (even if restricted/disallowed there), you cannot rescue it via Sec 37.
- **Book profit ≠ taxable profit** — reconcile, don't rebuild.

### Sec 36 key limbs
- (i)/(ib) insurance of stock, employee health (non-cash) / cattle.
- (ii) bonus/commission (not disguised dividend; also 43B).
- (iii) **interest on borrowed capital**; interest to acquire an asset until first put to use must be **capitalised**.
- (iv)/(iva)/(v) employer contribution to RPF/superannuation/NPS/gratuity fund.
- **(va) employee's PF/ESI:** deductible only if credited to fund by **due date under the PF/ESI Act** (not ITR date). Late = **permanently disallowed** (Checkmate Services).
- (vii) **bad debts actually written off** (must have been in income earlier); provision not allowed.
- (viia) provision for bad debts — banks/FIs only.

### Sec 37(1) locks
Not covered by 30–36; not capital; not personal; wholly & exclusively for business; **not for an offence/prohibited by law** (Expl 1; Expl 3 covers foreign-law offences and law-violating freebies); **CSR (Companies Act §135) deemed not for business** (Expl 2 — no Sec 37 deduction; 80G may apply). Penalty (punitive) disallowed; compensatory/liquidated damages allowed.

### Disallowances (40, 40A, 43B)
| Provision | Rule |
|---|---|
| 40(a)(i) | TDS default on non-resident payment → **100%** disallowed |
| 40(a)(ia) | TDS default on specified resident payment → **30%** disallowed (restored when TDS paid) |
| 40(a)(ii) | Income-tax (+ cess/surcharge) not deductible |
| 40(a)(iii) | Salary paid outside India / to NR without TDS → disallowed |
| 40(a)(iib) | Certain exclusive state levies on state PSU → disallowed |
| 40(b) | Partner interest ≤ **12% p.a. simple**; remuneration within book-profit slab, working partners only, deed-authorised & quantified. Mirror: amount allowed to firm = partner's Sec 28 income |
| 40(ba) | AOP/BOI payments to members disallowed |
| 40A(2) | Excessive related-party payment → **only the excess over FMV** disallowed |
| 40A(3)/(3A) | Cash payment > **₹10,000/person/day** (**₹35,000 transporters**), non-banking mode → **entire** payment disallowed; 40A(3A) reverses earlier-accrued liability later paid in cash. Exceptions: Rule 6DD |
| 40A(7)/(9) | Unpaid/unapproved gratuity provision; unapproved welfare fund → disallowed |

**40(b) remuneration slab (verify current):** first ₹3,00,000 of book profit (or loss): ₹1,50,000 or 90%, whichever higher; balance: 60%. Book profit = PGBP before partner remuneration but after 40(b) interest.

**Sec 43B — "actually paid" (overrides accrual):** tax/duty/cess/fee (a); employer PF/ESI/superannuation/gratuity (b); bonus/commission (c); bank/FI/NBFC interest (d)/(da); leave encashment (f); Indian Railways (g); **payment to micro/small enterprise beyond MSMED time limit (h)**. Safety valve: allowed in accrual year if paid by **Sec 139(1) return due date** — **except 43B(h)** which has **no grace** (pay within 15 days / 45 days with written agreement). Converting interest into a fresh loan is **not** payment.

### Depreciation (Sec 32 / block)
- Conditions: ownership (incl. beneficial/hire-purchaser/lessee-builder per Expl 1), used for business, in a block with prescribed rate, block subsists with positive WDV.
- **Block [Sec 2(11)]:** same-class same-rate assets pooled. Additions added, sale money deducted; ordinary sale triggers no capital gain while block survives.
- Computation: Opening WDV + cost acquired − money on assets sold = WDV before dep × rate = dep; balance = closing WDV.
- **Half-depreciation:** asset **acquired AND put to use < 180 days** → 50% of rate (year of acquisition only). Bought but not used = **nil** depreciation.
- **Max rate 40%** (buildings 10%, furniture 10%, general P&M 15%; verify schedule).
- **Additional depreciation [32(1)(iia)]:** extra **20%** (10% if <180 days; balance 10% next year) on new P&M by manufacturer/production (and certain power). Not on ships/aircraft, office appliances, second-hand, road transport vehicles, or fully-deducted machinery.
- **Power sector SLM [32(1)(i)]:** may opt SLM per asset → terminal depreciation/balancing charge.
- **Sec 50 (deemed STCG):** (a) block ceases (all sold) → STCG/STCL; (b) sale money > opening WDV + additions → excess is STCG, WDV nil. Always add **all** additions before testing negativity.
- **Unabsorbed depreciation [32(2)]:** c/f **indefinitely**, set off against any head **except salary**, no timely-filing / business-continuation condition.
- **Set-off order:** current depreciation → b/f business loss (8-yr limit) → b/f unabsorbed depreciation (no limit).
- Sec 43(1) actual cost = cost less subsidy/contribution (Explanations adjust for personal-use assets, sale-and-leaseback, inflated second-hand cost).

### Presumptive (44AD/ADA/AE)
| Section | Who | Presumed income | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 44AD | Resident ind/HUF/firm (not LLP), eligible business | **8%** (cash) / **6%** (digital, by due date) | Turnover ≤ ₹2 cr (₹3 cr if cash ≤ 5%) |
| 44ADA | Resident ind/firm (not LLP), specified profession | **50%** of gross receipts | Receipts ≤ ₹50 L (₹75 L if cash ≤ 5%) |
| 44AE | Owns ≤ **10** goods carriages | Heavy (>12,000 kg): **₹1,000/tonne GVW/month**; other: **₹7,500/vehicle/month** | ≤10 vehicles at any time |

- All Sec 30–38 deductions deemed allowed; **WDV notionally reduced** as if depreciation claimed.
- 44AE: part-month = full month; hire-purchase counts; 11th vehicle even briefly ousts scheme.
- No further 40(b) partner salary/interest deduction (withdrawn FA 2016; verify).
- **44AD 5-year lock-in:** opt out within 5 years → barred 5 AYs + books/audit if income > basic exemption. **44ADA has no lock-in.**
- 44AD advance tax: single instalment by **15 March**.
- Declare lower than presumed only with books + 44AB audit.

### Audit / books & Sec 41
- **44AA books:** specified professions if receipts > ~₹1,50,000 (all 3 preceding yrs); others if income > ~₹1,20,000 **or** turnover > ~₹10,00,000 (verify).
- **44AB tax audit:** business turnover > **₹1 cr** (**₹10 cr** if cash receipts & payments ≤ 5%); profession > **₹50 L**; or presumptive assessee declaring lower & exceeding exemption.
- **Sec 41 clawback (deemed business income, even if business defunct):** 41(1) remission/cessation of trading liability (unilateral write-back counts); 41(2) balancing charge (SLM assets); 41(3) sale of Sec 35 research asset; 41(4) recovery of bad debt (only amount recovered); 41(5) loss of discontinued business set off against 41 income.
- Non-resident presumptive: 44B (shipping 7.5%), 44BB (oil services 10%), 44BBA (aircraft 5%), 44BBB (turnkey power).
- Sec 145A: GST-inclusive (inclusive method) inventory valuation.

## Worked mini-example
Firm's Net Profit per P&L ₹12,50,000. Adjust: (1) machinery ₹2,00,000 wrongly charged as repairs → add back; (2) cash payment ₹45,000 (>₹10,000) → **fully** disallowed ₹45,000; (3) GST ₹30,000 unpaid by due date → add ₹30,000 (₹80,000 paid = allowed); (4) bank interest ₹90,000 unpaid → add ₹90,000 [43B]; (5) excess partner salary ₹1,00,000 → add; (6) commission ₹50,000 no TDS → **30%** = ₹15,000 add; (7) unapproved gratuity provision ₹70,000 → add [40A(7)]; (8) allowable dep ₹45,000 more than book → less ₹45,000.

Total add-backs = 2,00,000 + 45,000 + 30,000 + 90,000 + 1,00,000 + 15,000 + 70,000 = ₹5,50,000. 12,50,000 + 5,50,000 − 45,000 = **PGBP ₹17,55,000.**

## Exam traps & must-remember
- **40A(3) is a cliff:** ₹10,001 cash → whole amount gone; aggregate to one person in one day counts.
- **40(a)(ia) = 30% (resident); 40(a)(i) = 100% (non-resident).**
- **43B turns on Sec 139(1) return due date**, not 31 March — except 43B(h) MSE (no grace); interest converted to fresh loan ≠ paid.
- **Employee PF [36(1)(va)] (PF/ESI Act clock, permanently lost if late) ≠ Employer PF [43B] (ITR clock).**
- Income-tax disallowed [40(a)(ii)]; GST/duties are 43B items (allowed if paid).
- Always **reverse book depreciation, substitute Sec 32**; depreciation is **compulsory** (Expl 5).
- "Repairs" that are a new asset → add back, cost re-enters via depreciation.
- 180-day rule needs **acquired AND put to use**; add all additions before testing block negativity.
- **Goodwill no longer depreciable.**
- **40A(2): only excess disallowed; 40A(3): whole disallowed.**
- 40(b): only working partners, deed-authorised & quantified; sleeping partner remuneration fully disallowed.
- Sec 41 survives discontinuance; CSR not a Sec 37 deduction.

## One-line recall
- Business income = net; reconcile from book profit — add what lowered it, deduct what raised it.
- Anti-abuse locks: 40A(3) cash trail, 40A(2) related-party excess, 40 TDS chain, 43B pay-or-lose, 36(1)(va) worker's stricter clock.
- Depreciation: block/WDV, half-rate <180 days, max 40%, additional 20%, compulsory; Sec 50 STCG only if block dies/goes negative.
- Presumptive: 44AD 8%/6% (≤₹2cr/₹3cr, 5-yr lock-in), 44ADA 50% (≤₹50L/₹75L, no lock-in), 44AE ₹1,000/tonne or ₹7,500 (≤10 vehicles).
- Carry-forward: business loss 8 yrs, unabsorbed dep indefinite (not salary), speculation loss 4 yrs.
- Sec 41 claws back written-back liabilities & recovered bad debts even after the business closes.
