# GST: Concept & Framework — Revision Note

## Snapshot
GST = single, destination-based, value-added tax on consumption, with seamless ITC; delivered as a DUAL levy (Centre + State) because India is a federation. Cures 3 pre-GST diseases: (1) cascading/tax-on-tax, (2) fragmented non-fungible credit silos, (3) inter-State barrier (CST + check-posts). Taxable event = supply (not manufacture/sale/service).

**Sanity identity:** Net GST to govt (whole chain) = Rate × Final consumption value. If it fails → a credit link leaked (composition dealer / exempt supply / blocked credit u/s 17(5) / unregistered link).

## Core concepts
- **Value-added view = consumption view:** sum of value additions along a chain = final selling price; taxing increments collects same total as taxing the whole once. Collected in self-policing instalments (buyer demands invoice for ITC → pulls seller into net; fights evasion too).
- **Destination-based:** tax accrues where consumed, not where produced/originated.
- **IGST is a clearing pipe, NOT a third tax:** IGST = CGST rate + SGST rate; Centre collects, apportions SGST share to destination State via Art. 269A. Buyer's act of claiming credit triggers revenue to reach correct govt.

## Key provisions / rules

### Constitutional pillars (101st Amendment Act, 2016)
| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| 246A | Concurrent power to Centre & States to levy GST; also carves out petro *timing* |
| 269A | Centre levies & collects IGST on inter-State supply, then apportions |
| 279A | GST Council (279A(5) lists 5 petro products for Council to decide levy date) |
| 366(12A) | Defines GST; excludes alcoholic liquor for human consumption (by definition) |

### Charging sections
- **Sec 9 CGST** — intra-State supplies, value u/s 15, rate ≤ 20%. 9(2) petro deferral; 9(3) RCM notified; 9(4) RCM from unregistered (notified); 9(5) ECO liability.
- **Sec 5 IGST** — inter-State + imports; ceiling 40%. IGST on imported **goods** collected under Customs Tariff Act at customs (NOT GST machinery).

### Which tax applies
| Supplier location vs Place of supply | Nature | Tax |
|---|---|---|
| Same State/UT | Intra-State (Sec 8 IGST) | CGST + SGST/UTGST |
| Different State/UT, import, export, SEZ | Inter-State (Sec 7 IGST) | IGST |

SEZ supply = inter-State (IGST) even within same State.

### UTGST vs SGST — test is LEGISLATURE, not statehood
- Have legislature → SGST: **Delhi, Puducherry, J&K**.
- No legislature → UTGST: Chandigarh, A&N, Lakshadweep, Ladakh, DNH&DD.

### ITC set-off order (Sec 49, 49A, 49B; Rule 88A)
| Credit of | Used against (in order) | Never against |
|---|---|---|
| IGST | IGST → CGST → SGST (exhaust first; leftover split freely) | — |
| CGST | IGST → CGST | SGST |
| SGST/UTGST | IGST → SGST | CGST |
Central & State money never cross directly; IGST is neutral bridge.

### Thresholds (Sec 22) — aggregate turnover (Sec 2(6): PAN-based, all-India, excl. GST)
| Category | Goods | Services |
|---|---|---|
| Normal States | ₹40 lakh | ₹20 lakh |
| Special category States | ₹20 lakh | ₹10 lakh |
₹40 L goods limit only if EXCLUSIVELY goods. Sec 24 compulsory registration overrides thresholds (inter-State goods supplier, casual/non-resident TP, RCM liable, ECO, ISD, agents).

### Composition (Sec 10) — verify figures
~₹1.5 cr goods (1%: 0.5+0.5 traders/mfr), restaurants 5% (2.5+2.5), Sec 10(2A) services 6% up to ₹50 L. No ITC, no tax collection, no inter-State outward supply, Bill of Supply.

### GST Council (Art. 279A)
- Chair = Union FM; members = Union MoS Finance + State FMs; Vice-Chair from States.
- Quorum = 1/2. Decision = ≥ **3/4** of weighted votes. Centre = **1/3**, States (together) = **2/3**.
- Mutual veto: Centre alone 33.3% < 75% (can't pass, but can block); States alone 66.6% < 75% (can't pass without Centre). Recommendations **recommendatory**, not binding (Mohit Minerals).

### Taxes subsumed / kept out
- **Central subsumed:** Excise, Service Tax, CVD, SAD, central cesses.
- **State subsumed:** VAT, CST, Entry Tax/Octroi, Luxury, Entertainment (non-local), Purchase, betting/lottery.
- CVD/SAD in (IGST-on-imports replaces them); **BCD out** (a protective tariff).
- **Kept OUT (5 kings):** Alcoholic liquor (by definition — needs amendment), 5 petro products (by timing — needs Council date: crude, HSD, petrol, natural gas, ATF), Electricity, Stamp duty, Basic Customs Duty. Tobacco IN but central excise also possible.

## Worked mini-example
Intra-State chain @18%, Mfr base ₹10,000 → Distributor +₹4,000 → Retailer +₹6,000.
- Mfr: GST 1,800, ITC 0, net **1,800**.
- Distributor: sale 14,000, GST 2,520, ITC 1,800, net **720**.
- Retailer: sale 20,000, GST 3,600, ITC 2,520, net **1,080**.
- Total to govt = 1,800+720+1,080 = **3,600** = 18% × 20,000. ✔ Cascade dead.

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. IGST is NOT a third tax; = CGST + SGST rate, Centre collects, routes to destination.
2. CGST ↮ SGST can NEVER offset each other; only IGST bridges (may force cash even when other-head credit sits idle).
3. IGST credit must be exhausted FIRST; then leftover split freely (Rule 88A) — steer to head lacking other credit to minimise cash.
4. ₹40 L limit = goods only & exclusively goods; services ₹20 L (₹10 L special).
5. Sec 24 compulsory registration overrides "turnover below threshold."
6. Inter-State SGST share → CONSUMING State, not supplier's State.
7. SEZ = inter-State even within same State.
8. IGST on imported goods under Customs Tariff Act; BCD not subsumed.
9. Liquor out by **definition** (Art 366(12A), needs amendment); petro out by **timing** (Council date).
10. Council vote: Centre 1/3, States 2/3, pass 3/4, quorum 1/2 → mutual veto.
11. Taxable event = SUPPLY, not manufacture.
12. Composition/exempt/unregistered link breaks "rate × final value" identity.

## One-line recall
- GST = single destination-based value-added consumption tax; dual levy (federation) = CGST + SGST/UTGST; inter-State = IGST.
- Constitutional: 246A (power + petro timing), 269A (IGST), 279A (Council), 366(12A) (defines, excludes liquor); 101st Amendment.
- Set-off: IGST first (split leftover); CGST never→SGST; SGST never→CGST.
- Thresholds: goods ₹40L (exclusively)/services ₹20L; special ₹20L/₹10L; Sec 24 overrides.
- Council: 1/3–2/3, pass 3/4, quorum 1/2, recommendatory.
- Out: liquor (definition), 5 petro (timing), electricity, stamp duty, BCD.
