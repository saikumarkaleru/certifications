# Working Capital Management

## Snapshot
Profit is annual and optional; solvency is daily and non-negotiable. A profitable firm can be **solvent but illiquid** and fail on a Tuesday. WCM = sizing the money frozen in the operating pipeline, shrinking it, and funding it.

**Net Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities** = (Inventory + Receivables + Cash) − (Payables + short-term dues).

Three-way distinction: **profitability** (income statement) ≠ **liquidity** (near-term) ≠ **solvency** (long-term). Technical insolvency kills before balance-sheet insolvency appears.

## Core concepts
- **Liquidity–profitability trade-off:** more current assets = safety, lower ROI (ROI = Profit ÷ Investment; WC in denominator). Conservative (safe, low ROI) / Moderate (matching) / Aggressive (risky, high ROI).
- **Gross WC** = total current assets (treasurer's view); **Net WC** = CA − CL (financier's view).
- **Permanent WC** (always-full core, a rising staircase — funded long-term) vs **Fluctuating/temporary WC** (seasonal spikes, self-liquidating — funded short-term).
- **Cycle differs by industry:** trading light (no WIP), heavy engineering huge WIP, fast-food/transport can be negative (paid before paying suppliers), service = big debtors no inventory.
- **Determinants:** nature of business, scale, production cycle length, seasonal fluctuations, credit policy given/received, growth, operating efficiency/turnover, inflation, RM availability, dividend/depreciation policy.

## Key provisions / rules

**Operating cycle (denominators differ — the #1 slip)**
| Leg | Formula (days) | Denominator flow |
|---|---|---|
| RM holding (R) | Avg RM stock ÷ (Annual RM consumption ÷ 365) | RM consumption |
| WIP holding (W) | Avg WIP ÷ (Annual cost of production ÷ 365) | Cost of production |
| FG holding (F) | Avg FG stock ÷ (Annual COGS ÷ 365) | COGS |
| Debtors (D) | Avg debtors ÷ (Annual credit sales ÷ 365) | Credit sales |
| Creditors (C) | Avg creditors ÷ (Annual credit purchases ÷ 365) | Credit purchases |

- **Gross Operating Cycle = R + W + F + D**
- **Cash Conversion Cycle = R + W + F + D − C**
- Cycles per year = 365 ÷ cycle days; **WC ≈ annual cash operating cost ÷ (365 ÷ cycle days)** (cross-check).
- **Consumption = Opening stock + Purchases − Closing stock** (RM leg uses consumption; creditors leg uses purchases — back out purchases if only consumption given).
- Use average of opening/closing when both given; else state assumption.

**Estimation (line-by-line)**
- Current assets: RM (consumption × days ÷ 365); **WIP (RM at 100%, labour & overheads at % completion, often 50%)**; FG (COGS × days); Debtors (at cost of sales OR sales per instruction); cash float; prepaid/advances to suppliers (add).
- Less spontaneous liabilities: creditors (purchases × days), outstanding wages (wages × lag), outstanding overheads, advances received from customers (deduct).
- Net WC = CA − CL; add contingency margin only if asked.
- **Cash-cost basis:** strip non-cash depreciation from FG and debtors valuation; leave creditors/wages untouched.
- **% of sales method** = quick first-cut (WC ≈ % × forecast sales); blind to efficiency changes.

**Inventory**
| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| EOQ | √(2 × A × O ÷ C) — A=annual usage, O=order cost, C=carry cost/unit/yr |
| At EOQ | total ordering cost = total carrying cost |
| Orders/year | A ÷ EOQ; average inventory = EOQ ÷ 2 (+ safety stock) |
| Re-order level | Max usage × Max lead time |
| Minimum level | Reorder level − (normal usage × normal lead time) |
| Inventory turnover | COGS ÷ average inventory |

Also: ABC analysis (vital few A vs trivial many C), safety stock, JIT, quantity discounts (compare total annual cost = purchase + ordering + carrying at EOQ vs each discount qty).

**Receivables (credit policy):** relax credit only if **extra contribution from extra sales > extra debtor-carrying cost + extra bad debts + extra discounts**. Opportunity cost charged on **investment in debtors (at cost), not sales value**. Levers: credit period, cash discount (e.g. 2/10 net 30), credit standards (5 Cs — Character, Capacity, Capital, Collateral, Conditions), collection effort. Monitor: ageing schedule, average collection period.

**Payables (spontaneous, free until discount forgone):**
- **Cost of forgoing discount ≈ [ d ÷ (100 − d) ] × [ 365 ÷ (credit period − discount period) ]**
- Take discount if this annualised cost > short-term borrowing rate; else keep free credit. Denominator uses *actual delay* if firm stretches beyond net date.
- Spontaneous finance (trade credit, outstanding wages) vs negotiated (cash credit, overdraft, CP — always priced).

**Cash:** three motives — transaction, precautionary, speculative.
| Model | Formula | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Baumol (steady) | C* = √(2 × b × T ÷ i) | predictable drain (EOQ twin) |
| Miller-Orr spread | Z = 3 × ∛(3 × b × σ² ÷ 4i) | volatile two-way flows |
| Miller-Orr return point | Lower limit + (Spread ÷ 3) | |
| Miller-Orr upper limit | Lower limit + Spread | |
Baumol: at C*, transaction cost = interest forgone; conversions/year = T ÷ C*; avg cash = C*/2. Float toolkit: speed collection (lock-box, e-transfer), slow disbursement (pay on due date), concentration banking.

**Financing — matching (hedging) principle:** fund asset with source of matching maturity. Permanent WC ← long-term (equity, debentures, term loan, retained profit); fluctuating WC ← short-term (cash credit, overdraft, bill discounting, CP, trade credit). Short cheaper but carries **refinancing risk + interest-rate risk**.
- Short-term sources: trade credit (spontaneous), cash credit/overdraft, bill/invoice discounting, **factoring** (recourse = firm bears bad debt, cheaper / non-recourse = factor bears it, dearer), commercial paper (high-rated only), public deposits.
- **Working-capital gap (MPBF) = Current assets − current liabilities other than bank borrowing.** Tandon Method I: bank funds 75% of gap (borrower 25%). Method II: borrower funds 25% of *total current assets* (stricter, target current ratio ≈ 1.33:1). Method III: strictest (core CA also long-term-funded). Verify exact % / AY.

## Worked mini-example
**Estimation with WIP nuance (Ganga Products):** 60,000 units. RM ₹80, labour ₹30, OH ₹30, SP ₹160. Annual RM 48,00,000; labour 18,00,000; OH 18,00,000; total cost 84,00,000. Norms: RM 1 mth; WIP 0.5 mth (RM 100%, conversion 50%); FG 1 mth; Debtors 2 mth at cost; creditors 1 mth; wages lag 0.5 mth; +10% contingency; cash ₹1,50,000.
- RM stock = 48,00,000 × 1/12 = 4,00,000
- WIP: RM 48,00,000 × 0.5/12 × 100% = 2,00,000; Labour 18,00,000 × 0.5/12 × 50% = 37,500; OH = 37,500
- FG = 84,00,000 × 1/12 = 7,00,000; Debtors = 84,00,000 × 2/12 = 14,00,000; Cash = 1,50,000
- **Total CA = 29,25,000**
- Creditors = 48,00,000 × 1/12 = 4,00,000; Wages = 18,00,000 × 0.5/12 = 75,000; **CL = 4,75,000**
- Net WC = 24,50,000; +10% = 2,45,000; **WC required = ₹26,95,000**
(If debtors at sales: 96,00,000 × 2/12 = 16,00,000, net WC +₹2,00,000.)

**Cost of forgoing discount (2/15 net 45, borrow 18%):** [2/98] × [365/30] = 0.020408 × 12.1667 = **24.8%** > 18% → **take the discount**. (If firm stretches to day 60: [2/98] × [365/45] = 16.5% < 18% → forgo discount, pay day 60.)

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **Wrong denominator per stage** (RM→consumption, WIP→cost of production, FG→COGS, debtors→sales, creditors→purchases). Using "sales" for all = #1 error.
2. WIP conversion cost scaled to completion; RM in WIP at 100% (don't halve RM).
3. Debtors at cost vs sales — read the instruction, state assumption.
4. Debtor/creditor periods use **credit** (not total) sales/purchases.
5. **Consumption vs purchases** in creditors leg — back out purchases if needed.
6. 365 vs 360 vs 12×30 days — use as specified, don't mix mid-solution.
7. Contingency margin — apply only if asked, on net WC.
8. Cash-cost basis: strip depreciation from FG & debtors; leave creditors/wages.
9. Cost of forgoing discount — take discount when its cost **exceeds** borrowing rate; denominator = (net period − discount period) or (actual delay − discount period) if stretched.
10. Opportunity cost on debtors **at cost**, not sales.
11. Baumol (steady) vs Miller-Orr (volatile) — naming wrong model loses conceptual marks.
12. Square-root damping: 4× rise in order/transaction cost only **doubles** the optimum.
13. Keep gross/net and permanent/fluctuating pairs straight.
14. Trade credit free only until discount forgone (then 20–40% implied).
15. MPBF % — verify current norms; rely on principle (bank funds only the gap).

## One-line recall
- Profit is annual and optional; solvency is daily and non-negotiable.
- CCC = R + W + F + D − C; each leg = avg balance ÷ (annual flow ÷ days); match numerator valuation to denominator flow.
- WIP: RM 100%, conversion at % completion; debtors at cost or sales per instruction.
- EOQ = √(2AO/C); Baumol C* = √(2bT/i); at optimum the two costs are equal; 4× input → 2× optimum.
- Take a cash discount when [d/(100−d)]×[365/(credit−discount period)] > borrowing rate.
- Matching principle: permanent WC ← long funds, fluctuating WC ← short funds; MPBF = CA − non-bank CL.
