# Ratio Analysis (Financial Analysis & Planning)

## Snapshot
A ratio = one figure ÷ another chosen so the answer means something (manufactures a yardstick). Ratios are scale-free and compared against three benchmarks: own past (trend), rival (cross-section), industry norm. Organised by four families serving the three FM decisions. No single "correct" value — context-dependent.

## Core concepts
- **Four forms:** proportion "x:1" (same kind of thing), "%" (return/part-of-whole), "times" (coverage/velocity), "days" (duration). Times × days = period; turnover and holding period are reciprocals scaled by period.
- **Stock ratio** (two balance-sheet items, no averaging) vs **flow-to-stock ratio** (P&L flow over balance stock → use **average of opening & closing** denominator).
- **Liquidity-profitability trade-off** is why a *panel* of ratios is needed; high liquidity lowers return.
- Reading direction: Debt-Equity, Capital Gearing, Debt Ratio → higher = riskier. Proprietary + all coverage ratios → higher = safer.

## Key provisions / rules

| # | Ratio | Formula | Ideal/sense |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Liquidity** ||||
| 1 | Current | Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities | 2:1 |
| 2 | Quick/Acid | (CA − Inventory − Prepaid) ÷ CL | 1:1 |
| 3 | Cash/Absolute | (Cash+Bank+Marketable sec) ÷ CL | 0.5:1 |
| — | Net Working Capital | CA − CL | positive cushion |
| **Solvency/Leverage** ||||
| 4 | Debt-Equity | Long-term Debt ÷ Shareholders' Funds | ≤2:1 |
| 5 | Proprietary | Shareholders' Funds ÷ Total Assets | higher safer |
| 6 | Capital Gearing | (Pref + Debt) ÷ (Equity cap + Reserves) | context |
| 7 | Interest Coverage | EBIT ÷ Interest | higher safer |
| — | Debt to Total Assets | Total Debt ÷ Total Assets = 1 − Proprietary | — |
| — | DSCR | (PAT + Dep + Int) ÷ (Int + Principal) | ~1.5-2 |
| — | Fixed Charges Coverage | (EBIT + fixed charges pre-tax) ÷ (Int + fixed charges + Pref div grossed up) | — |
| **Activity/Turnover** ||||
| 8 | Inventory Turnover | COGS ÷ Avg Inventory | higher=leaner |
| 9 | Inventory period | 360 ÷ Inventory Turnover | fewer days |
| 10 | Debtors Turnover | Credit Sales ÷ Avg Receivables | higher |
| 11 | Collection period | 360 ÷ Debtors Turnover | fewer days |
| 12 | Creditors Turnover | Credit Purchases ÷ Avg Payables | context |
| 13 | Payment period | 360 ÷ Creditors Turnover | longer=free finance |
| 14 | Operating Cycle | Inventory period + Collection period | fewer |
| 15 | Cash Conversion Cycle | Inv + Debtor − Creditor period | fewer |
| 16-19 | Fixed Asset / Total Asset / Capital / WC Turnover | Sales ÷ (Net FA / Total Assets / Capital Employed / NWC) | higher |
| **Margins (÷Sales)** ||||
| 20 | Gross Profit | Gross Profit ÷ Sales | higher |
| 21 | Operating Profit | EBIT ÷ Sales | higher |
| 22 | Operating Ratio | (COGS + Op exp) ÷ Sales | Op Ratio + Op Profit Ratio = 100% |
| 23 | Net Profit | PAT ÷ Sales | higher |
| **Returns (÷Capital)** ||||
| 24 | ROCE | EBIT ÷ Capital Employed | higher |
| 25 | ROE / Return on Net Worth | PAT ÷ Shareholders' Funds | higher |
| 26 | ROA | PAT (or EBIT) ÷ Total Assets | higher |
| — | Sustainable Growth Rate | ROE × Retention (b) | growth w/o new equity |
| **Valuation (÷share)** ||||
| 27 | EPS | (PAT − Pref Div) ÷ No. equity shares | higher |
| 28 | P/E | Market Price ÷ EPS | growth signal |
| 29 | Dividend Payout | DPS ÷ EPS | policy |
| 30 | Retention | 1 − Payout | policy |
| 31 | Dividend Yield | DPS ÷ MPS | income |
| 32 | Earnings Yield | EPS ÷ MPS = 1 ÷ (P/E) | valuation |
| 33 | Book Value/Share | (Equity cap + Reserves) ÷ equity shares | — |
| — | Dividend Cover | EPS ÷ DPS = 1 ÷ Payout | higher safer |
| — | Market-to-Book | MPS ÷ Book Value/Share | >1 = value created |

- **Capital Employed** = Total Assets − Current Liabilities = Shareholders' Funds + Long-term Debt.
- **Quick Assets** = CA − Inventory − Prepaid. **Shareholders' funds** = Equity cap + Reserves + Preference cap − fictitious assets.
- **DuPont 3-step:** ROE = (PAT/Sales) × (Sales/Total Assets) × (Total Assets/Shareholders' Funds) = Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier (levers = operating/pricing, investing, financing).
- **DuPont 5-step:** ROE = (PAT/PBT) × (PBT/EBIT) × (EBIT/Sales) × (Sales/Assets) × (Assets/Equity) = Tax burden × Interest burden × Op margin × Asset TO × Leverage.
- **ROCE 2-factor:** ROCE = Capital Turnover × Operating Margin.
- Manufacturing operating cycle splits into RM + WIP + FG holding periods + debtors − creditors.

## Worked mini-example (reverse ratios)
Current Ratio 2.5:1, Quick 1.5:1, WC ₹6,00,000, Inventory TO 8× on COGS, GP 25%, collection 36.5 days (365-yr).
- CL: 2.5x − x = 1.5x = 6,00,000 → x = **CL ₹4,00,000**, **CA ₹10,00,000**.
- Quick Assets = 1.5 × 4,00,000 = 6,00,000 → **Inventory = 10,00,000 − 6,00,000 = ₹4,00,000**.
- COGS = 8 × 4,00,000 = 32,00,000; Sales = 32,00,000 ÷ 0.75 = **₹42,66,667**.
- Debtors = 36.5 × 42,66,667 ÷ 365 = **₹4,26,667** (fits inside quick assets ✓).

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. Quick assets also remove **prepaid expenses**, not just inventory.
2. Bank overdraft in quick ratio — chronic OD sometimes excluded; **state assumption**.
3. Debtors turnover uses **credit sales**; creditors uses **credit purchases**.
4. Turnover ratios use **average** (opening+closing)/2 when opening available.
5. Capital Employed two routes must reconcile (TA − CL = SHF + LTD); else misclassification.
6. ROCE numerator = **EBIT** (pre-interest, pre-tax), not PAT.
7. EPS = earnings **after** preference dividend ÷ equity shares.
8. High ratio ≠ good (CR 6:1 = idle cash; high debtors period = lax credit).
9. Slower payables = often favourable (free finance); not "faster = better."
10. DuPont ROE denominator must be consistent (total vs equity-only funds).
11. Inventory turnover = **COGS ÷ Inventory** (both at cost), not Sales.
12. Fictitious assets (preliminary exp, discount on shares, accumulated losses) → deduct from SHF & total assets.
13. Gross up post-tax charges (pref div, principal) by ÷(1−t) in coverage ratios.
14. Use correct units: "2:1", "6 times", "60 days", "%".
15. Effect-on-ratio: ratio >1 → equal addition to both sides pulls down toward 1, equal subtraction pushes up; <1 reverses; intra-side swap = no change.
16. Times ↔ days are reciprocals scaled by 360/365.

## One-line recall
- Ratio manufactures a yardstick; judge against trend, peer, industry — never in isolation.
- Four families: Liquidity, Solvency, Activity, Profitability/Valuation.
- Capital Employed = TA − CL = SHF + LTD; Op Ratio + Op Profit Ratio = 100%; Debt Ratio + Proprietary = 100%.
- DuPont ROE = margin × turnover × leverage (must reconcile to direct PAT/SHF).
- SGR = ROE × retention; Earnings Yield = 1/(P/E).
- Window-dressing: paying a creditor before B/S date raises current ratio (>1) without real improvement.
