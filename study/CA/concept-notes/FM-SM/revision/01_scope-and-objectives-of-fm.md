# Scope & Objectives of Financial Management

## Snapshot
FM plans and controls the firm's financial resources through three (+1) decisions serving one master goal: maximise shareholder **wealth**. Wealth = NPV = present value of expected future *cash* flows discounted for time and risk. Core engine of the whole subject = Time Value of Money.

## Core concepts
- **Three decisions:** Investment (capital budgeting + working capital), Financing (capital structure, minimise k), Dividend (payout vs retain). **Fourth silent one:** Liquidity (sub-part of investment; profitability vs liquidity tension).
- **Traditional view (pre-1950s):** finance = procurement of funds only. **Modern view:** procurement AND efficient utilisation (continuous, decision-oriented). ICAI examines modern.
- **3 charges against traditional view:** (i) outsider-looking (only fund suppliers' viewpoint); (ii) over-emphasised episodic long-term financing, ignored working-capital finance; (iii) fund-raising only, ignored allocation/utilisation.
- **Investment decision = most important** — it *creates* value on the asset side; financing/dividend merely redistribute/re-price it.

## Key provisions / rules
Decision → tool: Investment(LT)→NPV/IRR/PI/payback; Investment(ST)→operating cycle, liquidity-profitability; Financing→WACC, leverage, EBIT-EPS; Dividend→Walter/Gordon/MM, payout ratio.

**Profit vs Wealth maximisation** — profit fails on 4 counts: **Ambiguous** (PBT/PAT? total/per share? short/long run) · **Ignores timing** (time value) · **Ignores risk** · **Ignores cash quality** (profit = opinion, cash = fact). Wealth fixes all four. Profit = *necessary* condition for survival but *wrong objective*. EPS maximisation still fails on timing/risk/dividend and is manipulable. ICAI objective = **shareholder** wealth (not total firm value) within ethical/stakeholder constraints (constraints, not competing objectives).

**Discount rate decomposition:** k ≈ real risk-free rate + inflation premium + risk premium.

| Concept | Formula |
|---|---|
| NPV (wealth) | NPV = Σ [Cₜ ÷ (1+k)ᵗ] − C₀ ; accept if NPV > 0 |
| FV single sum | FV = PV × (1+i)ⁿ  (factor = FVIF) |
| PV single sum | PV = FV ÷ (1+i)ⁿ  (factor = PVIF) |
| Intra-year compounding | FV = PV × (1 + i/m)^(m×n) |
| Effective annual rate | EAR = (1 + i/m)^m − 1 ; continuous → e^i − 1 |
| FV ordinary annuity | FVA = A × [((1+i)ⁿ − 1) ÷ i]  (FVIFA) |
| PV ordinary annuity | PVA = A × [(1 − (1+i)⁻ⁿ) ÷ i]  (PVIFA) |
| Annuity due | multiply ordinary result × (1+i) |
| Deferred annuity | PV = A × PVIFA(i,n) × PVIF(i,d) ; d = deferment |
| Perpetuity | PV = A ÷ i |
| Growing perpetuity | PV = A₁ ÷ (i − g) ; needs i > g |
| Sinking fund (deposit) | A = FV × [i ÷ ((1+i)ⁿ − 1)] = FV ÷ FVIFA |
| Loan instalment / capital recovery | A = PV × [i ÷ (1 − (1+i)⁻ⁿ)] = PV ÷ PVIFA |
| Rule of 72 | doubling years ≈ 72 ÷ rate% (quick check only) |

- **Sinking-fund factor = 1/FVIFA; capital-recovery factor = 1/PVIFA** — memorise 2 factors, invert when instalment is unknown.
- **Amortisation:** interest = opening balance × i (on *declining* balance); principal = instalment − interest; total interest = (n × instalment) − principal.
- **Agency:** owner(principal) vs manager(agent) → agency costs = **monitoring** (principal) + **bonding** (agent) + **residual loss**. NOT the manager's salary. Fixes: ESOPs/incentive pay, board & audit, debt covenants, takeover threat, SEBI. **Second conflict:** shareholders vs debt-holders (asset substitution, excess dividends) → curbed by covenants.
- **Treasurer** (outward/forward: funds, cash, credit, investment, insurance) vs **Controller** (inward/backward: accounting, audit, budgeting, tax, internal control). Both under CFO.

## Worked mini-example
Retain-or-pay (₹10,00,000 surplus; shareholders' opportunity rate k = 12%). Retain only if reinvestment r > k.
- Scenario A: r = 15% → ₹11,50,000 in 1 yr → PV = 11,50,000 × 0.8929 = ₹10,26,835 > ₹10,00,000 → **Retain** (+₹26,835).
- Scenario B: r = 9% → ₹10,90,000 → PV = ₹9,73,261 < ₹10,00,000 → **Pay out** (−₹26,739).
- Break-even r = k = 12%: retention → ₹11,20,000 → PV = ₹10,00,000 (indifference).

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. "Beginning of each year" = **annuity due** → × (1+i). Most common lost mark.
2. "12% compounded quarterly" ≠ 12% effective → use i/m and m×n, or EAR. Monthly EMIs: m=12.
3. **FVIFA vs PVIFA:** accumulate to future target → FVIFA (sinking fund); value stream today / loan instalment → PVIFA.
4. NPV uses **cash flows**, not accounting profit (add back depreciation).
5. Different risk ⇒ different discount rate. Never lazily apply one rate to two projects.
6. "Equal total cash ⇒ equal projects" — false; timing & risk differ; always discount.
7. Perpetuity A/i only for "forever"; fixed years → PVIFA.
8. Growing perpetuity needs **i > g**; if g ≥ i formula invalid.
9. Wealth maximisation ≠ share-price manipulation; it's *sustainable* value within ethics/stakeholders.
10. Agency cost = cost of conflict + control (monitoring/bonding/residual), NOT salary.
11. **Deferred annuity:** PVIFA reaches one period *before* first flow, then extra PVIF discount.
12. Amortisation interest on **declining** balance (flat-rate error overstates interest).
13. Profit maximisation: state it's *necessary but insufficient*, not "stupid."
14. EAR ceiling = e^i − 1 (continuous); more frequency = diminishing returns.
15. Objective = **shareholder** wealth, not total firm value (matters when value shifts lender↔owner).

## One-line recall
- Wealth = Σ Cₜ/(1+k)ᵗ − C₀; beats profit on timing, risk, ambiguity, cash quality.
- Three decisions: invest · finance · dividend (+ liquidity); investment decision creates value.
- Compound to go forward, discount to come back; sinking-fund & loan factors are inverted annuity factors.
- Retain only if r > k; else distribute.
- Agency cost = monitoring + bonding + residual loss; align via ESOPs, covenants, takeover threat.
- Amortisation: interest = opening × i on declining balance; principal = instalment − interest.
