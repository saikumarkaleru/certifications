# Incorporation of a Company

## Snapshot
Companies Act 2013 + Companies (Incorporation) Rules 2014. Incorporation = a public, verified birth granting separate personality + limited liability, in exchange for a registered constitution (MOA outward + AOA inward). Three balancing doctrines: **ultra vires** (managers can't exceed objects), **constructive notice** (outsiders deemed to know public docs — protects company), **indoor management/Turquand** (outsiders may assume internal compliance — protects outsider). Certificate of incorporation is conclusive on technical defects but NOT a fraud shield (Sec 7(5)–(7)).

## Core concepts
- Promoter [2(69)]: named in prospectus/return, OR controls affairs, OR Board accustomed to act on his instructions. Professional-capacity carve-out. Fiduciary — no secret profit, full disclosure to independent Board/members.
- Pre-incorporation contract: company **cannot ratify** (no principal existed) but **can adopt** under Specific Relief Act 1963 Secs 15(h)/19(e) if for company's purposes + adopted. Promoter personally liable (*Kelner v Baxter*: signed as agent → liable; *Newborne v Sensolid*: signed in company's own name → no contract, no one liable).
- Ultra vires the COMPANY (beyond MOA objects) = void ab initio, un-ratifiable even unanimously (*Ashbury*). Ultra vires the DIRECTORS/articles = within capacity, ratifiable. Ultra vires borrowing: loan void but lender recovers via tracing/subrogation.
- MOA supreme over AOA; both subordinate to the Act.

**Conversions:** Private→Public (SR + raise members 7/directors 3, file RoC — no RD, easier). Public→Private (SR + **Regional Director** approval — harder, reduces oversight). OPC↔Private/Public via INC-6 (2021 removed mandatory thresholds & 2-yr wait). Sec 8: formed by CG licence, may drop "Limited", conversion needs surrender of licence + RD approval.

**Registered office move (jurisdiction, not State, is the trigger):** same local limits → Board resolution + INC-22; another city same RoC → SR + INC-22; different RoC (even same State) → SR + RD; another State → SR + RD [13(4)] + advertisement + creditor window.

**Separate personality package** (*Salomon*): separate entity, limited liability, perpetual succession, *Macaura* (no member insurable interest), *Lee* (shareholder can be employee), *Bacha Guzdar* (dividend not agricultural income), company not a citizen (*STC*).

## Exam traps & must-remember
- Ratify vs Adopt: company cannot ratify pre-incorporation contract but CAN adopt (Specific Relief Act).
- Ultra vires the company (void, un-ratifiable) vs ultra vires directors (ratifiable). Ultra vires borrowing: lender recovers via tracing/subrogation, not "gets nothing".
- Constructive notice protects the COMPANY; indoor management protects the OUTSIDER. Turquand never cures forgery (*Ruben*), or where outsider knew/was put on inquiry (*Anand Bihari Lal*), or acts beyond ostensible authority.
- Certificate conclusive on technical defects (even the date) but Sec 7(5)-(7) allows NCLT action for fraud.
- Sec 11 commencement certificate omitted (2015); current gate is **Sec 10A (2018)** — 180 days/INC-20A, only share-capital companies. Loan on day 40 = penalised breach, not void.
- MOA fixes only the **State**; exact address is Sec 12/INC-22. RD confirmation needed only if move crosses RoC jurisdiction, not merely State line.
- Disclosure of secret profit to a controlled Board = no disclosure (*Erlanger*).
- Alteration of articles valid despite breaching a contract → give two-part answer (valid + damages).
- Sec 8 name may drop "Limited"; MOA alteration needs CG approval.
- Sec 3A is live law (2017) — grace 6 months + member "aware"; only post-grace debts, only aware members.
- Entrenchment after formation needs ALL members (not bare SR); can only make alteration harder.
- Sec 464 ceiling is **50** (cap 100) — different from private-co 200-member cap; HUF & professionals excepted.
- Small company is a status not a formation type; toggles off on crossing thresholds.
- A lone director / company secretary cannot bind the company by office alone.

## One-line recall
- Ultra vires company = void & un-ratifiable; ultra vires directors = ratifiable; constructive notice shields company, Turquand shields outsider (not for forgery/knowledge/suspicion).
- Sec 10A: 180 days to prove money (INC-20A) + 30 days to prove address (INC-22); share-capital cos only.
- MOA alteration = SR (+CG name / +RD another State); AOA = SR; certificate conclusive but not a fraud shield (7(5)-(7)).
- Pre-incorporation contract: promoter personally liable (Kelner) / no contract (Newborne); company adopts (not ratifies) under Specific Relief Act.
- Sec 464 illegal association >50 (cap 100); Sec 3A below-minimum + >6 months + aware → personal liability.
- Conversion carries debts (18(3)); Public→Private needs RD; Private→Public does not.
