# The General Clauses Act, 1897

## Snapshot
The "law about laws" — Central Act No. 10 of 1897, ~30 sections. A **meta-statute** supplying default meanings + interpretation rules for **every Central Act, Regulation and Ordinance**. Two things bundled: a **default dictionary** (S.3) and a **default rulebook** (Ss.5-30). Governing phrase: **"unless there is anything repugnant in the subject or context" / "unless a different intention appears"** → it is a **fallback, not a dictator**. Statutory embodiment of *generalia specialibus non derogant* (special beats general): **check the parent Act first; fall back to GCA only on silence.** An **aid to interpretation**, not a substantive law; NOT a code of interpretation (literal/golden/mischief live elsewhere).

## Core concepts
- Applies only to **Central** Acts/Regulations (+ Ordinances via S.30). States have their own General Clauses Acts.
- S.3 definitions apply to the GCA + all Central Acts **made after** 1897; S.4 extends a **subset** to earlier Acts.
- **"includes" = extending/open** (adds to ordinary meaning); **"means" = exhaustive/closed.**
- **Repeal changes the future, not the finished past** (S.6 five survivors).

|---|---|
| S.3(21) Financial Year | Year commencing **1 April** (Companies Act **S.2(41)** overrides for company law). |
| S.3(22) Good Faith | Done **honestly, whether negligently or not** (contrast **IPC** = due care & attention). |
| S.3(23) Government | Includes **both Central and State**. |
| S.3(26) Immovable Property | Includes land, benefits arising out of land, things attached to earth. |
| S.3(35) Month | **British/Gregorian calendar** month (NOT 30 days). |
| S.3(36) Movable Property | Everything **except** immovable property ("means" = exhaustive). |
| S.3(38) Offence | Any **act or omission** punishable by law in force. |
| S.3(42) Person | **Includes company, association, body of individuals**, incorporated or not. |
| S.3(66) Year | British calendar (1 Jan-31 Dec). |
| S.5 Commencement | If Act silent, in force on day of **assent**; effective from **start of that day** (preceding midnight). Fallback only for silence — a named/to-be-notified date displaces it. |
| **S.6 Effect of repeal** | Unless different intention appears, repeal does NOT: **(a)** revive anything not in force; **(b)** affect previous operation / things duly done; **(c)** affect right/privilege/obligation/liability **acquired, accrued or incurred**; **(d)** affect penalty/forfeiture/punishment incurred; **(e)** affect investigation/proceeding/remedy — may be **continued** as if not repealed. |
| S.6A | Repeal of an Act that merely **amended** another → amendment already made **stays**. |
| S.7 | **Revival** of a repealed enactment must be **expressly** stated. |
| S.8 | Reference to repealed & re-enacted provision → read as the **corresponding** new provision (needs a corresponding provision). |
| S.9 "from/to" | **"from" EXCLUDES** the first day; **"to" INCLUDES** the last day. ("From = First out, To = Terminal in.") |
| S.10 | Act in a **court/office** due on a day/last day that is **closed** → in time if done on **next open day**. **Proviso: does NOT apply to Limitation Act, 1963** matters (its own S.4). Only **last-day** closure counts (mid-period holiday = no extension). |
| S.13 | Masculine **includes feminine**; singular **includes plural** (and vice versa). |
| S.14 | Powers exercisable **from time to time** (not exhausted by one use). |
| S.15 | Appointment by **naming a person or designating the office**. |
| S.16 | Power to appoint **includes power to suspend or dismiss**. |
| S.21 | Power to make notifications/orders/rules/bye-laws **includes power to add/amend/vary/rescind** (same procedure). Not for quasi-judicial orders. |
| S.22 | Rules may be made **before commencement** (effective on commencement). |
| S.24 | Rules/orders/notifications under repealed law **continue** under re-enacted law, **so far as consistent, until superseded**. |
| S.25 | CrPC fine-recovery machinery applies to fines. |
| S.26 | Act an offence under **two+ enactments** → prosecute under **either/any**, but **NOT punished twice** for the **same offence** (same act + same ingredients). Mirrors Art. 20(2). |
| S.27 | Service "by post" deemed effected by **correct addressing + pre-paying + registered posting** (all three); time = ordinary course of post, **rebuttable** ("unless the contrary is proved"). |
| S.30 | GCA applies to **Ordinances** (Arts 123/213). |

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **Financial year:** GCA = 1 April; **Companies Act S.2(41) overrides** — special beats general.
2. **Good faith:** GCA = honesty (negligence irrelevant); **IPC = due care**. Answer depends on which Act governs.
3. **S.9:** "from" excludes first day; "to" includes last day. One-day error flips the answer.
4. **S.10 does NOT apply to Limitation Act 1963** matters; only **last-day** closure extends (not mid-period).
5. **"Month" = calendar month, not 30 days.** In a **non-leap February** this can pull a deadline *earlier*; where corresponding date doesn't exist, period ends on last day of that month.
6. **S.6 = default** ("unless different intention"); repealing Act may switch survivors off. Never state S.6 as absolute.
7. **Accrued right vs mere hope (6(c)):** only a right **perfected before** repeal survives; a pending application (still forming) can be extinguished.
8. **Repeal vs expiry:** S.6 saves on **repeal**; a temporary Act that **expires by efflux of time** is not "repealed" — check its own saving clause.
9. **S.26:** may be **prosecuted** under multiple laws, **not punished twice** — and only for the **same offence** (same act + same ingredients).
10. **S.27:** time-of-service presumption is **rebuttable, not conclusive**; needs **registered** post + correct address + pre-payment (all three); bare denial usually insufficient.
11. GCA governs **Central** Acts/Regulations/Ordinances only — not State laws.
12. **"includes" (open) vs "means" (closed)** — getting it backwards flips coverage.
13. **S.5 fallback only for silence** — "on such date as CG may notify" = dormant until notified (common Companies Act pattern; staggered commencement valid).
14. **S.21** does not let an authority reopen a **quasi-judicial** order.

## One-line recall
- GCA = **Dictionary + Rulebook**, both **default only** ("unless a different intention appears") — special beats general.
- **S.6 five survivors:** "No zombies · Past stands · Rights kept · Penalties due · Cases go on."
- **S.9:** "From = First out, To = Terminal in"; **month = calendar month**, not 30 days.
- **S.10:** office closed on last day → next open day (NOT for Limitation Act).
- **Living-power block (14/16/21):** make it & amend it; hire & fire; use it again — powers don't die on first use.
- **S.26** bars double *punishment* not double *prosecution*; **S.27** = Address + Pre-pay + Registered post = served (time rebuttable).
