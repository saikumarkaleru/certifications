# Registration of Charges — CA Inter Revision Note

> Companies Act 2013, Chapter VI, Sections 77–87 + Companies (Registration of Charges) Rules, 2014.

## Snapshot
- A charge is created by contract; **registration only perfects** it (makes it effective against third parties). Registration does NOT create the charge.
- Core bargain: register → whole world deemed to know (S.80) → priority + survives liquidation. Don't register → **void against liquidator & creditors** (S.77(3)), but **valid between company & lender** and **debt survives** (S.77(4)).
- Duty on **both** company (S.77) and charge-holder (S.78).
- Two registers: Registrar's public register (S.81) + company's own register at registered office (S.85).
- Certificate of registration (CHG-2) is **conclusive evidence** of due registration.

## Core concepts
- **Charge — S.2(16):** interest or lien on company property/assets as security; **includes mortgage**. Wide definition catches substance not label. Covers hypothecation (charge over movables, **no possession**). Pledge (possession passes to lender) generally NOT a S.77 registrable charge; lien generally not registrable. Registration matters most where asset **stays with company** (no outward warning). 2013 Act sweeps in *all* charges (dropped 1956's nine-category list).
- **Fixed vs floating charge:** Fixed = specific identified asset, company can't deal freely, strong priority. Floating = shifting class (stock, book debts), company trades in ordinary course, weaker priority, **crystallises** on (i) default + lender intervention, (ii) winding-up, or (iii) cessation of business. Memory: *fixed = frozen; floating = free till it crystallises*.
- **Priority sting:** a LATER fixed charge can beat an EARLIER floating charge on the same asset (floating charge licensed ordinary-course dealings) — unless a **negative-pledge clause** was registered giving S.80 deemed notice. "First registered" ≠ "first in priority".
- **Deemed notice — S.80:** runs **forward from date of registration**, only of **registered particulars**. Late registration gives no notice for the gap; understated filing binds only to understated amount.

## Key provisions / sections
| Section | Rule | Limit / detail |
|---|---|---|
| 2(16) | Definition of charge | Interest/lien as security; includes mortgage; covers hypothecation |
| **77** | Duty of company to register | Within **30 days** of creation; Form CHG-1 (non-debenture)/CHG-9 (debenture) |
| 77 post-2 Nov 2018 | Extension ladder | 30 → **60 days** (additional fee) → **120 days** (ad valorem fee). After 120 days: **cannot register**. All measured **from creation** |
| 77 pre-2 Nov 2018 | Old ladder | 30 → **300 days** (additional fee) → then within **6 months of 2 Nov 2018** (ad valorem) |
| 77(3) | Non-registration effect | Charge **not taken into account by liquidator (Act/IBC) or creditor** unless registered + certificate issued |
| 77(4) | Debt survives | Repayment obligation unaffected; lender becomes **unsecured** creditor |
| — | Certificate | CHG-2 = **conclusive evidence** of due registration |
| **78** | Registration by charge-holder | If company fails within 30 days, lender may apply; Registrar gives company **14 days'** notice; lender **recovers fee** from company; still bound by outer 60/120 caps |
| **79** | Modification | S.77 rules apply; **30 days**; CHG-1/CHG-9; unregistered increase void as to excess |
| **80** | Deemed notice | Anyone acquiring property deemed to have notice from date of registration |
| 81 | Registrar's register | Public searchable register |
| **82** | Satisfaction | Intimate within **30 days** (ext. **300 days** on additional fee — survived 2019); Form CHG-4 → Registrar records CHG-5; 14-day notice to charge-holder unless it has signed |
| 83 | Registrar's power | May record satisfaction/release on evidence; inform company in **30 days** |
| 84 | Receiver/Manager | Notice to company + Registrar within **30 days** of appointment AND cessation; Form CHG-6 |
| **85** | Company's own register | At registered office, Form CHG-7; members/creditors inspect **free**, others on fee. NOT S.88 (members) |
| **86** | Punishment | 86(1) penalty on company + every officer in default; 86(2) wilful false/suppressed info → **S.447 fraud** |
| **87** | Rectification by CG (Regional Director) | Extend time/rectify for **satisfaction** & errors if accidental/inadvertent & **not prejudicial**. **NO longer covers late registration of creation/modification** (post-2019) |

**Forms:** CHG-1 register/modify (non-deb), CHG-9 (deb), CHG-2 certificate, CHG-3 modification cert, CHG-4 intimation of satisfaction, CHG-5 memorandum of satisfaction (by Registrar), CHG-6 receiver, CHG-7 company register, CHG-8 CG application/condonation, CHG-10 reasons for delay.

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **"Void against the company" — WRONG.** Void only vs liquidator & creditors; valid vs company (it made the charge).
2. **Debt does NOT vanish** — S.77(4). Lender drops to unsecured, keeps pro-rata claim. "Loses entire amount" overstates.
3. **Old 300-day vs new 30→60→120:** For creation/modification **on or after 2 Nov 2018**, max = **120 days**; 300-day route gone. 300 days survives **only for satisfaction (S.82)** and pre-2 Nov 2018 charges.
4. **S.87 no longer condones late creation** post-2019 — only satisfaction/rectification. Don't cite for late new charge.
5. **Both 60 & 120 windows run from creation (Day 0)**, not from each other. Don't add 60 to end of first extension.
6. **Registration perfects, doesn't create** — charge exists from contract date.
7. **Certificate CHG-2 is conclusive** — can't reopen for technical defects.
8. **Two registers:** S.81 (Registrar, public) vs S.85 (company, at registered office). Don't confuse S.85 (charges) with S.88 (members).
9. **Satisfaction needs charge-holder confirmation or 14-day notice.**
10. **Lie (false/suppressed particulars) = fraud (86(2)/447)**, not mere penalty (86(1) delay). Watch fact patterns hiding a knowing misstatement.
11. **First registered ≠ first in priority** (fixed can beat earlier floating unless negative pledge registered).
12. **Asymmetry:** creation capped at 120 (late creation harms innocent third party); satisfaction survives at 300 (late release only over-states debts — safe direction).
13. **Numerical drill:** Day 0 = creation; classify filing day into 0–30 (normal) / 31–60 (additional fee) / 61–120 (ad valorem) / >120 (cannot register, void).

## One-line recall
- A secret security is a security the law won't honour where it counts — register within 30 days or lose priority in insolvency.
- Post-2018 ladder: **30 → 60 → 120** days (creation); satisfaction **30 → 300** days.
- Unregistered charge = **void vs liquidator/creditors, valid vs company; debt survives as unsecured claim** (77(3)/(4)).
- Deemed notice (S.80) runs **forward from registration**, only of registered particulars; certificate CHG-2 is **conclusive**.
- Duty on both company (S.77) and charge-holder (S.78); lender can self-file + recover fee.
- S.87 rectifies **satisfaction/errors only** now — never revives late creation/modification.
