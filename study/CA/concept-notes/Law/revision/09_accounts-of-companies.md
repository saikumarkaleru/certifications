# Accounts of Companies — CA Inter Revision Note

> Companies Act 2013, Chapter IX, Sections 128–138 (+ NFRA S.132).

## Snapshot
- The chapter is one long war on **information asymmetry** between managers (know the truth) and investors (don't). Mandatory because disclosure is a public good the fox won't self-provide.
- Information supply chain (each section = a quality gate): Transactions → **[128 books]** → **[129/133 statements]** → **[134 Board owns/signs]** → **[143 auditor checks]** → **[136 members]** → **[137 public filing]** → **[132 NFRA polices the checkers]**.
- Design patterns: **proportionality by size** (135/138/132), **named human accountability** (MD/WTD-finance/CFO/CS), **reserve criminality for dishonesty** (128/129 keep jail; 134/135/137 decriminalised to money 2019/20).

## Core concepts
- **Books of account (2(13))** ⊂ **books and papers (2(12))**; output = **financial statements (2(40))** = Balance Sheet + P&L + Cash Flow + Statement of Changes in Equity + Notes.
- **Cash Flow Statement exempt** for OPC, small company, dormant company, specified start-up.
- **"True and fair view"** (not "true and correct") + accounting standards (133) + Schedule III form = anti-manipulation lock.
- **IFC (Expl. to 134(5)(e)):** policies/procedures for orderly & efficient conduct, adherence to policies, safeguarding assets, prevention/detection of frauds & errors, accuracy & completeness of records, timely reliable financial info.

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **7 days** ROC notice if books kept elsewhere (≠ 30-day AOC-4 filing).
2. **8 years** retention (not 7/10); longer if investigation; all years if younger.
3. **CSR trigger = any ONE** (500/1000/5), preceding FY; **2% of avg last-3-years** profit (not current year).
4. **Ongoing → 30 days → Unspent CSR A/c (3 yrs); non-ongoing → 6 months → Schedule VII.** Most-tested CSR point.
5. **Cash Flow exempt** for OPC/small/dormant/start-up.
6. **CFS covers associates & JVs**, not just subsidiaries.
7. **NFRA vs ICAI:** once NFRA initiates on a Rule-3 company, ICAI stands down; NFRA doesn't cover small/private (ICAI retains).
8. **Internal audit ≠ statutory audit:** internal auditor may be an employee/CMA; **private co tested only on turnover ₹200 cr & borrowings >₹100 cr** (not paid-up/deposits).
9. **"Approved by Board" ≠ authenticated/signed** (need 2 directors incl. MD + CEO/CFO/CS); auditor reports AFTER authentication.
10. **CSR now "comply or transfer and explain"** with civil penalty (not "no penalty").
11. **Normal-course-of-business activities are NOT CSR** (nor political contributions, employee benefits, outside-India except sports training, marketing sponsorships).
12. **"True and fair," not "true and correct."**
13. **IFC-effectiveness clause in DRS = listed only**; separate from auditor's 143(3)(i) IFC report.
14. **Director's inspection right (128), not member's.**
15. **NFRA penalties: individual 5×, firm 10×; debar 6 months–10 years.**
16. **137 = per-day civil penalty** (₹10,000 + ₹100/day, capped); late filing decriminalised, unlike false books (128 keeps jail).

## One-line recall
- Capture (128 books, 8 yrs, accrual+double-entry) → present true & fair (129/133) → consolidate group (129(3) CFS + AOC-1) → own & sign (134 DRS + IFC) → give back (135 CSR 2%) → members (136, 21 days) → public file (137 AOC-4, 30 days) → NFRA polices (132).
- CSR triggers **500cr/1000cr/5cr** (any one) → spend **2%** avg-3-yr profit; unspent ongoing → 30 days/3 yrs; non-ongoing → 6 months Schedule VII; penalty 2× or ₹1 cr.
- Internal audit thresholds differ by class; private co only turnover ₹200 cr / borrowings >₹100 cr.
- NFRA overrides ICAI for large/listed once it initiates; penalty up to 5×/10× fees + debar 6 mo–10 yrs.
- Jail survives for dishonesty (128/129); procedural defaults (134/135/137) decriminalised to money.
