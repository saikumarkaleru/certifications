# Management & Administration — CA Inter Revision Note

> Companies Act 2013, Chapter VII, Sections 88–122 + Section 12 (registered office) + Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014.

## Snapshot
- Three pillars: **Visibility** (office, register of members, SBO, annual return), **Fairness of decision-making** (notice, explanatory statement, quorum, proxy, poll/e-voting/postal ballot), **Proof** (filed resolutions, minutes).
- Threshold pricing by disruption: **10%** to force the Board's hand; **1% or ₹5 lakh** to put an item on the calendar; **95%** to waive protection (short notice); **75%** to change the bargain (special resolution).
- Audiences: outsiders get filed returns/resolutions; members get participation & inspection; company gets internal certainty.

## Core concepts
- **Registered office (S.12):** legal home to be found & served. Publication: name + address outside office (English + local language), name on seal, CIN + contact on all letters/billheads; OPC prints "(One Person Company)". Four-tier change ladder by disruption.
- **Beneficial interest (S.89) vs SBO (S.90):** S.89 = simple single-layer nominee, **no threshold** (even 1 share). S.90 = ultimate **individual** (natural person) behind layers, **≥10%** OR significant influence/control. Both can bite at once.
- **AGM (S.96):** yearly reckoning; ordinary business = accounts, dividend, retiring directors, auditor appointment (closed list of 4). EGM has NO ordinary business.
- **Show of hands vs poll:** show of hands = one member one vote (heads); poll = counted by shareholding. Validly demanded poll overrides; chairman can't refuse.
- **Proxy vs corporate representative:** proxy (S.105) can't vote on show of hands, can't (usually) speak, **NOT counted for quorum**. Corporate rep (S.113) / President-Governor rep (S.112) = member "in person": vote on show of hands & poll, **count for quorum**, may speak.

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **"Clear days"** excludes both day of sending and day of meeting + ~2 days postal.
2. **Proxy ≠ corporate representative** — proxy no show-of-hands vote, not counted for quorum; corporate rep does both.
3. **Special resolution = 75% of votes CAST** (not total membership) + intention stated in notice. Both limbs.
4. **First AGM = 9 months, NOT extendable;** subsequent = 6 months; gap ≤ 15 months; extension up to 3 months (later AGMs only).
5. **Quorum 5/15/30** by member count (public); private always 2. Proxies don't count; corporate/State reps do.
6. **Requisition threshold = 1/10th** (also poll voting-power limb & S.111). **₹5,00,000** limb applies only to **poll** and **special notice**, not EGM requisition.
7. **Postal ballot** not for ordinary business or right-to-be-heard items.
8. **Proxy deposit ≤48 hrs before** is a **maximum** — a proxy lodged 24 hrs before is valid; article demanding >48 hrs advance is void.
9. **S.102(4) = disgorgement (benefit returned) + separate penalty** (₹50,000 or 5× benefit). Don't merge.
10. **Board minutes NOT open to members** (S.119 = GM minutes only).
11. **OPC (S.122):** no AGM, S.100–111 excluded, BUT still keeps registers, files annual return, keeps minutes.
12. **Accidental omission of notice (101(4))** doesn't void; **deliberate** omission does.
13. **Special notice ≠ special resolution** — removal of director (S.169) needs special notice but passes by **ordinary resolution**.
14. **Minutes = "evidence until contrary proved,"** not conclusive proof.
15. **SBO is always an individual** — look through holding companies; influence/control limb catches someone below 10%.
16. **S.97 (AGM not held, member applies) vs S.98 (any other meeting impracticable, also Tribunal suo motu).**
17. **MGT-14 not "special resolutions only"** — S.179(3) Board resolutions also filed within 30 days.

## One-line recall
- A company acts only through people → every collective act must be **visible, fair, and recorded** so power traces to a legitimate source.
- Thresholds priced by disruption: **10% force / 1% or ₹5 lakh calendar / 95% waive / 75% change**.
- Notice **21 clear days**; special resolution = **≥3× against (75% of votes cast) + stated in notice**.
- Proxy can't vote on show of hands or count for quorum; corporate/State representative can.
- AGM: 1st in **9 months (not extendable)**, later **6 months**, gap **≤15 months**.
- Minutes are **evidence until the contrary is proved**; Board minutes closed to members.
