# Audit & Auditors — CA Inter Revision Note

> Companies Act 2013, Chapter X, Sections 139–148 + Companies (Audit & Auditors) Rules, 2014.

## Snapshot
- Solves the **agency gap**: owners fund, managers spend and write their own report card. Auditor = independent expert appointed **by owners**, reporting **to owners**, whom management **cannot silence**.
- **"True and fair"**, not "true and correct" — auditor gives an **opinion, not a guarantee**; watchdog, not bloodhound (*Kingston Cotton Mill*).
- Section spine: **139 hire · 140 fire · 141 who's fit · 142 pay · 143 do the job · 144 don't moonlight · 145 sign · 146 attend · 147 punish · 148 cost.**
- Four capture routes blocked: **Selection** (139/141) · **Money** (142/141(3)/144) · **Tenure/familiarity** (139(2)) · **Termination** (140).
- Five independence threats: self-interest · self-review · advocacy · familiarity · intimidation.

## Core concepts
- **Eligibility (141(1)/(2)):** only a **CA** (or firm/LLP where majority of partners practising in India are CAs); only CA partner signs.
- **Rotation (139(2)):** individual **1×5 yrs**; firm **2×5 yrs**; then **5-yr cooling-off**; no common-partner/same-network swap; past service counted (look-back). Applies to listed + unlisted public (paid-up ≥ ₹10 cr) + private (paid-up ≥ ₹50 cr) + any co with public borrowings/deposits ≥ ₹50 cr. **OPC & small companies exempt.**
- **Four exits:** removed before term (140(1): Board res → CG/ADT-2 → special res); resigns (140(2): ADT-3, 30 days, reasons); not re-appointed at term end (140(4): special notice + representation + ordinary res); ejected for fraud (140(5): Tribunal + 5-yr debar + S.447).
- **Fraud reporting (143(12)):** on **reason to believe** fraud **against company by officers/employees**; ≥ ₹1 cr → CG (ADT-4, Board 45-day reply + 15 days); < ₹1 cr → Board/AC in 2 days + Board's Report.

## Key provisions / sections
| Section | Rule | Limit / detail |
|---|---|---|
| 139(6) | First auditor (non-Govt) | **Board within 30 days** of registration; if fails, members at EGM within **90 days** (from incorporation); holds till 1st AGM |
| 139(7) | First auditor (Govt) | **C&AG within 60 days**; then Board 30 days; then members 60 days |
| 139(1) | Subsequent (non-Govt) | Members at AGM, **5-year block** (till 6th AGM); consent + eligibility certificate; company files **ADT-1 within 15 days**. **Ratification abolished** (w.e.f. 7 May 2018) |
| 139(5) | Subsequent (Govt) | **C&AG within 180 days** of FY start, every year; no 5-yr block, no rotation |
| 139(8) | Casual vacancy | Resignation → Board 30 days + **members approve within 3 months**; other cause (death/disqualification) → Board 30 days; Govt → C&AG 30 days |
| 139(2) | Rotation | 1×5 (individual), 2×5 (firm), cool off 5 yrs; OPC/small exempt |
| 140(1) | Removal before term | Board resolution → **CG (ADT-2) within 30 days** → **special resolution** within 60 days of CG approval → hearing. Sequence fixed |
| 140(2)/(3) | Resignation | **Auditor** files **ADT-3 within 30 days** with reasons; penalty ₹50,000 or remuneration (lower) + ₹500/day |
| 140(4) | Non-reappointment | Special notice → copy to retiring auditor → written representation circulated/read out → **ordinary resolution** (NO CG, NO special res); Tribunal may bar defamatory representation |
| 140(5) | Tribunal removal for fraud | NCLT directs change; **5-yr debar** + S.447; interim CG appointment |
| 141(3) | Disqualifications | Body corporate (except LLP); officer/employee; partner/employee of officer; holds security/interest (relative cushion **₹1 lakh face value**, 60-day cure); indebted **> ₹5 lakh**; guarantee **> ₹1 lakh**; business relationship; relative is director/KMP; full-time employed elsewhere or auditor of **> 20 companies**; convicted of fraud (< 10 yrs); renders S.144 services |
| 141(4) | Vacation | Disqualification after appointment → vacate immediately (casual vacancy) |
| 142 | Remuneration | General meeting (or as it determines); **first auditor by Board**; excludes fees for other services |
| 143(1) | Powers | **Access at all times** to books/vouchers incl. subsidiaries/associates; require info & explanation |
| 143(2)/(3) | Report | To **members**; true & fair; proper books; agreement with books; AS compliance; director disqualification (164(2)); IFC adequacy; Rule 11 disclosures |
| 143(8) | Branch audit | Company's auditor or branch auditor; foreign branch by locally-qualified person; branch auditor reports to principal auditor (who retains judgement) |
| 143(12) | Fraud reporting | ≥ ₹1 cr → CG via ADT-4 (45+15 days); < ₹1 cr → Board/AC in 2 days + Board's Report; 143(15) penalty listed ₹5 lakh / others ₹1 lakh; good-faith protection |
| 144 | Prohibited services | Directly/indirectly to co/holding/subsidiary: **A-I-D-A-I-I-O-M** = Accounting, Internal audit, Design of FIS, Actuarial, Investment advisory, Investment banking, Outsourced financial, Management. Tax rep/statutory certification allowed |
| 145 | Signing | Only appointed auditor/authorised partner signs; qualifications read in GM |
| 146 | Attend meetings | Right to notice + attend GM + be heard |
| 147 | Liability | Co: fine ₹25,000–₹5,00,000, officers ₹10,000–₹1,00,000. Auditor: ₹25,000–₹5,00,000 or 4× remuneration (lower); wilful deceit → **jail up to 1 yr + ₹50,000–₹25,00,000 (or 8× remuneration)**; refund fee + damages (147(3)); firm + connived partner joint & several |
| 148 | Cost audit | Cost records for prescribed industries; **Cost Accountant** (not CA); **appointed by Board** (remuneration ratified by members); statutory auditor cannot be cost auditor; S.143 applies; forms **CRA-1** (records) / **CRA-2** (appoint) / **CRA-3** (report to Board) / **CRA-4** (file with CG, 30 days) |
| 132 | NFRA | Disciplines auditors of large/listed; debar **6 months–10 years**; ousts ICAI jurisdiction once it initiates |

**Forms:** ADT-1 (company files appointment, 15 days) · ADT-2 (removal to CG) · ADT-3 (auditor files resignation, 30 days) · ADT-4 (fraud ≥ ₹1 cr to CG).

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **Ratification abolished** (7 May 2018) — don't require annual ratification.
2. **Face value, not market value** for the ₹1 lakh relative-securities limit.
3. **Auditor/partner have NO ₹1 lakh cushion** — any security disqualifies; cushion is for a **relative** only.
4. **Direction of debt:** indebtedness (> ₹5 lakh) = auditor **owes** company (company owing auditor ≠ this disqualification).
5. **Individual 1×5, firm 2×5, cool off 5**; past service counts; no common-partner/network swap.
6. **Who appoints:** financial auditor → members; first auditor → Board (30 days); Govt → C&AG; cost auditor → Board. Casual vacancy by resignation needs members' approval (3 months); other cause → Board alone.
7. **Removal (140(1): CG + special res)** vs **non-reappointment (140(4): special notice + ordinary res, no CG)** — most-swapped point.
8. **Removal sequence:** Board res → CG (ADT-2) → special res (never before CG approval).
9. **Fraud thresholds:** **₹1 cr** line; ≥ → ADT-4 to CG (45+15); < → Board/AC in 2 days + Board's Report.
10. **143(12) triggers on fraud "against the company by officers/employees"**, on "reason to believe" (not proof).
11. **Body corporate can't audit — except LLP.**
12. **S.144 "directly or indirectly"** catches relatives/network/affiliate; but **tax rep/statutory certification allowed**.
13. **Govt company = ≥ 51%** holding; 49% is not (C&AG doesn't appoint).
14. **Cost auditor = Cost Accountant, Board-appointed**; statutory auditor cannot double as cost auditor.
15. **20-company ceiling** excludes OPC/dormant/small/private (paid-up < ₹100 cr); 20 per partner for a firm.
16. **Disclaimer = lack of evidence; adverse = material & pervasive error** — don't swap.
17. **Access "at all times"** — no permission needed.
18. **Company files ADT-1; auditor files ADT-3** — don't mix.
19. **NFRA ousts ICAI** for matters within its remit.

## One-line recall
- Auditor appointed by/reports to owners; competent (CA, 141) + independent (141(3)/144/139(2) rotation) + un-fireable (140).
- Timeline: first auditor **30/90 days**; subsequent **5-yr block (till 6th AGM)**; Govt C&AG **60/180 days**; ADT-1 in **15 days**.
- Money hook **"1-5-1"**: relative securities ₹1 lakh (face), indebtedness ₹5 lakh, guarantee ₹1 lakh; ceiling **20** companies.
- Four exits: removed (140(1) CG+SR), resigns (140(2) ADT-3), not re-appointed (140(4) special notice+ordinary res), fraud ejection (140(5) Tribunal+5-yr bar).
- Fraud report ≥ ₹1 cr → CG (ADT-4, 45+15 days); < ₹1 cr → Board/AC 2 days + report.
- S.144 ban = **A-I-D-A-I-I-O-M**; cost audit = Cost Accountant, Board-appointed, CRA-1/2/3/4.
