# The Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008

## Snapshot
LLP = a **hybrid**: outside it behaves like a **company** (body corporate, separate legal entity, perpetual succession, limited liability); inside it behaves like a **partnership** (private LLP Agreement, no compulsory board/AGM). Mental model: "a partnership wearing a company's skin." In force largely from 31 March 2009 (LLP Rules 2009). Built for professionals first. **An LLP is a body corporate but NOT a company** (every company is a body corporate; not every body corporate is a company).

## Core concepts
- **Shield:** each partner's liability limited to agreed contribution; **one partner is NOT liable for another partner's wrongful act** (deletes the 1932 mutual-agency trap). "Your own sin, your own skin."
- **Partner is agent of the LLP, not of other partners** (S.26) — makes S.28 no-cross-liability coherent.
- **First Schedule** fills gaps **matter-by-matter** (only where the LLP Agreement is silent on that specific point).
- **Designated Partners** = the accountable human interface to the Registrar (price of the shield).
- Five doors through the shield: own wrongful act (S.28) · fraud with intent (S.30) · solo beyond 6 months (S.6) · personal guarantee (outside the Act) · unpaid agreed contribution (S.32-33).

## Key provisions / sections
| Section | Rule |
|---|---|
| S.2 | Definitions. *Business* includes profession. *Body corporate* excludes co-operative society & corporation sole. Foreign LLP = incorporated outside India **+ establishes place of business in India**. |
| S.3 | LLP is a **body corporate**, separate legal entity, **perpetual succession**. |
| S.4 | **Indian Partnership Act 1932 does NOT apply** to an LLP. |
| S.5 | Partner = any individual or body corporate. Disqualified individual: unsound mind (finding in force), **undischarged** insolvent, or pending insolvency application. **No minor** (unlike 1932 benefits route). |
| S.6 | **Min 2 partners; NO maximum.** Below 2 for **> 6 months** + sole partner **knows** + business carried on → sole partner **personally liable (jointly with LLP)** for obligations of that excess period only. |
| S.7 | **Min 2 Designated Partners, both individuals, ≥ 1 resident in India** (resident = stayed **≥ 120 days** in FY; amended down from 182 — *confirm*). Each needs **DPIN** + prior consent. If <2 DPs, **every partner deemed a DP**. Body corporate can be partner but not DP (acts via nominee individual). |
| S.11 | Incorporation document filed with Registrar; **"with a view to profit"** (no charitable LLP); two-signature statement of compliance (professional advocate/CA/CS/CMA **+** a subscriber). |
| S.12 | Registrar registers within **14 days**; **Certificate of Incorporation = conclusive evidence.** |
| S.13 | Registered office; service of documents. |
| S.14 | Powers: sue/be sued, own & dispose property; **common seal OPTIONAL.** |
| S.15-17 | Name must end with **"LLP"/"Limited Liability Partnership"**; not undesirable/identical; reservation & CG-directed change. |
| S.21 | Name, registered-office address & registration number on invoices/correspondence. |
| S.22-23 | LLP Agreement governs mutual rights; filed in **Form 3**; **First Schedule** fills silence matter-by-matter. |
| S.24 | Cessation: per agreement, or **30 days' written notice**, or death/dissolution/unsound mind/insolvency. Ex-partner's interest → **financial claim** (contribution + accumulated profits); **still a "partner" to third parties until notice to them OR to Registrar.** |
| S.25 | File change of partners within **30 days** (Form 4). |
| S.26 | Partner is agent of **LLP**, not of other partners. |
| S.27 | LLP liabilities met from **LLP property**. "Not bound" is a **two-limb cumulative test**: (a) partner had no authority AND (b) outsider knew of no authority or didn't believe him a partner. |
| S.28 | Partner not personally liable **solely by reason of being a partner**; not liable for another's wrong; **liable for own** wrong. |
| S.30 | **Fraud with INTENT to defraud → UNLIMITED liability** for LLP + participating partners + penalties. Innocent partner shielded. Mere negligence/recklessness ≠ fraud. |
| S.31 | Whistle-blower protection. |
| S.32-33 | Contribution = tangible/intangible incl. money, property, **services (sweat equity)**. Agreed contribution enforceable; a **creditor who relied on the original figure** can enforce it despite later reduction. No minimum capital (*confirm*). |
| S.34 | Books; **Statement of Account & Solvency (Form 8) within 6 months** of FY-end; audit needed only if **turnover > ₹40 lakh OR contribution > ₹25 lakh** (disjunctive; *confirm*). FY ends 31 March. |
| S.35 | **Annual Return (Form 11) within 60 days** of FY close. |
| S.55-57 | Conversion **INTO** LLP: Firm (S.55/Sch 2), Private company (S.56/Sch 3), **Unlisted** public company (S.57/Sch 4). All partners/shareholders (and no one else) become partners; no subsisting charge; assets & liabilities **vest automatically**; old entity deemed dissolved; conversion disclosed on correspondence (commonly 60 days). Old debts follow the assets. |
| S.58-59 | Registrar's certificate conclusive; Foreign LLPs. |
| S.60-62 | Compromise/arrangement/amalgamation (Tribunal). |
| S.63-65 | Winding up voluntary or by **Tribunal (NCLT)**. Tribunal grounds incl.: below 2 partners **> 6 months**; inability to pay debts; acts against sovereignty; **default in filing S.34/35 for 5 consecutive FYs**; just & equitable. |

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. Partnership Act 1932 does **NOT** govern LLPs (S.4).
2. Min **2** partners, **no max**; min **2 DPs**, both individuals, **≥1 resident** (**120 days**, not 182 — confirm).
3. Below 2 partners = **6-month grace**, then only the **knowing sole partner** is liable (also a winding-up ground after 6 months).
4. A partner is **NOT** liable for a co-partner's wrong (S.28) — but **is** liable for his **own** wrong; **fraud (intent)** → unlimited (S.30).
5. Silence → First Schedule: **equal profit share, NO remuneration**, unanimous consent for new partner/change of business nature; ordinary matters by majority (one vote each).
6. **Common seal OPTIONAL** (S.14). Certificate of Incorporation **conclusive** (S.12).
7. Two filings, two deadlines: **Annual Return Form 11 = 60 days**; **Statement of Account & Solvency Form 8 = 6 months** ("structure quick, accounts need closing"). Don't swap.
8. Body corporate can be **partner** but not **DP** (nominee acts).
9. Audit test is **OR** (turnover > ₹40 lakh **or** contribution > ₹25 lakh), not AND.
10. **No LLP → company** conversion route under this Act (only firm/company → LLP).
11. LLP cannot be for **charitable/not-for-profit** object (needs "view to profit"); only **unlisted** public company can convert.
12. Liability capped at **contribution, not zero** — unpaid agreed contribution recoverable; personal guarantee enforceable outside the shield.
13. Cessation binds outsiders only after **notice to them or the Registrar**; minor cannot be an LLP partner.

## One-line recall
- "A partnership wearing a company's skin" — freedom inside (S.23), shield outside (S.3, S.28).
- "Your own sin, your own skin" — own wrong reaches you; fraud (intent) kills the shield entirely (S.30).
- "Silence speaks the First Schedule" — equal shares, no salary, unanimous new partners.
- "2 DPs, 1 in India, both individuals" (S.7); deemed-DP safety net if they lapse.
- Five doors through the shield: own wrong · fraud · solo > 6 months · guarantee · unpaid contribution.
- Form 11 = 60 days; Form 8 = 6 months; convert only INTO an LLP.
