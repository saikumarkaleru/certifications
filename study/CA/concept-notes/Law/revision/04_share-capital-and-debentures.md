# Share Capital & Debentures — CA Inter Revision Note

> Companies Act 2013, Chapter IV, Sections 43–72 + Companies (Share Capital & Debentures) Rules, 2014.

## Snapshot
- Villain of the chapter = **abuse of control over capital**. Two lamps govern all sections:
  - **Capital Maintenance** (protect creditors' buffer): 52, 53, 55, 63, 66, 68/69, 71 → "keep buffer honest / don't let it leak."
  - **Fair Dealing / Equality** (protect members inter se): 47, 48, 54, 62 → "no group enriched at another's expense."
- Master sentence: every rule either **(a) makes nominal = real at issue** (52/53/54/62) or **(b) stops real falling below nominal later** (55/63/66/68/71).
- Capital ladder (all ceilings key off **paid-up**, NOT authorised): Authorised → Issued → Subscribed → Called-up → Paid-up.
- Private company may relax **member-protective** rules (43, 47, 62 periods, ESOP) but **NEVER creditor-protective** rules (53, 66, 68, 71).

## Core concepts
- **Preference share defaults if silent:** Cumulative · Non-participating · Non-convertible · Redeemable. Irredeemable preference = **prohibited** (55).
- **Buffer-replacement test:** nothing replaces it → Tribunal (66); CRR/fresh issue replaces it → no Tribunal (55, 68/69); reserve moves INTO capital → buffer grows, light conditions (63); merely reshuffled → ordinary resolution (61).
- **Diminution (61) vs Reduction (66):** cancel shares *never taken up* = diminution (ordinary resolution, no Tribunal); cancel *lost/surplus* capital or pay capital back = reduction (special res + Tribunal + creditor hearing).
- **Share vs Debenture:** share = owner/dividend/votes/paid last/no discount; debenture = creditor/interest (even in loss)/no votes (71(2))/paid before members/**may be issued at discount**.

## Key provisions / sections
| Section | Rule | Limit / detail |
|---|---|---|
| 43 | Kinds of capital | Equity (voting or DVR) + Preference (priority dividend + repayment). Private co may override 43 & 47 via MOA/AOA |
| 46 | Share certificate | **Prima facie** (not conclusive) evidence; register is primary. Duplicate register + heavy penalty for fraud |
| 47 | Voting rights | 1 share 1 vote; preference votes only on matters affecting them, BUT full vote on **every** resolution if dividend unpaid **≥ 2 years**; always vote on winding-up / repayment of pref capital |
| 48 | Variation of class rights | Written consent of **≥ 3/4** of class OR special resolution at class meeting; if prejudices another class → that class consents too; dissenting **≥ 10%** of class (who didn't consent) → Tribunal within **21 days** |
| 52 | Securities premium | To Securities Premium A/c; uses ONLY: bonus, write-off preliminary expenses, write-off issue expenses/commission, premium on redemption of pref/debentures, buy-back. **52(3)** AS-compliant cos: only bonus + issue expenses + redemption premium. **No dividend.** Arises even on non-cash issue |
| 53 | Discount prohibited | Issue below **face value = VOID** (measured vs face, not market); refund + interest + penalty. Carve-out 53(2A): discount to creditors on debt→equity under statutory scheme |
| 54 | Sweat equity | To directors/employees for know-how/IP/value. **Special resolution** (valid 12 months); caps: annual **15% of paid-up equity OR ₹5 cr, whichever higher**; overall **≤ 25%** at any time (start-up **50%**); lock-in ~3 yrs; Register SH-3. **1-year wait OMITTED w.e.f. 7 May 2018** |
| 55 | Redemption of preference | Must be redeemable within **20 years** (infra: beyond, phased). Redeem only from (a) profits available for dividend, or (b) **proceeds of fresh issue for purpose** (nominal only counts). **Only fully-paid** may be redeemed. Profit-funded portion → **CRR** (equal nominal). 55(3): can't redeem → with **≥ 3/4** pref consent + **Tribunal**, issue fresh pref |
| 47/DVR | DVR shares | Authorised by articles + ordinary resolution; profit track / no default; cap on % of post-issue paid-up equity (verify); no conversion of existing equity to/from DVR |
| 56 | Transfer & transmission | Transfer = voluntary, **SH-4** stamped + executed, deliver within **60 days**. Transmission = by law (death/insolvency), only proof. Certificates: **2 months** (share allotment) / **6 months** (debenture allotment) / **1 month** (transfer). Refusal communicated in 30 days. Forged transfer = void ab initio |
| 58/59 | Refusal / rectification | Private co appeal: 30 days (notice) / 60 days (lodgment); public co: 60/90 days. Public co shares freely transferable |
| 61 | Alteration of capital | If authorised by articles, **ordinary resolution**: increase authorised / consolidate-divide / convert to stock / sub-divide (paid-up proportion constant) / cancel unissued (**diminution**). Consolidation changing voting % → **Tribunal**. No Tribunal otherwise |
| 62 | Further issue | (a) **Rights** to existing holders pro-rata, offer **15–30 days**, notice **≥ 3 days** before, right of renunciation, unsubscribed disposed "not disadvantageously"; (b) **ESOP** special resolution (ordinary for private co); (c) **Preferential allotment** to others → special resolution + **registered valuer** price. 62(3) loan-conversion approved by special res before loan → no rights offer |
| 63 | Bonus shares | Free, from free reserves / securities premium / CRR. **NOT** from revaluation reserve; authorised by articles + Board + members; no default on debt-securities/statutory dues; partly-paid made fully paid first; **cannot be withdrawn** once announced; **not in lieu of dividend** (63(3)). Must fit within authorised ceiling |
| 64 | Notice of alteration | File **SH-7 within 30 days** |
| 66 | Reduction of capital | Special resolution **+ Tribunal (NCLT) + creditor hearing**. Forms: extinguish unpaid liability / cancel lost capital / pay off surplus. Barred if in arrears on deposits |
| 68/69/70 | Buy-back | Sources: free reserves / securities premium / fresh issue proceeds. Caps: **value ≤ 25% (paid-up capital + free reserves)**; **quantity ≤ 25% paid-up equity/yr**; post-buy-back **debt : equity ≤ 2:1**. Board (≤10%) or special res (≤25%). Fully-paid only; complete in **1 yr**; extinguish in **7 days**; no same-kind re-issue **6 months**; gap between buy-backs **1 yr**; nominal → **CRR (69)**. Barred if in default (curable + 3 yrs) |
| 71 | Debentures | No voting (71(2)); convertible needs special res before issue; **Debenture Trustee** (independent) mandatory if offered to public or **> 500 persons**, appointed BEFORE issue; **DRR** out of profits (unlisted non-NBFC/HFC; listed + NBFC/HFC exempt since 2019); **DRI** ~15% of next-year maturities by **30 April** |
| 72 | Nomination | Nominee gets shares on death; **overrides will** for vesting |

**Forms:** SH-3 (sweat equity register) · SH-4 (transfer) · SH-7 (alteration) · PAS-3 (return of allotment).

## Computations (formulas — take the tightest cap)
| Computation | Formula | Base | Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buy-back value cap | 25% × (paid-up capital + free reserves) | paid-up | using authorised |
| Buy-back quantity cap | 25% × paid-up equity | paid-up equity alone | mixing bases |
| Buy-back debt test | post debt ÷ owned funds ≤ 2:1 | — | often the binding cap |
| Sweat equity annual | higher of 15% × paid-up equity OR ₹5 cr | paid-up equity | taking the lower |
| Sweat equity overall | 25% (start-up 50%) | paid-up equity, cumulative | ignoring overall cap |
| CRR on redemption | nominal redeemed **out of profits** | face only | counting fresh-issue premium |
| Value of one right | ex-rights (TERP) − issue price | TERP | using cum-rights price |
| TERP | [(N × cum-price) + issue price] ÷ (N+1) | N = old shares per new | wrong ratio |
| Bonus feasibility | reserves ≥ shares × face AND post-bonus paid-up ≤ authorised | exclude revaluation | missing authorised ceiling |

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **Alteration (61) vs Reduction (66):** cancel unissued = diminution (ordinary, no Tribunal); real reduction needs Tribunal + creditors. Ask "did money go back / buffer shrink?"
2. **Redemption (55) ≠ reduction** — no Tribunal because CRR/fresh issue replaces instantly; only fully-paid pref redeemable.
3. **Discount (53) vs sweat equity (54):** sweat equity is lawful (non-cash value in), not "void." Discount measured vs **face value**, not market — below-market-above-face is legal.
4. **Securities premium ≠ free profit** — no dividend; closed list (narrower under 52(3)); arises even on non-cash issue; can't be released without Sec 66.
5. **S.47 trigger = 2 years** unpaid dividend (not first miss; "18 months" ≠ triggered); once armed, votes on EVERY resolution.
6. **Cumulative is default** if silent — skipped dividend still payable.
7. **Rights window 15–30 days** (min 15); must mention renunciation; lapse (not renunciation) forfeits value.
8. **S.48:** pass = **3/4**; challenge = **≥10%** of class within **21 days** (measured on issued class shares, non-consenters only). Don't confuse the two thresholds.
9. **Preference redeemable within 20 years** — no perpetual/irredeemable preference (55). But **debentures CAN be perpetual** (debt outside buffer).
10. **Debentures: no voting (71(2))**, votes only after conversion; **may be issued at discount** (unlike shares); interest payable **even in loss year** (contractual).
11. **Bonus:** not from revaluation reserve; can't withdraw once announced; not in lieu of dividend; must fit authorised ceiling (may need Sec 61 increase first).
12. **Buy-back:** compute ALL 3 ceilings + 2:1 debt ratio, take **minimum** (examiner rigs so debt ratio binds); fully-paid; extinguish 7 days; CRR; 1-yr gap.
13. **Authorised vs paid-up decoy:** all caps (buy-back 25%, sweat 15%/25%, DVR) use **paid-up equity**, never authorised. Only bonus tests authorised ceiling.
14. **"Proceeds of fresh issue" for redemption = nominal only** — premium does NOT reduce CRR; fresh issue must be *for the purpose*.
15. **Transfer (SH-4, voluntary) vs transmission (proof, by law).** LR can transfer without becoming member. Nomination overrides will for vesting (72).
16. **Public co shares freely transferable (58); private co restricts** — apply right refusal standard to right type.
17. **Trustee: > 500 persons/public**, appointed before issue, independent (no shareholding/indebtedness). DRR exempt for listed + NBFC/HFC since 2019.
18. **DRR (reserve) + DRI (actual deposit by 30 April) are additive, not alternatives.**
19. **Private placement (42): ≤ 200 persons per kind per FY** (ex-QIB/ESOP); banking channel only; funds frozen till PAS-3; breach = deemed public offer.

## One-line recall
- Two lamps: **Capital Maintenance** (52/53/55/63/66/68/71) + **Fair Dealing** (47/48/54/62). Ask "capital leaking out, or one group cheating another?"
- Buffer test: unreplaced → Tribunal (66); replaced by CRR/fresh issue → no Tribunal (55/68); reshuffle → ordinary res (61).
- Preference: cumulative/redeemable defaults; full vote after **2 yrs** unpaid dividend; redeemable **≤ 20 yrs**; profit-redemption → CRR.
- Rights **15–30 days** + renunciation; preferential allotment needs **valuer + special res**; bonus not from revaluation, not withdrawable, not in lieu of dividend.
- Debenture = creditor: no votes, discount allowed, interest even in loss; trustee if **> 500**; DRR + DRI.
- All numerical caps use **paid-up equity** (not authorised); compute every cap, answer with the **minimum**.
