# Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (Basics)

## Snapshot
FEMA replaced **FERA, 1973** after the 1991 liberalisation flipped the problem from **scarcity/control** to **adequacy/facilitation**. FERA (81 sections, criminal, "prohibited unless permitted," accused presumed guilty) → **FEMA (49 sections, 7 chapters, civil, "permitted unless restricted," presumption of innocence).** In force **1 June 2000**; extends to **whole of India + branches/offices/agencies outside India owned or controlled by a resident** (extra-territorial by **control**, not soil). Object (Preamble): **facilitate external trade & payments; orderly development/maintenance of the forex market** (a purposive interpretive aid — ambiguity read towards facilitation). Twin levers: **who is the person** (residence decides *whether* FEMA bites) → **what is the transaction** (current/capital decides *how*).

## Core concepts
- **Accountant's test:** flow hits **P&L (income/expense) = current (default FREE)**; flow hits **Balance Sheet (creates/removes cross-border asset or liability) = capital (default REGULATED)**.
- **Mirror rule:** Current (S.5) = *free unless restricted* by **Central Government**; Capital (S.6) = *restricted unless permitted* by **RBI**.
- Breach = **civil contravention** → penalty → compounding; jail (civil imprisonment) only for **non-payment**.
- Residence is **binary** (2(v) or 2(w)); no "partly resident." **Residence, not citizenship.**

## Key provisions / sections
| Provision | Rule |
|---|---|
| **2(v) Resident in India** | Individual **> 182 days in *preceding* FY**, BUT **purpose/intention overrides**: leaves for employment/business/uncertain-period abroad → non-resident immediately; comes to India to settle → resident from arrival (comes temporarily → not resident). Day-count is for **individuals only**. Also includes: **body incorporated in India (auto)**; India-branch of a foreigner (by control); **abroad-branch of a resident** (by control). |
| **2(w) Resident outside India** | Anyone "not resident in India." |
| **2(u) Person** | Individual, HUF, company, firm, AOP/BOI, artificial juridical person, and any agency/office/branch of such person. |
| **2(n) Foreign exchange** | Foreign currency; incl. deposits/credits/balances payable in FC; instruments in rupees but **payable in FC**; instruments drawn abroad but payable in rupees. Follow **currency of ultimate payment**, not the face. |
| **2(o) Foreign security** | FC-denominated securities + rupee securities where redemption/interest payable in FC. |
| **2(h) Currency** | Notes, coins, postal/money orders, cheques, drafts, TCs, LCs, bills, promissory notes, credit cards + others RBI notifies. |
| **2(j) Current account txn** | "**A transaction other than a capital account transaction**" + (illustrative) trade/services/**short-term** banking & credit; **interest on loans & net investment income**; **living expenses of family abroad**; **foreign travel/education/medical** of family. Capital-definition is the master key. |
| **2(e) Capital account txn** | **Alters assets/liabilities (incl. contingent) outside India of residents, or in India of non-residents.** Symmetric (inbound + outbound). Includes S.6(3) classes. |
| **2(c) Authorised Person** | Authorised dealer / money changer / off-shore banking unit / other, authorised by **RBI under S.10(1)**. Genus = AP; species = dealer/money changer/OBU. |
| **S.3** General prohibition | No dealing/transfer of forex to non-AP; no payment to/for a non-resident **"in any manner"**; no receipt on behalf of a non-resident (**catches hawala**) — **except with RBI/AP permission**. |
| **S.5** Current account | Any person may sell/draw forex for a current transaction. **Proviso:** Central Govt may, in **public interest** & **consulting RBI**, impose **reasonable restrictions** (Current Account Rules 2000). |
| **S.6** Capital account | 6(1) forex for capital txn **only to the extent permitted**; **6(2)** RBI (with CG) specifies permissible **classes & limits** (post-2015: CG rules for **debt** instruments, RBI for **non-debt**); **6(3) omitted by Finance Act 2015**; **6(4)** resident may hold/transfer foreign assets **acquired when non-resident or inherited from a non-resident**; **6(5)** symmetric for non-resident's Indian assets. |
| **S.7** Export | **Declare full export value** (or expected arm's-length value) + take **reasonable steps to realise & repatriate** (anti-under-invoicing). |
| **S.8** Repatriation | Forex due/accrued to a resident → **reasonable steps** to realise & repatriate. |
| **S.9** Exemptions | Small holdings, permitted FC accounts, legacy/gift/inherited forex, earnings from services abroad — keeps the Act proportionate. |
| **S.10** Authorised Persons | RBI **licenses**, conditions, **revokes** (after hearing). AP has **positive due-diligence duty**: require declaration, satisfy itself, **refuse & report** suspected contravention. |
| **S.11** | RBI directs/inspects APs; penalties. |
| **S.13** Penalties | **Up to 3× the sum** (quantifiable) or **up to ₹2,00,000** (non-quantifiable) + **up to ₹5,000/day** continuing. A **ceiling** ("up to"), authority may levy less. (13(1A)+ 2015: foreign-asset threshold breaches — higher penalty/confiscation/imprisonment; *confirm*.) |
| **S.14** Civil imprisonment | If **penalty unpaid within 90 days** → civil imprisonment — for **non-payment, NOT the breach**; duration scales with unpaid amount (slab around ₹1 crore; *verify*). |
| **S.15** Compounding | Contravention **may be compounded** on application to RBI/ED; **within 180 days**; then no further proceedings. Authority's **discretion**; serious/PMLA-linked cases may be refused. |
| **S.16** Adjudicating Authority | CG officer **adjudicates** (not "prosecutes") after hearing. |
| **S.47** | RBI frames Regulations. |
| Appeals | Adjudicating Authority → **Special Director (Appeals)** → **Appellate Tribunal** → **High Court (question of law only, within 60 days**, +60 extendable). Tribunal may require **pre-deposit**. **Directorate of Enforcement** runs FEMA (civil) + PMLA (criminal). |

**Current Account Rules 2000 schedules:** **I = prohibited** (lottery winnings/tickets, racing income, dividend-balancing); **II = prior Central Govt approval**; **III = RBI approval above a monetary limit** (below = free) — governed by **LRS**. Sequence: Sch I? → Sch II? → Sch III over limit? → else **FREE**. **LRS:** resident individual up to **USD 2,50,000 / FY** (*confirm*), covering **both** certain current **and** certain capital uses (the one shared door in the wall).

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **Object = facilitation** (not "conserve/regulate" — that was FERA).
2. Residence: **> 182 days in *preceding* FY** + **purpose overrides**; do NOT import Income-tax **60/182 current-year** dual test.
3. **Citizenship ≠ residence.**
4. **Loan = capital; interest on it = current** (2(j)(ii)); **short-term** trade credit = current, **long-term** loan = capital.
5. Current default is free but **NOT absolute** (Schedules I-III).
6. **Current → Central Govt; Capital → RBI** — don't swap; capital is *restricted unless permitted*.
7. FEMA civil, but **S.14 civil imprisonment for non-payment within 90 days**.
8. Penalty = **up to 3× / up to ₹2,00,000 / up to ₹5,000 per day** — a **ceiling**.
9. **Two clocks: compounding 180 days; penalty payment 90 days** — don't swap.
10. **Hawala** caught by S.3 even with no border-crossing (substance over form).
11. FEMA reaches **branches abroad controlled by residents** (by control).
12. **S.6(3) omitted (Finance Act 2015)**; debt/non-debt split introduced.
13. **No day-count for companies** — Indian-incorporated = resident auto; foreign branch in India = resident by control.
14. **Contingent liabilities (guarantees) = capital** even with no cash flow.
15. **Inbound** (non-resident buying Indian assets) is covered — 2(e) is symmetric.
16. **Compounding = right to APPLY, not to settle** (may be refused).
17. **6(4)/6(5)** protect **legacy** assets (acquired while opposite status / inherited); only **fresh** acquisitions regulated — split by **timing of acquisition**.
18. **LRS is dual-natured** (current + certain capital).
19. Ss.7-8 = **"reasonable steps"** (effort, not guaranteed result).
20. **High Court appeal = question of law only**, within 60 days.

## One-line recall
- FERA (R = Restriction, criminal) → FEMA (M = Management, civil): the R→M change is the whole philosophy.
- **P&L = current (free, S.5, Central Govt); Balance Sheet = capital (regulated, S.6, RBI).** Mirror rule.
- Answer spine: **Person → Transaction → Schedule/limit → Channel (AP) → Enforcement.**
- Penalty up to **3×** / ₹2,00,000 / ₹5,000 per day; **compound within 180 days**; **pay within 90 days** or civil imprisonment (S.14 — for non-payment, not the breach).
- FEMA is **symmetric** (inbound + outbound) and **extra-territorial by control**; hawala caught "in any manner" (S.3).
- **6(4)/6(5)** protect legacy assets; **LRS USD 2,50,000/FY** is the shared current+capital door.
