# Acceptance of Deposits — CA Inter Revision Note

> Companies Act 2013, Sections 73–76A + Companies (Acceptance of Deposits) Rules, 2014.

## Snapshot
- A **deposit** is economically a loan; regulated because the lender is a *helpless, unsecured, diffuse public saver*. Protects the weak lender.
- Architecture in one line: **define widely (2(31)), exempt narrowly (Rule 2(1)(c)), fence what remains (73–76A)**.
- Vulnerability ladder → regulation intensity: Banks/Govt (exempt) → directors/insiders (exempt with declaration) → members (S.73, light) → public (S.76 + rating, heaviest).
- Each promise gets a **provable, independent** provision: circular (disclose), file with ROC (record), DRRA (fund), insurance, no-default certificate, credit rating, trustee.

## Core concepts
- **Deposit — 2(31):** "receipt of money by way of deposit or loan or in any other form," minus prescribed exemptions. Substance over label — "advance/loan/subscription" relabelling defeated.
- **Anti-parking clocks:** share application money — allot in **60 days**, refund in **15 days** after, else deposit; trade/service advance — adjust in **365 days**, else deposit. *Later refund does NOT un-convert.*
- **Three lanes:** exempt (Rule 2(1)(c)) → Govt company (RBI regime) → **Eligible company (S.76)** = public co with net worth **≥ ₹100 cr OR turnover ≥ ₹500 cr** (public + members) → ordinary company (**members only, S.73**).
- **Base for all caps** = paid-up capital + **free reserves** + securities premium. Exclude **revaluation reserve** (not a free reserve, 2(43)).

## Computation logic (deposit ceilings)
- Base = paid-up capital + free reserves + securities premium (**exclude revaluation reserve**).
- Ordinary co: 35% × base − existing member deposits = headroom.
- Eligible co: test 10% (members) and 25% (public) **separately** — cannot merge or lend headroom between lanes.

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **"Includes loan"** — a loan from the public IS a deposit; exemption list is the only escape.
2. **60/15 and 365 clocks** — expiry silently converts to deposit; **later refund does not un-convert**.
3. **Members (35%) vs public** — only eligible co touches public (25%), its members slice drops to **10%**.
4. **Eligibility = OR not AND** — net worth ₹100 cr OR turnover ₹500 cr.
5. **Special (public/76) vs ordinary (members/73)** resolution — don't swap.
6. **DRRA = 20%** (was 15%) of **next year's** maturities, by **30 April**, separate a/c — not "15%", not "total deposits", not "31 March".
7. **Deposit insurance deferred** — know it exists, flag postponement.
8. **Director loan needs non-borrowed declaration; relative exemption only for private co** (+ declaration).
9. **6 to 36 months** (not 60); 3-month short-term is narrow exception ≤10%.
10. **DPT-3 also reports non-deposit receipts/loans** — file even with no deposits.
11. **Govt eligible co = 35% public** (vs 25% private-sector).
12. **S.74 = old (pre-2013) deposits** — one-time bridge, don't confuse with ongoing repayment.
13. **Revaluation reserve is NOT a free reserve** — exclude from base (2(43)).
14. **"Company" ≠ firm/LLP** in inter-corporate exemption.
15. **Company fine floor = lower of ₹1 cr or 2× deposits** — small contravention gets 2× (e.g. ₹30 lakh deposit → ₹60 lakh); fine is **in addition to** repayment.
16. **Eligible-co buckets don't merge** — 10% and 25% tested separately.
17. **Employee security deposit** must be non-interest + within annual salary.
18. **Promoter loan exemption dies with the bank loan.**
19. **Circular has 6-month shelf life** — collecting on stale circular = contravention.

## One-line recall
- Define widely (2(31)), exempt narrowly (Rule 2(1)(c)), fence in proportion to lender helplessness, force provable safety nets, punish default as public wrong.
- Eligible co = net worth **₹100 cr OR** turnover **₹500 cr**; caps **35%** (ordinary members) / **10% members + 25% public** (eligible) / **35%** (Govt public) / **100%** (exempt private).
- Clocks: share money **60+15**, trade advance **365**; tenure **6–36 months**.
- DRRA **20%** of next-yr maturities by **30 April**; DPT-3 return by **30 June** (31 March snapshot).
- Default: NCLT (73(4)) → unlimited personal liability for fraud (75) → 76A penalty (company lower of ₹1 cr/2×, up to ₹10 cr; officer ₹25 lakh–₹2 cr + 7 yrs).
- Forms: DPT-1 invites, DPT-2 protects, DPT-3 reports, DPT-4 transitions.
