# Declaration & Payment of Dividend — CA Inter Revision Note

> Companies Act 2013, Chapter VIII, Sections 123–127 + Companies (Declaration & Payment of Dividend) Rules, 2014 + IEPF Rules, 2016.

## Snapshot
- One rule: **dividend only out of genuinely earned + realised profit, NEVER out of the capital that protects creditors** (capital maintenance).
- Two anchors: **A — Real profit test** (protects creditors); **B — Money must actually move** (protects shareholders).
- The switch: **recommendation = reversible hope; declaration = irreversible debt.** Explains irrevocability, 30-day clock, interest penalties.
- Queue: **creditors before owners, always** (residual-claimant logic).

## Core concepts
- **Dividend — 2(35):** "includes any interim dividend" (plugs the interim escape hatch). Paid on **paid-up** value, not face (S.51).
- **Free reserves — 2(43):** distributable per latest audited BS, EXCLUDING revaluation / notional / unrealised / fair-value gains. Securities premium & CRR are NOT free reserves (ring-fenced). "Free = free of fantasy."
- **Final** dividend: Board recommends, **members declare** at AGM (can reduce, NOT increase). **Interim** dividend: **Board declares**.
- **"Declared" ≠ "Paid":** declaration creates debt; payment discharges it. A validly declared but unpaid dividend = S.127 offence.

## Key provisions / sections
| Section / Rule | Rule | Limit / detail |
|---|---|---|
| 123(1) | Sources | (a) current-year profit after depreciation, (b) past undistributed profit after depreciation, (c) Govt guarantee money. **1st proviso:** set off carried-over losses + past unprovided depreciation first. **2nd proviso:** transfer to reserves = **VOLUNTARY** (no min/max; 1956 compulsory transfer abolished) |
| 123(2) | Depreciation | Compulsory per **Schedule II** (useful-life based); current + arrears; always mandatory even in bumper year |
| Rule 3 | Dividend from past reserves (inadequate/nil profit) | **"3-10-plug-15":** rate ≤ avg of last **3 years**; amount ≤ **10%** of (paid-up capital + free reserves); set off current losses first; balance reserves ≥ **15%** of paid-up capital. All four apply together. Rate cap waived if no dividend in all 3 preceding years |
| 123(3) | Interim dividend | Board declares (during FY or between FY-end and AGM) from surplus P&L / current-year profit / profit to preceding quarter. **Loss-brake:** if loss up to end of preceding quarter, rate ≤ **3-year average** |
| 123(4) | Separate account | Deposit dividend in separate scheduled-bank account within **5 days** of declaration |
| 123(5) | Mode / payee | **Cash only** (incl. cheque/warrant/electronic — NOT in kind); to registered holder / order / banker |
| 123(6) | Deposit-defaulter bar | Company failing S.73/74 (deposits) cannot declare **any equity dividend** while failure continues |
| 124(1) | Unpaid Dividend A/c | Unclaimed/unpaid after 30 days → transfer to Unpaid Dividend Account within **7 days** (= **day 37**) |
| 124(3) | Default interest | **12% p.a.** on amount not transferred (to members' benefit) |
| 124(2) | Website disclosure | Statement of names/amounts on website within **90 days** |
| 124(5) | Money to IEPF | Unclaimed **7 years** from transfer to Unpaid Dividend A/c → IEPF |
| 124(6) | **Shares to IEPF** | Shares unpaid/unclaimed dividend for **7 CONSECUTIVE years** → transferred to IEPF Authority (one claim resets clock); reclaimable |
| 124(7) | Penalty | Company + officers fined (confirm current figures) |
| 125 | IEPF | Fund for investor education + refunds; credited with unpaid dividends, refundable application money, matured deposits/debentures, etc., unclaimed 7 yrs. Owner reclaims from **IEPF Authority** (Form IEPF-5), not company |
| 126 | Abeyance (mid-transfer) | Dividend → Unpaid Dividend A/c (unless registered holder authorises transferee); rights & bonus **held in abeyance** till transfer registered |
| 127 | Non-payment of declared dividend | Not paid/warrant not posted in **30 days** → every director party to default: **jail up to 2 yrs + fine ≥ ₹1,000/day**; company: **18% p.a.** interest. **5 exceptions** below |

**S.127 five exceptions (no offence):** (1) couldn't pay due to operation of law; (2) shareholder's directions couldn't be complied with; (3) dispute over right to receive; (4) lawful **set-off** against sum due from shareholder (e.g. call in arrears); (5) non-payment not due to company's default.

## Computation logic (Rule 3 — dividend from reserves)
- Convert "reserves" → **free reserves** first (strip revaluation reserve, securities premium, CRR).
- Test all 4 caps; find the binding one: rate (≤3-yr avg), amount (≤10% of cap+free reserves), set-off current loss, floor (reserves ≥15% of paid-up capital).
- Different caps bind at different rates — rate cap often decisive.

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **Transfer to reserves is VOLUNTARY** (2013 Act) — compulsory graded transfer is 1956 law. But Rule 3 imposes a conditional **15% floor** on withdrawals.
2. **Members can reduce, NOT increase** the Board's recommended final dividend.
3. **Revaluation surplus never distributable** (2(43)); also excludes notional/fair-value gains.
4. **37 days** to Unpaid Dividend A/c (= 30 + 7), not 30 or 7 alone.
5. **SHARES also move to IEPF** (124(6)) after **7 consecutive years** — not just money.
6. **IEPF money/shares reclaimable** forever from the Authority (125(3)(a)).
7. **12% (S.124) vs 18% (S.127)** — don't swap. 18% = worse offence (not paying shareholder at all).
8. **No dividend in kind** — cash/cheque/electronic only.
9. **Interim loss-brake:** loss up to preceding quarter → rate ≤ 3-yr average.
10. **Deposit-defaulter bar** (123(6)) — no equity dividend while S.73/74 default continues (cure default → gate opens).
11. **30 days from declaration**, not from AGM notice/record date.
12. **Depreciation always mandatory** (123(2)), even in high-profit year.
13. **Set off past losses + unprovided past depreciation** first, even from current profit.
14. **"Consecutive"** = unbroken for share transfer; one claim resets. Money clock (7 yrs from parking) ≠ share clock (7 consecutive years).
15. **Securities premium / CRR never distributable.**
16. **Final = members declare; interim = Board declares** — don't swap.
17. **All 4 Rule 3 caps together** — fail one, dividend dies.
18. **Dividend on paid-up value** (S.51), not face — partly-paid trap.
19. **S.127 exceptions** — if facts fit (esp. set-off / returned undelivered), no offence, no 18%.

## One-line recall
- Dividend only from real realised profit (current/past after depreciation or Govt guarantee); set off past losses first; reserve transfer voluntary.
- Rule 3 raid = **"3-10-plug-15"** on **free** reserves (strip revaluation).
- Timeline: **5** ring-fence · **30** pay · **37** park · **90** publish · **7 years** to IEPF (money AND shares, reclaimable).
- Interest: **12%** parking late (124), **18%** paying never (127); director jail up to 2 yrs + ₹1,000/day.
- Interim = Board declares with loss-brake (≤3-yr avg); final = members declare (can't exceed Board rate).
- Deposit-defaulter (73/74) cannot declare equity dividend (123(6)); creditors before owners.
