# Companies Act 2013 — Preliminary & Key Definitions

## Snapshot
Definitions live in Section 2 of the Companies Act 2013. A company is an artificial legal person: separate legal entity, limited liability, perpetual succession, separate property, capacity to sue/be sued, transferable shares, common seal (now optional). It is a person but NOT a citizen (acts through agents). Core exam battleground: classification (private/public/OPC/small/listed), the holding-subsidiary-associate control ladder, Government/foreign/Sec 8 companies, and lifting the corporate veil. "means" = exhaustive; "includes" = extensive; "means and includes" = exhaustive for listed items.

## Core concepts
- Two privileges are separable: separate personality (company distinct, owns its assets) vs limited liability (members' cap). An unlimited company has personality but no liability cap.
- Liability axis (MOA choice): Unlimited [2(92)] → Limited by guarantee (guarantee sum callable ONLY on winding up, not working capital) → Limited by shares (unpaid amount). Guarantee + share capital possible.
- Members vs shareholders differ: member = name in register of members [2(55)]; subscriber to MOA becomes member on incorporation before allotment.
- Veil-lifting is the counter-doctrine: narrow, issue-specific, only for fraud/sham/evasion/enemy character/statutory command — never mere creditor inconvenience.

## Key provisions / sections
| Section | Term | Rule / limit |
|---|---|---|
| 2(20) | Company | Incorporated under this/previous Act (creature of statute) |
| 2(68) | Private company | Restricts share transfer; **max 200 members** (past/present employee-members excluded; joint holders = 1); no public invitation; **min 2**; min 2 directors |
| 2(71) | Public company | Not private; **subsidiary of public = deemed public** despite articles; **min 7 members**, **min 3 directors**; no max members |
| 2(62), 3 | OPC | **One** member + nominee; only natural person, Indian citizen & resident; one OPC + nominee of one only; min 1 director; **is a private company**; cannot do NBFI/invest in securities; minor barred |
| 2(85) | Small company | Private + paid-up capital ≤ ₹4 cr **AND** turnover ≤ ₹40 cr (confirm); status DYNAMIC (tested yearly, no registration); excludes public/holding/subsidiary/Sec 8/special-Act |
| 2(87) | Subsidiary | Control of Board composition **OR** **>50%** voting power (direct + indirect); layers limited (≈2) |
| 2(46) | Holding | Company whose subsidiaries exist (incl. body corporate — 2017) |
| 2(6) | Associate | Significant influence = **≥20% voting power** OR control of business decisions by agreement; NOT subsidiary; JV included |
| 2(52) | Listed | ANY securities listed on recognised stock exchange (2020 carve-outs; "listed" ≠ "listed equity") |
| 2(11) | Body corporate | Incl. foreign cos & LLPs; excl. co-op societies & specified bodies |
| 2(45) | Government company | **≥51%** paid-up capital held by Central/State/both; subsidiary of Govt co is also Govt co; NOT State/agent, employees not govt servants |
| 2(42) | Foreign company | Incorporated outside India + place of business/operations (incl. electronic mode) in India |
| 2(92) | Unlimited company | Members bear full liability |
| Sec 8 | Non-profit | Charitable objects; profits only to objects; no dividend; may drop "Limited"; formed by CG licence (revocable); cannot be "small"; MOA alteration needs CG approval |
| Sec 3 | Formation | Public 7 / Private 2 / OPC 1 (memory: 7-2-1); max directors 15 (more by SR) |
| Sec 9 | Effect of incorporation | Separate person, perpetual succession, common seal optional, capacity to sue |

**Control ladder:** ≥20% (or agreement) = Associate (influence) → >50% or board control = Subsidiary/Holding. Count direct + indirect. Exactly 50% ≠ subsidiary on votes.

**Veil-lifting grounds** — Judicial: fraud/sham/cloak (*Gilford v Horne*, *Jones v Lipman*); tax/obligation evasion (*Dinshaw Petit*); enemy character (*Daimler*); single economic entity where justice requires (*Renusagar*). Statutory: Sec **339** (fraudulent trading — personal unlimited liability), Sec **34/35** (prospectus misstatement), Sec **447** (fraud). *Prest v Petrodel*: pierce only for evasion, not mere concealment.

**Landmark cases:** *Salomon* (separate entity, even one-man co) • *Lee v Lee's Air Farming* (member can also be employee) • *Macaura* (100% shareholder has no insurable interest in company property) • *Bacha F. Guzdar* (dividend from tea co not agricultural income in member's hands) • *STC / Tata Engineering* (company not a citizen).

## Exam traps & must-remember
- **No minimum paid-up capital** since 2015 amendment (old ₹1 lakh/₹5 lakh gone). Common seal optional since 2015.
- Small company: both capital AND turnover must be under limits; holding/subsidiary/Sec 8/special-Act NEVER small regardless of numbers.
- Private company member cap is **200** (not 50 — that was 1956 Act); joint holders = 1; past/present employee-members excluded.
- Subsidiary voting test is **">50%"** not "≥50%"; exactly 50% ≠ subsidiary on votes. Government company is **≥51%**.
- Turns on **voting power**, not shareholding % (preference/DVR shares can vote).
- Associate = **≥20%** significant influence (or agreement limb — even <20% with veto agreement counts); don't confuse with control (>50%).
- Salomon holds even for one-man companies — don't lift veil just because one person controls or the company can't pay.
- Perpetual succession ≠ immortality — company can be wound up / struck off.
- OPC is a private company (gets private-co treatment plus extra reliefs: may dispense with AGM, no cash-flow statement). Residency day-count amended (182→120, verify).
- Government company remains a separate legal person; employees not government servants.
- OPC conversion thresholds (₹50 lakh capital / ₹2 cr turnover) REMOVED in 2021.

## One-line recall
- 7-2-1 = min members Public/Private/OPC; max members Private 200, Public unlimited; min directors 3/2/1.
- Control ladder: ≥20% Associate, >50% or board control Subsidiary/Holding; count direct + indirect.
- Government co ≥51%; Foreign co = incorporated abroad + place of business in India; Sec 8 = charity, no dividend, may drop "Limited".
- Veil lifted only for fraud/sham/evasion/enemy character/statute (Sec 339, 34/35, 447) — narrow & issue-specific.
- No minimum paid-up capital (2015); common seal optional (2015).
- Amendments to verify: small-co limits ₹4cr/₹40cr, OPC thresholds removed (2021), subsidiary "voting power" wording (2017), listed carve-outs (2020).
