# Prospectus & Allotment of Securities — CA Inter Revision Note

> Companies Act 2013, Chapter III, Sections 23–42 + Companies (Prospectus & Allotment of Securities) Rules, 2014 + SEBI (ICDR) for listed issues.

## Snapshot
- Cures the information asymmetry: company knows all, investor knows nothing. Three moves — **force truth out (disclosure)**, **punish the lie (liability)**, **guard the money (entry controls)**.
- Golden rule (*New Brunswick v Muggeridge*): disclose with scrupulous accuracy, no concealment → parent of S.26/34/35.
- *Derry v Peek*: fraud needs knowing/reckless falsehood → so **civil S.35 (needs only untruth + reliance + loss)** is easier to win than **criminal S.34 (routes to 447 fraud)**.
- Three time-zones: BEFORE money (23,25,26,27,31,32,33) · AT inducement (34,35,36,38) · AFTER collection (39,40,42).

## Core concepts
- **Prospectus — S.2(70):** ANY document/notice/circular/ad inviting the public to subscribe/purchase securities; includes RHP & shelf. Trigger = **invitation to the public**, not the label. Four ingredients: document · invitation to public · from public to subscribe/purchase · securities of a body corporate. Purely oral invite ≠ prospectus (but may be S.36).
- **"Public" = a number, not a vibe:** offer to **50 or more** persons (ex-QIB, ex-ESOP) in a FY is deemed offer to public. Domestic/private circle ≠ public (*Nash v Lynde*).
- **Four species:** Deemed (25 = disguise), Shelf (31 = stock 1 year), Red Herring (32 = price Hidden), Abridged (33 = tiny, on the form). Ordinary full prospectus (26) = baseline.
- **Misstatement:** untrue if misleading in form/context, OR omission calculated to mislead. *Half-truth = whole lie.*
- **Void vs irregular:** no listing permission (S.40) = allotment **VOID**; no minimum subscription (S.39) = allotment **irregular/barred**.

## Key provisions / sections
| Section | Rule | Limit / detail |
|---|---|---|
| 23 | Modes of issue | Public co = 3 doors (public offer / private placement / rights-bonus); Private co = 2 (NO public door, S.2(68)) |
| 2(70) | Prospectus defined | Any document inviting public |
| **25** | Deemed prospectus | Allot to issuing house → offer for sale = deemed prospectus of company. Presumption if EITHER (i) public offer **within 6 months** of allotment, OR (ii) consideration **not fully received** at offer date. Two limbs are ALTERNATIVE |
| **26** | Contents & registration | Signed by directors, delivered to Registrar before publication; valid **90 days** from registration; expert consent (expert unconnected + written consent not withdrawn); material contracts + reports attached |
| **27** | Variation in terms/objects | **Special resolution** by **postal ballot**; dissenters get **exit** (listed/SEBI, per ICDR); notice published English + vernacular + website with justification |
| **31** | Shelf prospectus | Prescribed class (banks/NBFCs/FIs); valid up to **1 year**; each tranche = information memorandum **Form PAS-2**; withdrawal right on material change |
| **32** | Red herring | Price/quantity omitted; file RHP → bid → file final prospectus with price; file RHP ≥ **3 days** before offer opens |
| **33** | Abridged prospectus | Must accompany **every application form**; exception = underwriting invite / not offered to public |
| **34** | Criminal liability | Untrue/misleading statement or omission → every person who **authorised issue** → **S.447 fraud** (6 mo–10 yr; min 3 yr if public interest; fine = amount to 3×). Defence: immaterial OR reasonable belief up to issue |
| **35** | Civil liability | Subscriber **relied + lost** → directors, to-be-named directors, promoters, authorisers, **experts** (own part) **jointly & severally compensate**. Defences: withdrew consent before issue / no knowledge + public notice / reasonable belief / expert reliance. Defences BLOCKED if statement made to defraud (35(3), unlimited liability) |
| **36** | Fraudulent inducement | Knowing/reckless false statement/forecast/concealment to induce investment → **S.447**. Wider than 34 (any means, not just prospectus) |
| **37** | Class action | Group may sue company/directors/auditors/experts; route via **S.245 (NCLT)** |
| **38** | Personation | Fictitious name / multiple applications / inducing allotment to fake names → **S.447**; disgorgement, seizure, gains to **IEPF**; warning must be printed in prospectus & form |
| **39** | Minimum subscription | No allotment until minimum subscription received; SEBI floor = **90%** of issue; application money ≥ **5%** of nominal value; refund if not met (≈15 days from close); delay → **15% p.a.** interest; money held in trust |
| **40** | Listing + money handling | Apply to stock exchange before prospectus; **no listing = allotment VOID**, repay; application money in **separate scheduled-bank account**, used only for allotment/refund; commission cap **5% shares / 2.5% debentures** or articles' rate (whichever less), authorised by articles + disclosed |
| **41** | GDR | Issue depository receipts abroad after **special resolution**; custodian/overseas depository structure; depository = registered member |
| **42** | Private placement | Offer to **≤ 200** persons/FY per kind of security (ex-QIB, ex-ESOP); identified persons only via **PAS-4** (serially numbered); record in **PAS-5**; NO public solicitation; banking channel only; separate account; **allot in 60 days** else refund in **15 days** else **12% p.a.**; can't use money till allotment + **PAS-3** filed (15 days); breach = deemed public offer + penalty (amount raised or **₹2 cr**, whichever lower) + refund in 30 days |

**Forms:** PAS-2 (shelf tranche IM) · PAS-3 (return of allotment) · PAS-4 (offer letter) · PAS-5 (record of offers).

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **34 vs 35 swap.** S.34 = criminal (jail via 447); S.35 = civil (compensation). Criminal needs NO investor loss; civil needs reliance + loss.
2. **Omission is not "safe"** — misleading omission = misstatement (34). Half-truth = whole lie.
3. **50 vs 200:** 50 = public-offer trip-wire; 200 = private-placement annual cap. Both exclude QIBs & ESOP.
4. **Deemed (25) ≠ Red herring (32):** 25 = disguise via issuing house; 32 = genuine prospectus with price left out.
5. **Numbers:** prospectus validity **90 days**; shelf **1 year**; deemed window **6 months**.
6. **Private company + public offer = impossible**, not merely penalised — must convert first.
7. **App money ≥ 5%; min subscription (SEBI) = 90%** — different figures, different jobs.
8. **Expert's defence** shields a director who reasonably relied; the **expert himself** stays liable for his part. Expert must be unconnected + written consent (26).
9. **"Reasonable belief"** must hold **up to issue** — later discovery + silence loses the shield.
10. **S.39 "no allotment" is a bar** — board CANNOT allot the part-subscribed and refund the rest.
11. **S.42 money untouchable pre-allotment** even in a valid placement (till PAS-3 filed).
12. **Void vs irregular:** listing fail (40) = VOID; min-subscription fail (39) = irregular/barred.
13. **S.25's two limbs are ALTERNATIVE** (6 months OR consideration not fully received) — not both.
14. **Secondary-market buyer CANNOT sue under 35** — protects the *subscriber who relied on the prospectus*, not a later exchange purchaser.
15. **Rescission** (contract remedy, unwinds allotment) dies at winding-up / affirmation / delay / inability to restore — distinct from S.35 compensation.
16. **S.36 wider than S.34** — catches any inducement (speech, forecast), not just a prospectus.
17. **Objects can't be silently changed** — needs special resolution + dissenter exit (27); silent diversion retro-makes prospectus misleading (34/35).

## One-line recall
- Prospectus = any document inviting the **public** (2(70)); public co = 3 doors, private co = 2 (no public door).
- Liability: **34 = Criminal → 447**, **35 = Civil (compensation, subscriber relied + lost)**, **36 inducement / 38 personation → 447**.
- Numbers ladder: **50** public trip-wire · **200** private cap · **90 days** prospectus · **1 year** shelf · **6 months** deemed · **5%** app money · **90%** min subscription · **60 days** allot / **15 days** refund / **12%** (private), **15%** (public).
- Void vs irregular: **no listing (40) = VOID; no min subscription (39) = irregular**.
- Deemed (25) = disguise via issuing house (6 months OR unpaid consideration); RHP (32) = price hidden.
- Breach of S.42 = deemed public offer + penalty (amount raised or ₹2 cr) + refund 30 days.
