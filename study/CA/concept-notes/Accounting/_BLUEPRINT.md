## Per-chapter internal structure (the deep standard)
Each concept chapter (expanded from the 8-part skeleton) will contain:
1. The Problem (real-world origin)
2. The Core Idea / intuition
3. Why it's built this way — every rule justified
4. **Full technical content** — all provisions/formats/computation steps needed for the exam, taught conceptually (this is the "textbook completeness" layer, woven in, not dumped)
5. Multiple reasoned + numerical examples, worked step by step
6. Presentation & disclosure (formats)
7. Connections across chapters/subjects
8. Traps, examiner tricks, and how questions are framed
9. First-principles recap
10. Quick-revision sheet (formulas/entries/sections on one page)

Each `qa/` bank will contain: concept-check questions, graded computational problems (easy→exam-hard), past-paper-style full questions with model answers, and MCQ/case scenarios with reasoning.
