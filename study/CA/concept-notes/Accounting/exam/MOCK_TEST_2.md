## Division B — Model Solutions

### Solution 1(a) — Borrowing Costs (AS 16)

**Step 1 — Specific borrowing**

| Particulars | ₹ |
|---|---|
| Interest on specific borrowing (15,00,000 × 11%) | 1,65,000 |
| Less: Income on temporary investment of specific borrowing | (40,000) |
| **Eligible borrowing cost on specific borrowing** | **1,25,000** |

**Step 2 — General borrowings**

Capitalisation rate = (40,00,000 × 10% + 20,00,000 × 12%) ÷ (40,00,000 + 20,00,000)
= (4,00,000 + 2,40,000) ÷ 60,00,000 = 6,40,000 ÷ 60,00,000 = **10.6667%**.

The 1 April expenditure of ₹15,00,000 is fully met by the specific borrowing of ₹15,00,000. The later expenditure is met from general borrowings:

| Date | Expenditure (₹) | Months outstanding | Weighted amount (₹) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Aug 2025 | 12,00,000 | 8 | 8,00,000 |
| 1 Dec 2025 | 9,00,000 | 4 | 3,00,000 |
| **Weighted-average expenditure from general borrowings** | | | **11,00,000** |

General borrowing cost = 11,00,000 × 10.6667% = **₹1,17,333**.
(This is well within the actual general borrowing cost of ₹6,40,000, so no cap applies.)

**Step 3 — Total borrowing cost to be capitalised**

= 1,25,000 + 1,17,333 = **₹2,42,333**.

### Solution 1(b) — Events after the Balance Sheet Date (AS 4)

1. **Insolvency of debtor (₹8,00,000; 40% recoverable)** — **Adjusting event.** The debtor's financial difficulty existed at the BS date; the insolvency merely confirms it. Provide for the loss of 60% → **write off ₹4,80,000** and carry the debtor at ₹3,20,000.
2. **Fire destroying inventory (₹12,00,000) on 5 May 2026** — **Non-adjusting event.** The condition (fire) arose after the BS date. No adjustment; disclose the nature and estimated financial effect in the notes (if material, and consider the going-concern impact).
3. **Proposed dividend ₹5,00,000** — Under **AS 4 (revised)**, proposed dividend is **not** recognised as a liability at the BS date (it is a non-adjusting event). It is only **disclosed** in the notes to accounts.
4. **Court case decided against the company (₹3,00,000)** — **Adjusting event.** A present obligation existed at the BS date and the judgment confirms it. **Provide ₹3,00,000** as a liability.

### Solution 1(c) — Accounting for Taxes on Income (AS 22)

**Step 1 — Current tax (taxable income)**

| Particulars | ₹ |
|---|---|
| Accounting profit before tax | 40,00,000 |
| Add: Depreciation as per books | 6,00,000 |
| Less: Depreciation as per Income-tax | (10,00,000) |
| Add: Provision for doubtful debts (disallowed) | 1,50,000 |
| Add: Donation (permanently disallowed) | 50,000 |
| **Taxable income** | **38,00,000** |
| **Current tax @ 30%** | **11,40,000** |

**Step 2 — Deferred tax on timing differences**

| Timing difference | Amount (₹) | Nature | Deferred tax @ 30% |
|---|---|---|---|
| Excess tax depreciation (10,00,000 − 6,00,000) | 4,00,000 | Taxable temporary difference → **DTL** | 1,20,000 (expense) |
| Provision for doubtful debts | 1,50,000 | Deductible temporary difference → **DTA** | 45,000 (income) |
| **Net deferred tax expense (DTL net of DTA)** | | | **75,000** |

*(The donation of ₹50,000 is a permanent difference — no deferred tax.)*

**Step 3 — Total tax expense**

= Current tax 11,40,000 + Net deferred tax 75,000 = **₹12,15,000**.

**Reconciliation:** (Accounting profit + permanent difference) × rate = (40,00,000 + 50,000) × 30% = **₹12,15,000** ✓.
Profit after tax = 40,00,000 − 12,15,000 = ₹27,85,000.

### Solution 1(d) — AS 5 Treatment

### Solution 2(b) — Segment Reporting (AS 17)

A segment is reportable if it satisfies **any one** of the following 10% thresholds (₹ in lakhs):

**(i) Revenue test** — 10% of total revenue (external + inter-segment) = 10% × (170 + 35) = 10% × 205 = **20.5**.
Segments with total revenue ≥ 20.5: A (100), B (50), C (30) → qualify; D (15), E (10) → do not.

**(ii) Result test** — All-profit total = 25 + 5 + 3 + 2 = 35; all-loss total = 10. Higher absolute figure = **35**; 10% = **3.5**.
Segments with |result| ≥ 3.5: A (25), B (10), C (5) → qualify; D (3), E (2) → do not.

**(iii) Asset test** — 10% of total assets = 10% × 170 = **17**.
Segments with assets ≥ 17: A (90), B (45), C (20) → qualify; D (8), E (7) → do not.

**Reportable segments: A, B and C.**

**75% rule check:** External revenue of A + B + C = 80 + 40 + 30 = 150; total external revenue = 170. 150 ÷ 170 = **88.2% ≥ 75%** — satisfied. No additional segment need be designated as reportable. (D and E may be combined into "All other segments".)
