### Solution 1(b) — Borrowing Costs (AS 16)

**Step 1 — Capitalisation rate** (weighted average rate of general borrowings):

| Borrowing | Amount ₹ | Interest ₹ |
|---|---|---|
| Bank Loan @ 10% | 30,00,000 | 3,00,000 |
| 12% Debentures | 20,00,000 | 2,40,000 |
| **Total** | **50,00,000** | **5,40,000** |

Capitalisation rate = 5,40,000 ÷ 50,00,000 = **10.8%**

**Step 2 — Weighted average expenditure on the qualifying asset:**

| Date | Amount ₹ | Period outstanding | Weighted amount ₹ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 April 2025 | 30,00,000 | 12/12 | 30,00,000 |
| 1 October 2025 | 18,00,000 | 6/12 | 9,00,000 |
| **Total** | | | **39,00,000** |

**Step 3 — Borrowing cost to be capitalised:**

= Weighted average expenditure × Capitalisation rate = 39,00,000 × 10.8% = **₹4,21,200**

This is **lower than** the actual borrowing costs incurred (₹5,40,000), so the full ₹4,21,200 is capitalised (AS 16 caps the amount capitalised at actual borrowing cost).

**Step 4 — Amount charged to the Statement of Profit and Loss:**

= Actual borrowing cost − Capitalised = 5,40,000 − 4,21,200 = **₹1,18,800**

### Solution 3(b) — Leases (AS 19)

**Case 1 — Delta Ltd. (equipment):** This is a **finance lease**.
Reasons: (i) The lease term (5 years) covers the **entire economic life** of the asset — a lease-term indicator of a finance lease; and (ii) the present value of the minimum lease payments (₹9,50,000) amounts to **95%** of the fair value (₹10,00,000), i.e. **substantially all** of the fair value. Although ownership is not transferred and there is no bargain purchase option, the substance is that substantially all the risks and rewards incidental to ownership have been transferred to the lessee. AS 19 looks to substance over legal form; the lease is therefore a finance lease.

**Case 2 — Omega Ltd. (office premises):** This is an **operating lease**.
Reasons: (i) The lease term (3 years) is a small fraction of the economic life of the building; (ii) ownership never passes and there is no purchase option; (iii) the present value of the minimum lease payments is only **40%** of fair value — not substantially all; and (iv) the lease is **cancellable** at short notice, leaving significant risks and rewards with the lessor. None of the finance-lease indicators are met, so the lease is an operating lease.

### Solution 5(b) — Earnings Per Share (AS 20)
