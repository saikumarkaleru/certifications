# Chapter 12 — Audit of Different Entities (Revision)

## Snapshot
One audit framework, radically different entities. Audit approach = f(dominant risk, governing law, objective). SAs run underneath everything: SA 315/330 (risk & response), SA 500 (evidence), SA 530 (sampling), SA 620 (expert), SA 600 (another auditor), SA 610 (internal auditors), SA 210 (terms), SA 700/705/706 (report). Three-axis reflex: (1) how diffuse/vulnerable the principal → weight of regulatory overlay & second report; (2) lag to truth → verification vs estimation/expert; (3) public stake → truth alone vs truth + legality + propriety + performance.

## Core concepts
- **Adaptation Principle:** audit shaped by (a) dominant business risk, (b) governing legal framework (appointment, powers, reporting duty), (c) objective (truth-and-fairness alone, or also propriety & efficiency).
- **"True and fair" is entity-relative:** bank → subject to RBI norms (NPA overrides accrual); insurer → subject to IRDAI formats + actuary; government → supplemented by "authority and propriety." Some engagements are certifications/limited reviews/additional reports, not full reasonable-assurance audits.
- **Agency problem:** audit matches whose money is at risk. Diffuse public principals (depositors, policyholders, taxpayers) → regulator inserted as super-principal + third-party reporting duty. Private principals (partners, proprietor) → contractual audit only.
- **Estimation-heavy entities get an expert layer:** actuary *makes* the estimate; auditor *evaluates the estimator* (SA 620) — never recomputes.

## Key provisions / SAs (entity + statute + key requirement)

**Banks — BR Act 1949 / RBI.** Appointment needs **RBI approval** (not plain Sec 139). Dominant risk = credit risk (bad loans overstated). Audit pyramid: **Statutory Central Auditors (SCAs)** sign consolidated FS; **Branch auditors** submit report + branch LFAR, relied on via **SA 600**; **Concurrent/internal auditors** relied on via SA 315/**SA 610**. Focus:
- **Advances** classified per **IRAC norms** → Standard / Sub-Standard / Doubtful (D1/D2/D3) / Loss; provisioning rises up the ladder. NPA trigger ≈ **90 days overdue** (term loan); "out of order" for CC/OD.
- **No income on NPA except on realisation** (not accrual).
- **Doubtful provisioning splits secured vs unsecured** on *realisable* (not book) security value; unsecured portion → 100% provision.
- **LFAR** in RBI-prescribed questionnaire; income leakage; restructured/evergreened accounts; investments (HTM/AFS/HFT); CRAR; CBS/computerised-environment audit.
*(Confirm exact provisioning % and ageing bands vs RBI Master Directions.)*

**Insurance — Insurance Act 1938 / IRDAI.** Often joint auditors. Dominant risk = **under-reserving** of future liabilities. Focus:
- **Actuarial valuation** by **Appointed Actuary**, relied on via **SA 620** (evaluate competence, objectivity, reasonableness of assumptions — do NOT redo maths).
- **Life** = long-dated actuarial policy reserve. **General** = **UPR (Unearned Premium Reserve)** + claims reserves **IBNR / IBNER**.
- Premium recognition (when due); claims incurred/outstanding; **reinsurance** (only *valid, collectible* netted off); IRDAI investment norms; **solvency margin** (auditor checks computation); **commission & management-expense caps** under the Act (breach reportable).
- Segregate **policyholders' fund vs shareholders' fund**; IRDAI-prescribed formats (Revenue A/c, P&L, BS).

**NBFC — RBI Act 1934 / RBI (+ Companies Act, Sec 139).** Dominant risk = credit risk + registration/classification risk. Focus:
- Valid **Certificate of Registration**; **50-50 principal-business test** (financial assets ≥50% of total assets **AND** financial income ≥50% of gross income — both limbs).
- **Net Owned Fund (NOF)** minimum; shortfall reportable.
- **Scale-based regulation** — identify layer (Base/Middle/Upper/Top); norms differ by layer.
- Deposit-taking (NBFC-D) vs non-deposit (NBFC-ND); IRAC/prudential norms broadly parallel to banks.
- **NBFC Auditor's Report to Board/RBI** — additional report certifying registration, principal business, capital adequacy, NOF; **exception-reporting duty directly to RBI** (survives an unqualified SA 700 opinion).

**Government audit — Constitution Arts 148–151 / C&AG (DPC) Act 1971.** C&AG = constitutional authority; independence engineered (salary charged on Consolidated Fund, cannot be varied to disadvantage; removal like SC judge). Audits Union/States + govt companies via **Sec 143(5)–(7)** (direct manner, comment on/supplement company auditor's report, order test/supplementary audit). Three limbs:
- **Financial (regularity) audit** — true & fair + within sanction.
- **Propriety audit** — prudence, no waste/undue benefit. **Canons:** (a) expenditure not prima facie more than occasion demands; (b) no authority orders to own advantage; (c) no spending for a person/section unless justified; (d) allowances not a source of profit.
- **Performance audit — 3 Es:** Economy (right quality, lowest cost), Efficiency (max output from inputs), Effectiveness (achieved objective). Can be economical + efficient yet ineffective.
Reports → President/Governor → laid before Parliament/Legislature → examined by **Public Accounts Committee (PAC)**.

**Cooperative societies — Cooperative Societies Act 1912 / State Coop Act (State subject); multi-State → MSCS Act 2002.** **Auditor appointed by Registrar** (often from Registrar panel). Focus:
- Compliance with Act/Rules/bye-laws; **Reserve Fund** appropriation (commonly ≥25% of net profit — confirm); **cooperative education fund** contribution; **dividend ceiling**; **single-member shareholding limit**; permitted **investment modes**.
- **Overdue debts** — classify by period, assess recoverability & bad-debt reserve (special reporting requirement).
- **Audit classification / grading (A/B/C/D)** — unique to coop audit.
- Report goes to Registrar too; must comment on infringements + attach schedules Registrar requires.

**NGO / Charitable Trust / Society.** Form-dependent: **Trust** (Indian Trusts Act / State Public Trusts Act); **Society** (Societies Registration Act 1860); **Section 8 company** (Companies Act, Sec 139 audit). Tax: **Income-tax Act 12A/12AB** registration + **Form 10B** where income exceeds exemption limit. Foreign funds → **FCRA 2010** (designated account, separate audit). Dominant risk = funds not applied to object. Focus:
- **Application of income to object** — **~85% application test** (apply ≥85% of income to charitable objects in India; ≤15% accumulable; larger accumulation needs prescribed conditions/filings). *(Confirm % for AY.)*
- Donations completeness (cash/anonymous — **anonymous donations taxable beyond threshold**); **corpus donation = capital receipt** (not income); restricted/tied funds (fund accounting); grant-utilisation certificates.
- **FCRA:** foreign funds in designated account, **no commingling** (mixing itself is a violation).
- Investments in **Sec 11(5)** permitted modes (breach can cost exemption).
- Completeness of donations relies on *controls* (receipt-book, counterfoils) — inherent limitation on completeness of income.

**LLP — LLP Act 2008.** Audit mandatory **only if turnover > ₹40 lakh OR contribution > ₹25 lakh** (either triggers). Below both → no statutory audit (voluntary allowed). Focus:
- **LLP Agreement** (profit-sharing, contribution, rights/duties); if none → defaults in **Schedule I**.
- Partners' **contribution** incl. non-cash (valued/recorded per Agreement).
- **Designated partners** & filing duties; **Statement of Account & Solvency (Form 8)** + annual return **(Form 11)** with Registrar.
- Clear engagement letter (**SA 210**) if voluntary. *(Confirm thresholds/form numbers.)*

**Sole proprietor.** **No statutory audit under general law.** Any audit is voluntary/contractual or **Sec 44AB tax audit** (turnover/receipts over limit → **Form 3CB** [not otherwise audited] / **Form 3CA** [already audited under other law] + common **Form 3CD**). Focus:
- **Engagement letter (SA 210)** defines scope AND limits exposure (contract is the only source of duties).
- **Separation of personal & business** transactions (same legal person → test drawings, personal use of assets, personal expenses).

**Partnership firm — Partnership Act 1932.** **No statutory audit;** voluntary or **Sec 44AB**. Purpose often to resolve inter-partner suspicion. Focus:
- **Partnership Deed** = primary reference (profit ratios, interest, salary, admission/retirement).
- **No deed → Act defaults:** equal profit sharing; no interest on capital; no salary/remuneration; **6% p.a. on partner advances/loans beyond capital**.
- Remuneration/interest also capped for deductibility by **Sec 40(b)**; deed-allows ≠ tax-allows; **no deed → nil remuneration deductible**.
- Capital/current accounts; on constitution change → revaluation & goodwill in correct (old) ratio, post-change in new ratio.
- Confirm appointment by **all partners**; report addressed to partners. Registration optional (unregistered firm cannot sue to enforce contracts).

## Exam traps & must-remember
- **NPA interest on accrual = wrong** — only on realisation. Watch evergreening (new loan services old) & restructured accounts dressed as standard.
- **Doubtful provisioning:** split secured/unsecured on *realisable* security — no single flat rate on whole balance.
- **LLP threshold is OR, not AND** (turnover >₹40L OR contribution >₹25L). Checking only turnover loses marks.
- **Government: "legally sanctioned" ≠ nothing to report** — propriety audit still comments on extravagance (canon: not more than occasion demands).
- **No blind reliance on actuary/expert** — SA 620 requires evaluating assumptions; certificate is not a shield → modify opinion (SA 705) if material.
- **Wrong appointing authority:** coop = **Registrar**; bank = **RBI approval**; not plain Sec 139.
- **Proprietor/firm: no statutory audit** exists — don't assert one.
- **Corpus vs general donation** — corpus is capital receipt; anonymous donations taxable beyond threshold.
- **Missing the 85% application test** for a trust (compute shortfall).
- **Coop reserve fund %** (commonly ≥25% — confirm); don't forget education fund + dividend ceiling.
- **NBFC 50-50 test needs both limbs;** identify layer + deposit vs non-deposit.
- **Regulator ≠ appointer** — RBI approves/regulates bank auditors but that differs from who appoints.
- **FCRA commingling** is itself a violation regardless of spend.
- **Two-report entities — don't write only SA 700 opinion:** Bank (**+ LFAR**), NBFC (**+ RBI report**), government company (**+ C&AG comments under Sec 143(5)-(7)**), Cooperative (**+ Registrar report + audit classification**).

## Procedure sequence
SA 210 appointment/engagement check (correct statute/authority) → SA 315 understand entity & regulatory framework → SA 330 responses on the pressure point → SA 620 experts where technical → SA 500 evidence (+ SA 600 bank branches, SA 530 sampling high-volume) → entity-specific compliance → SA 700/705/706 opinion in prescribed format/addressee + special reports → SA 230 documentation showing entity-tailored risk assessment.

## One-line recall
- Audit approach = f(dominant risk, governing law, objective) — derive all nine entities from this one equation.
- Bank = advances/IRAC/provisioning/LFAR (BR Act, RBI approval); no NPA income except on realisation; split secured/unsecured on realisable security.
- Insurer = under-reserving; rely-but-verify Appointed Actuary via SA 620; life = actuarial reserve, general = UPR + IBNR/IBNER (Insurance Act 1938, IRDAI).
- Government = Arts 148-151 + C&AG Act 1971; financial + propriety (canons) + performance (3 Es); PAC; Sec 143(5)-(7) for govt companies.
- Coop = Registrar-appointed, reserve fund/overdue debts/audit classification (State Coop Act / MSCS Act 2002); NGO = ~85% application, Sec 12AB/Form 10B/FCRA.
- LLP audit if turnover >₹40L OR contribution >₹25L (LLP Act 2008); proprietor & firm = no statutory audit, only Sec 44AB/contractual, deed/engagement letter (SA 210) is the compass.
