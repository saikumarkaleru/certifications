# Chapter 09 — Audit Report & Opinions (Revision Note)

## Snapshot
The audit report is the auditor's SOLE output to users — a standardised, structured communication of a conclusion. It states ONE thing: an **opinion** on whether the FS give a **true and fair view** per the applicable framework, on **reasonable (not absolute) assurance** — an opinion, never a certificate. It carries a graded "bad news" dial (clean → qualified → adverse/disclaimer), walled-off attention channels (EOM/OM/KAM), boundary-policing sections (management vs auditor responsibility, to shrink the **expectation gap**), plus Indian bolt-ons (CARO, IFC, s.143 fraud). Governing SAs: **700, 701, 705, 706** (+ 570, 260/265, 450) and Companies Act **s.143**.

## Core concepts
- **Expectation gap** = two sub-species: *knowledge gap* (users misunderstand what audit promises) — report can attack this via Responsibilities sections; *performance gap* (auditor fell short of standards) — report cannot cure this.
- **Fair-presentation framework** (India: Companies Act + AS/Ind AS) → opinion says FS *"give a true and fair view."* **Compliance framework** (special-purpose) → says FS *"prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with"* — drops "true and fair."
- **Two independent switches, NOT one dial:** Switch A = *what kind of trouble* — **disagreement** (have evidence, numbers wrong) vs **inability** (lack evidence, don't know). Switch B = *how far it spreads* — isolated vs **pervasive**. Opinion = the coordinate on a 2×2, never a point on a 1×4 ladder.
- **Reasonable assurance** = high but not absolute (sampling, estimates, cost/time limits) → justifies "opinion" not "certificate."
- Opinion is on **"the FS as a whole"** — holistic, not line-by-line.

## Key provisions / SAs

### SA 700 (Revised) — Forming an Opinion & Reporting on FS
Governs the **unmodified (clean)** opinion and architecture of EVERY report.
- **Forming opinion:** conclude whether **reasonable assurance** obtained that FS as a whole are free from material misstatement (fraud or error). Evaluate: sufficient appropriate evidence (SA 330/500); uncorrected misstatements material individually/in aggregate (SA 450); fair presentation qualitatively — policies appropriate & consistently applied, estimates reasonable, info relevant/reliable/comparable/understandable, disclosures adequate, terminology appropriate, and FS refer to/describe the framework.
- **Elements in required order:** Title ("Independent Auditor's Report") → Addressee ("To the Members") → **Opinion (placed FIRST)** → Basis for Opinion (states SAs followed, auditor **independent** per ICAI Code + Act, evidence sufficient/appropriate) → [Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern — SA 570, if applicable] → **Key Audit Matters (SA 701)** → Responsibilities of Management/TCWG → Auditor's Responsibilities → Other Reporting Responsibilities (CARO, s.143(3)) → Signature (engagement partner name, membership no., **FRN**, **UDIN**) → Place & Date.
- **Opinion paragraph must name:** (a) identify entity; (b) FS have been audited; (c) title of each statement (BS, P&L incl. OCI, cash flow, statement of changes in equity); (d) refer to notes incl. significant accounting policies; (e) date/period covered.
- **Report date** must NOT be earlier than: (i) date sufficient appropriate evidence obtained, (ii) date FS (incl. notes) prepared, (iii) date **Board** approved/took responsibility. Dating before board approval = defective report. Date asserts subsequent events (SA 560) considered.

### SA 705 (Revised) — Modifications to the Opinion
**Q1 — Reason to modify (only two):**
1. **FS materially misstated (disagreement)** — from (a) inappropriate accounting policies, (b) misapplication of policies, or (c) inadequate/missing/misleading disclosures.
2. **Cannot obtain sufficient appropriate evidence (scope limitation/inability)** — from (a) circumstances beyond control (fire, appointed after stock count), (b) nature/timing of auditor's work, (c) **management-imposed limitation**. For (c): request removal → if refused, communicate with TCWG → if effect could be material & pervasive and no alternative evidence, **disclaim** or (if permitted) **withdraw**.

**Q2 — Extent (two axes):**
- **Material** = could reasonably influence users' economic decisions.
- **Pervasive** (higher bar, three limbs — name the limb): (a) NOT confined to specific elements; or (b) if confined, a **substantial proportion** of FS; or (c) for disclosures, **fundamental to users' understanding**. Pervasive = reach/centrality, not size alone.

**2×2 matrix:**
|  | Material, NOT pervasive | Material AND pervasive |
|---|---|---|
| **Misstatement (disagreement)** | **Qualified** | **Adverse** |
| **Scope limitation (inability)** | **Qualified** | **Disclaimer** |

- **Qualified:** *"except for the effects of…"* (disagreement) / *"except for the possible effects of…"* (scope) — one word "possible" encodes the switch.
- **Adverse:** *"do NOT give a true and fair view"* — ONLY from disagreement + pervasive.
- **Disclaimer:** *"we do not express an opinion"* — ONLY from inability + pervasive. ("I can't tell," not "it's wrong.")
- **Presentation:** heading changes; Basis becomes "Basis for Qualified/Adverse/Disclaimer of Opinion" and must **describe + quantify** financial effect (quantifiable misstatement unquantified = substantive defect). For a **missing disclosure**, auditor includes omitted disclosure in the Basis section itself (where practicable). For **disclaimer**: Auditor's Responsibilities section shortened, evidence-sufficiency sentence removed, and **NO KAM reported**. Cannot express unmodified opinion on a single item within a report giving adverse/disclaimer on FS as a whole under same framework.

### SA 706 (Revised) — Emphasis of Matter & Other Matter (do NOT modify opinion)
- **EOM** — draws attention to a matter **already presented/disclosed IN the FS**, fundamental to understanding. Conditions: opinion NOT modified re the matter, and it is NOT a KAM (mutually exclusive). Para under "Emphasis of Matter" heading, refers to the note, states opinion not modified. **Three gates:** (1) matter appropriately disclosed (else → modify, not EOM); (2) sufficient evidence it's not materially misstated; (3) not a KAM/modification matter. Overuse dilutes signal. Examples: exceptional litigation uncertainty, major catastrophe, early adoption of a standard.
- **OM** — matter **NOT in the FS** but relevant to understanding the **audit/auditor's responsibilities/report**. Heading "Other Matter." Examples: prior-year figures audited by **predecessor auditor**; report distribution restriction; two frameworks.
- **Direction:** EOM points *into* FS; OM points *outside* FS. Both communicated to TCWG (with wording) before issue.

### SA 701 — Key Audit Matters
- **Definition:** matters that, in auditor's professional judgment, were of **MOST significance** in the current-period audit; **selected from matters communicated to TCWG**.
- **Mandatory for listed entities** (or when law/regulation requires, or auditor elects). NO separate size/turnover threshold — trigger is *listed status*.
- **Funnel:** all TCWG matters → those requiring **significant auditor attention** (higher assessed/significant risks per SA 315; significant judgments re estimates with high uncertainty; significant events/transactions) → those of most significance = KAM.
- **Each KAM describes:** (a) why it's a KAM, (b) how addressed in audit, (c) reference to related disclosure.
- **"Not original source" rule:** auditor must not become original source of undisclosed entity info; encourage management to disclose instead.
- **Not a substitute:** does NOT replace a modification, going-concern section, or EOM. A matter causing a modification or going-concern material uncertainty IS by nature a KAM but is reported in its OWN section (not KAM), with cross-reference. On a **disclaimer**, NO KAM at all. If a listed entity genuinely has NO KAM, must STATE so under the heading (can't drop the section).

### Companies Act s.143 & CARO 2020
- **s.143(1) — inquiry duties** (report BY EXCEPTION, only if adverse): whether loans on security properly secured & terms not prejudicial; book entries prejudicial; personal expenses charged to revenue; investments sold below cost, etc.
- **s.143(2)-(3) — report MUST state:** all info/explanations obtained; **proper books** kept; branch report received; BS & P&L **agree with books**; FS **comply with AS**; observations with adverse effect; any **director disqualified** u/s 164(2); **adequate IFC with reference to FS** and operating effectiveness (IFC report); qualifications/reservations.
- **s.143(12) — fraud reporting:** fraud by officers/employees **against the company** ≥ **₹1 crore** (*verify*) → **Central Government** in Form **ADT-4** (route: report to Board/Audit Committee for reply → forward report + reply to CG within prescribed window). Below threshold → **Audit Committee/Board** + disclosed in Board's Report. Good-faith reporting is protected. Extends to cost/secretarial/branch auditors.
- **CARO 2020** — issued u/s **143(11)**, separate annex, **21 clauses**. Excludes banking, insurance, s.8, OPC, small companies, and certain private companies below specified thresholds (*verify limits*). Key clauses: (i) PPE & intangibles (records, physical verification, **title deeds in company name**, benami); (ii) Inventory + working-capital limits >₹5cr & quarterly returns agree with books; (iii) Investments/loans/guarantees, related-party terms, overdue, evergreening; (iv) s.185/186; (v) Deposits s.73-76; (vi) Cost records s.148(1); (vii) Statutory dues (PF/ESI/GST etc., undisputed >6 months, disputed with forum); (viii) Unrecorded income surrendered in IT assessment; (ix) Default in loan repayment, wilful defaulter, end-use, short-term funds for long-term; (x) IPO/FPO & preferential allotment end-use; (xi) **Fraud** noticed/reported, ADT-4, whistle-blower; (xii) Nidhi; (xiii) **Related-party** s.177/188; (xiv) **Internal audit** system; (xv) Non-cash transactions with directors s.192; (xvi) s.45-IA RBI (NBFC/CIC); (xvii) Cash losses; (xviii) **Resignation of statutory auditors**; (xix) Material uncertainty on meeting liabilities within 1 year (ratios/ageing); (xx) Unspent **CSR** transfer s.135; (xxi) Qualifications in CARO of **consolidated** components. Unfavourable clause → state reasons; unable to opine → state fact with reasons. CARO remark does NOT auto-qualify main opinion (auditor separately considers).

### s.143(3)(i) — IFC Reporting
- Report whether company has **adequate internal financial controls with reference to FS (IFCoFR)** and their **operating effectiveness**. Guided by ICAI Guidance Note. Separate **annexure**; own opinion (unmodified/qualified/adverse/disclaimer).
- **Scope trap:** directors' responsibility (s.134) covers BROAD IFC (orderly business, asset safeguarding, fraud prevention, records, reliable info); auditor's s.143(3)(i) report is on NARROWER **IFCoFR** (financial-reporting subset only).
- **Severity ladder (SA 265):** control deficiency → significant deficiency (merits TCWG attention) → **material weakness** (reasonable possibility a MATERIAL misstatement won't be prevented/detected timely). Only a **material weakness** modifies the IFC opinion; significant deficiency only communicated to TCWG.
- **Exemption:** certain private companies (MCA notification 13 June 2017: OPC/small, OR turnover < ₹50cr AND borrowings < ₹25cr) exempt from reporting on operating effectiveness (*verify thresholds*).

## Exam traps & must-remember
1. **Adverse vs Disclaimer:** Adverse = disagreement + pervasive ("it's wrong"). Disclaimer = inability + pervasive ("I can't tell"). Identify **reason FIRST**, then extent.
2. **Qualified answers BOTH reasons** when material-but-isolated (scope-isolated ≠ disclaimer).
3. **Material ≠ Pervasive** — pervasive has 3-limb definition; name the limb.
4. **"Effects" vs "possible effects"** — disagreement vs scope; marks awarded on this word.
5. **EOM does NOT modify opinion**; requires matter ALREADY disclosed (if should-be-disclosed-but-isn't → misstatement → modify).
6. **EOM (inside FS) vs OM (outside FS).** Predecessor auditor = **OM**.
7. **KAM ≠ modification/substitute;** modification matter goes in own section; no KAM on disclaimer; if none, STATE so.
8. **KAM only mandatory for listed entities** — no numeric threshold.
9. **CARO remark ≠ auto-qualification;** every unfavourable clause needs reasons.
10. **IFC opinion separate** from FS opinion (clean FS + adverse IFC possible); only IFCoFR scope.
11. **Significant deficiency ≠ material weakness** — only material weakness modifies IFC opinion.
12. **Report date** not before evidence + board approval; signature needs **FRN, membership no., UDIN**.
13. **Opinion paragraph comes FIRST** (before Basis) in revised SA 700.
14. **Going concern has its own home:** adequate disclosure → "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" section (SA 570), NOT EOM; inadequate disclosure → modify (qualified/adverse); wrong basis (liquidation) → **adverse**.
15. **s.143(1) is report-by-exception** vs always-state s.143(3).
16. **Fraud** ≥ ₹1cr (*verify*) → CG via ADT-4; below → Audit Committee/Board; only fraud by officers/employees against company; good-faith protected.
17. **Aggregate before deciding** (SA 450) — several isolated errors can jointly become pervasive → adverse.

## One-line recall
- Reason (disagreement vs inability) × Extent (material-not-pervasive vs material-and-pervasive) → opinion; it's a 2×2 coordinate, never a 1×4 ladder.
- Adverse ← only disagreement; Disclaimer ← only inability; Qualified serves both when isolated.
- "Effects" = disagreement qualification; "possible effects" = scope qualification.
- Pervasive = not confined / substantial proportion / fundamental to understanding — name the limb.
- SA 700 order: Title → Addressee → **Opinion first** → Basis → [GC] → KAM → Responsibilities → Auditor's Responsibilities → Other Reporting → Signature (FRN/M.No./UDIN) → Place & Date.
- Indian bolt-ons: CARO 2020 (21 clauses, s.143(11)) + IFC (s.143(3)(i), material weakness modifies) + fraud (s.143(12), ADT-4).
