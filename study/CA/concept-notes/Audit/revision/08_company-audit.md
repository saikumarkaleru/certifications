# Chapter 08 — Company Audit (Companies Act 2013, Sec 139–148)

## Snapshot
The problem: the auditor is chosen and paid by the managers he audits — risk of **auditor capture**. Sec 139–148 wrench each control lever (appointment, tenure, eligibility, pay, removal, powers, permitted work, signing, hearing, punishment) out of management's hands and re-route it to **members / a fixed statutory rule / an independent authority (NFRA, NCLT, Central Government, C&AG)**. Two recurring moves: **relocation of power** (gatekeeper height rises with the danger of the act) and **forced visibility** (file / certify / disclose / read aloud). Statute (not just ICAI ethics) is needed because ethics binds only the auditor; only statute binds the company.

## Core concepts
- **Independence of mind** — form opinion free of compromising influences. **Independence in appearance** — avoid circumstances a reasonable third party would see as impairing objectivity.
- **Five ethical threats → sections:** Self-interest → 141(3)(d), 142, 144. Self-review → 144. Advocacy → 144. Familiarity → 139(2) rotation, 141(3)(f). Intimidation → 140(1) removal double-lock, 140(4).
- **Qualified vs Eligible:** *Qualified* 141(1)-(2) = right type of person/firm (CA in practice / firm with majority CA partners). *Eligible* 141(3) = free of entanglement with this client. A shareholding CA is qualified but ineligible.
- Audit is **compulsory for every company** regardless of size/profit; rules are statutory and non-waivable.

## Key provisions / SAs (section no. + requirement)

**Sec 141(1)-(2) — Who may audit.** Only a **CA in practice**; a **firm/LLP** if majority of partners practising in India are CAs; only CA partners sign. 141(3)(a) bars a **body corporate except an LLP**.

**Sec 139(6)/(7) — First auditor.**
- Non-Govt (139(6)): **Board within 30 days** of registration; if Board fails, **members at EGM within 90 days** (90 days measured from registration). Holds office **till conclusion of 1st AGM**.
- Govt (139(7)): **C&AG within 60 days**; failing which Board within next 30 days; failing which members within 60 days at EGM. Also till 1st AGM.
- Term is event-linked (conclusion of 1st AGM), not a date; if AGM delayed, term stretches.

**Sec 139(1) — Subsequent (real) appointment.** At 1st AGM, **members** appoint auditor from conclusion of that meeting **to conclusion of 6th AGM (5 years)**. Need **written consent + certificate of Sec 141 compliance BEFORE appointment**; file **Form ADT-1** within **15 days**. **Annual ratification proviso OMITTED by Companies (Amendment) Act 2017** — no annual ratification now. Five-year term ≠ rotation (term vs number of terms).

**Sec 139(2)-(4) — Rotation** (prescribed classes: all listed cos; unlisted public with paid-up capital ≥ ₹10 cr; private with paid-up ≥ ₹50 cr; any such company with public borrowings from banks/FIs or deposits ≥ ₹50 cr — Rule 5, verify; OPC & small cos excluded):
- **Individual auditor: max 1 term of 5 consecutive years.**
- **Audit firm: max 2 terms of 5 consecutive years (10 years).**
- **Cooling-off: 5 years** before re-appointment in same company.
- Anti-avoidance: firms with **common partners** barred; **same network/brand** aggregated; **past tenure counted** (transitional). Members may voluntarily resolve to rotate the **engagement partner** even where firm rotation doesn't apply.

**Sec 139(8) — Casual vacancy** (death, resignation, disqualification mid-term):
- Non-Govt: **Board within 30 days**; if due to **resignation, also members' approval at GM within 3 months**. Appointee holds office **till next AGM only** (never fresh 5 years).
- Govt: **C&AG within 30 days**; failing which Board within next 30 days.
- NOT casual vacancy: term-expiry retirement; Sec 140(1) removal.

**Sec 141(3) — Disqualifications (financial-entanglement filter).** Not eligible:
- (a) body corporate (except LLP);
- (b) officer/employee of company;
- (c) partner/employee of an officer/employee;
- (d) person (or relative/partner) holding **any security or interest** in company/subsidiary/holding/associate — **relative may hold up to face value ₹1,00,000** (verify); or **indebted beyond ₹5,00,000**; or **guarantee for another's debt beyond ₹1,00,000**. Auditor/partner/firm = **zero tolerance** (even one share); cushion is only for a relative, with a **corrective window (commonly 60 days, verify)** for a relative's excess. Indebtedness direction: auditor owing **to** the company (unpaid audit fees owed by company is a different issue).
- (e) business relationship with company;
- (f) relative is a **director or KMP**;
- (g) full-time employment elsewhere, or auditor holding **> 20 company audits** (ceiling; OPC/dormant/small/below-threshold private excluded; for a firm applied **per partner**, counted against signing partner);
- (h) convicted of fraud in preceding **10 years**;
- (i) renders prohibited services under Sec 144.
- **Sec 141(4):** disqualification incurred **after** appointment → **automatic vacation of office** → casual vacancy under 139(8). Work signed after the event is tainted.

**Sec 142 — Remuneration.** Fixed by **members in general meeting** (may delegate the fixing to Board); **Board fixes first auditor's** fee. **Includes** audit fee + expenses + facilities; **excludes** fees for other services (kept separately visible → foreshadows Sec 144).

**Sec 140 — Removal / resignation / special notice (hardest door):**
- **140(1) Removal before term:** **special resolution + prior Central Government approval**. Order: Board resolution → **ADT-2 to Central Government within 30 days** of Board resolution → CG approval → **special resolution within 60 days** of approval → **reasonable opportunity of being heard**. (CG approval comes BEFORE the special resolution.)
- **140(2)-(3) Resignation:** file **Form ADT-3** with company & Registrar (and C&AG for Govt cos) within **30 days**, stating **reasons and facts**; **penalty on the auditor** for default.
- **140(4) Not re-appointing retiring auditor:** appointing someone other than retiring auditor needs **special notice**; retiring auditor has **right of written representation, circulated/read at meeting**. **No CG approval.**
- **140(5) Tribunal removal:** **NCLT** (suo motu / CG / any person's application) may order change if auditor acted **fraudulently or colluded/abetted** fraud → **debarred 5 years** + **Sec 447** liability.

**Sec 143 — Powers & duties:**
- **143(1) Powers:** right of access **at all times** to books/accounts/vouchers **wherever kept**, incl. branches; require info/explanations. Proviso extends access to **subsidiaries/associates for consolidation**.
- **143(2)-(3) Report:** report to members whether financial statements give a **true and fair view**; **143(3)** must state: all info/explanations obtained; **proper books kept** + branch returns; accounts agree with books/returns; **AS compliance**; observations/qualifications with adverse effect; whether any **director disqualified under Sec 164(2)**; **adequacy & operating effectiveness of IFC**; **Rule 11** matters (pending litigation, foreseeable losses on long-term contracts, dividends, etc.). Three flavours: positive assurance / reason-if-negative / report-the-fact.
- **143(12) Fraud reporting:** on **reason to believe** (not proof) an officer/employee fraud against the company — **≥ ₹1 crore → Central Government** (two-step: forward to Board/Audit Committee for reply within ~45 days, then **Form ADT-4** to CG within ~15 days; Rule 13, verify); **< ₹1 crore → Board/Audit Committee** + disclosure in Board's report. Good-faith reporting = **statutory safe harbour, not breach of confidentiality**. Duty extends to **cost & secretarial auditors**.
- **143(5)-(7):** Govt cos — C&AG may direct manner, receive report, do **supplementary audit** and **test audit**.
- **143(9)-(10):** auditor must comply with **auditing standards (SAs)** prescribed by Central Government (recommended by NFRA/ICAI).
- **143(8) Branch audit:** branch audited by **principal auditor or a branch auditor** (foreign branch: local qualified person). Branch auditor sends report to principal auditor, who **deals with it as necessary**; principal auditor **not automatically liable** if handled properly (SA 600), but not a rubber stamp — single accountability stays with him.

**Sec 144 — Prohibited (non-audit) services.** Auditor (directly or indirectly, incl. via relative, associated entity, network/brand, partners/firm) must NOT render to company / its holding / subsidiary: **accounting & book-keeping; internal audit; design & implementation of financial information systems; actuarial; investment advisory/investment banking; outsourced financial services; management services; any other prescribed**. Closed list — **tax, company-law, MRLs, certain certification generally permitted** (verify). Rendering a prohibited service also disqualifies under **141(3)(i)**.

**Sec 145 — Signing.** Auditor (or authorised CA partner) **signs the report**; **qualifications/observations/comments having adverse effect must be read out at the AGM** and open to inspection (only the qualifications, not the whole clean report). Signature = personal accountability.

**Sec 146 — Attendance at AGM.** All notices/communications of general meetings sent to auditor; auditor must **attend** (personally or via representative qualified to be auditor) with **right to be heard**, unless **members** exempt him (not management).

**Sec 147 — Punishment (tiered):**
- Company/officers in default (contravene 139–146): fines (company); fine and/or imprisonment (officers).
- **Auditor** (contravenes 139, 143, 144, 145): **fine** (min, up to cap or 4× audit fee whichever lower); if **wilful & intended to deceive** → **imprisonment up to 1 year + higher fine + refund of audit fee + damages**.
- Firm with partners acting **fraudulently/colluding** → **joint & several** civil and criminal liability. Links to **Sec 245 class action**.

**Sec 148 — Cost audit.** For specified classes producing goods/providing services (regulated/strategic industries), CG may direct **cost records** + **cost audit** by a **Cost Accountant (ICMAI)**, **appointed by the Board**, **remuneration ratified by members**. Report to Board / CG. **Statutory auditor cannot be the cost auditor.** Same fraud-reporting duty (143(12)) applies. Forms: **CRA-2** (appointment intimation), **CRA-3** (report to company), **CRA-4** (company files with CG) — verify.

**Related SAs / sections:** Sec 132 (NFRA oversight, can debar); SA 200 / Code of Ethics (independence + 5 threats); SA 210 (engagement terms); SA 240 (fraud, dovetails 143(12)); SA 260/265 (communication with TCWG / control deficiencies); SA 600 (branch/other auditor, 143(8)); SA 700/705/706 (report format); SA 230 (documentation); Sec 164(2); Sec 245; CARO (expands 143(3) reporting); CA Act First Schedule Clause 8 (communicate with retiring auditor).

## Exam traps & must-remember
- First auditor holds office **till conclusion of 1st AGM** — NOT 5 years, NOT till next Board meeting.
- First auditor: Board **30 days**; only on Board failure do members get **90 days** at EGM (measured from registration). Don't merge.
- Casual-vacancy appointee holds office **till next AGM** — not a fresh 5 years.
- **Rotation: individual = 1 term (5 yr), firm = 2 terms (10 yr); cooling-off 5 yr.** Don't reverse; applies only to prescribed classes.
- **Private ≠ exempt from rotation** — borrowings/deposits can pull a small private company in.
- **Annual ratification is GONE** (2017 Amendment).
- Casual vacancy by **resignation** needs members' approval within **3 months**.
- **140(1) removal** = special resolution + CG approval (CG approval BEFORE special resolution). **140(4) non-re-appointment** = special notice + right of representation, NO CG approval. Most confused pair — match to **timing of event** (mid-term ouster vs term ending).
- **Qualified vs eligible** — shareholding CA is qualified but ineligible.
- Thresholds: relative security **₹1,00,000** (auditor himself **zero**); indebtedness **₹5,00,000**; guarantee **₹1,00,000**; ceiling **20 companies** (OPC/dormant/small/below-threshold private excluded — net out before concluding breach). Indebtedness is auditor owing **to** company.
- **141(4)**: disqualification after appointment = **automatic vacation** (not "should resign"); work after event tainted.
- **Sec 144** is a bright-line list with directly/indirectly reach — small fee / different department / sister firm is no defence; but it's a **closed list** (tax & company-law generally permitted).
- Fraud: **≥ ₹1 cr → Central Government** (via Board/AC then ADT-4); **< ₹1 cr → Board/AC** + Board's report. Standard = **"reason to believe,"** waiting for proof breaches the duty.
- Only **qualifications** read at AGM (145); only **members** may exempt auditor from AGM (146).
- **Sec 147**: fine only (non-wilful) vs fine + jail + refund + damages (wilful to deceive) — classify state of mind first.
- **Cost auditor ≠ statutory auditor**; Cost Accountant (ICMAI), Board-appointed, fee ratified by members (reverse emphasis).
- Branch auditor reports **up** to principal auditor; principal not auto-liable but not a rubber stamp.
- **Only members appoint the subsequent auditor.** Board only for first auditor, casual vacancy (resignation caveat) and cost auditor.

## One-line recall
- The watched must never control the watchman — that is all of Sec 139–148.
- Members hire and pay; Board only bridges gaps; the State (CG) must bless a removal.
- Rotation: individual 1×5 yr, firm 2×5 yr, cool off 5 yr — kills familiarity by the calendar.
- 141 quarantines the conflict (auditor zero shares, relative ≤ ₹1L, debt ≤ ₹5L, ≤ 20 audits); 144 cuts the silence-bribe.
- Mid-term ouster needs Central Government (140(1)); merely not re-appointing needs only special notice (140(4)).
- Fraud ≥ ₹1 cr → CG via ADT-4; < ₹1 cr → Board/AC; standard is "reason to believe," not proof. No safeguard has teeth without Sec 147.
