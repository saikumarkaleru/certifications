# Chapter 10 — Audit Documentation & Quality Control (Revision Note)

## Snapshot
Four pillars answering "who audits the auditor?":
- SA 230 — Audit Documentation (engagement): make invisible work visible/provable.
- SA 220 — Quality Control for an Audit of FS (engagement): partner responsible for quality on *this* audit.
- SQC 1 — Quality Control for Firms (firm level): system giving *reasonable* assurance across *all* engagements (six elements).
- EQCR — Engagement Quality Control Review: independent objective review of significant judgements before the report is dated.

Numbers from the **date of the auditor's report**: assembly ≤ 60 days; retention ≥ 7 years.

## Core concepts
- **Documentation = the evidence; quality control = the process the evidence must show was good.** One proves *that* you worked; the other proves the work was worth trusting.
- Documentation is a *contemporaneous act* (a verb), not filing after the audit; the audit file is the residue.
- Two levels of QC because risks differ: an engagement can fail (SA 220) or a firm can systematically fail (SQC 1). One level cannot fix the other.
- EQCR exists to break **self-review bias** — the partner who made a tough call is worst-placed to spot its flaw.
- "Prove it" matrix: *that* work done → SA 230; *this* engagement good → SA 220; *every* engagement good → SQC 1; *high-stakes* opinion independently checked → EQCR.

## Key provisions / SAs

### SA 230 — Audit Documentation
- **Definition:** record of (a) audit procedures performed, (b) relevant audit evidence obtained, (c) conclusions reached. Old term "working papers"; whole = **audit file** (one/more folders or storage media, **physical or electronic**, for a *specific* engagement).
- **Benchmark of "enough":** documentation sufficient to enable an **experienced auditor, having no previous connection with the audit, to understand**:
  (a) **nature, timing and extent** of procedures performed;
  (b) **results** of procedures and audit evidence obtained;
  (c) **significant matters** arising, **conclusions** reached, and **significant professional judgements** made.
- **"Experienced auditor"** = individual (internal or external) with **practical audit experience** and **reasonable understanding** of audit processes, the SAs and legal/regulatory requirements, the entity's business environment, and industry-specific reporting issues. Skilled but disconnected → file supplies what is *specific to this engagement*, not generic knowledge.
- **Identifiability rule — record:** who **performed** the work + **date completed**; who **reviewed** + **date** + **extent** of review.
- **Identifying characteristics** of items tested must be recorded (e.g., PO numbers + range; systematic sample source, starting point, interval; inquiry date/name/designation; observation process/date/person). Enables re-performance/trace-back.
- **Significant matters:** document discussions with management/TCWG/others incl. *when* and *with whom*. Examples: matters giving rise to significant risks; results indicating possible material misstatement or need to revise risk assessment; significant difficulty applying procedures; findings that could lead to modified opinion or EOM.
- **Oral explanations** by the auditor alone are **not** adequate support — but **may** clarify/explain info already in the documentation. Cannot *substitute*, only *illuminate*.
- **Departure from a *relevant* SA requirement** (exceptional circumstances): document **how alternative procedures achieve the aim** of that requirement **and reasons for departure**. A *conditional* requirement whose condition is absent is simply inapplicable — no "departure" documentation.
- **Matters arising *after* the report date** (new/additional procedures or new conclusions): document circumstances, new procedures/evidence/conclusions, and **when and by whom** file changes were made and reviewed. Anti-backdating. Links to SA 560.
- **Assembly of final file:** on a timely basis after report date; **ordinarily not more than 60 days** (per SQC 1). Administrative only.
- **Permissible in assembly window:** delete/discard superseded documentation; sort, collate, cross-reference; sign off assembly completion checklists; document evidence obtained, discussed and agreed with team **before the report date**. **Not permissible:** new procedures, new conclusions, fresh evidence on unresolved matters. Bright line = **report date**, not the 60-day date.
- After assembly, **must not delete/discard** documentation before retention period ends.
- **Retention:** ordinarily **no shorter than 7 years** from the date of the auditor's report (or later, the group auditor's report date).
- **Ownership:** working papers are the **property of the auditor**, not the client (even though client pays fee). Auditor may share portions/extracts at discretion (courtesy, not a right). Owes **confidentiality** + **safe custody**.
- **Form/contents/extent** driven by professional judgement: size/complexity of entity, nature of procedures, identified RoMM, significance of evidence, nature/extent of exceptions, need to document a conclusion not readily determinable. Meet the benchmark — no more, no less.
- **Need NOT document:** every matter/judgement (only significant ones); need not retain superseded drafts, preliminary/incomplete notes, duplicates, copies corrected for typos.

### SA 220 — Quality Control for an Audit of FS (Engagement)
- Sits *inside* SQC 1. **Engagement partner responsible for overall quality** and must take the lead.
- **Reliance principle:** partner generally **entitled to rely** on the firm's SQC 1 system **unless information indicates otherwise**.
Partner's responsibilities:
- **Leadership** on quality (not delegated away).
- **Ethics incl. independence** — remain alert throughout; form a conclusion on independence (threats, safeguards, firm info).
- **Acceptance/continuance** — satisfied appropriate procedures followed and conclusions appropriate; if info would have caused firm to decline, communicate promptly to firm.
- **Assignment of team** — team (incl. auditor's experts not on team) collectively has **competence and capabilities**.
- **Engagement performance** — direction, supervision, review; **more experienced review less experienced**. Partner need not review all documentation but reviews significant judgements, significant risks and other important matters on a timely basis.
- **Consultation** on difficult/contentious matters — happened, **agreed** with party consulted, and **implemented** (two-part test).
- **EQCR** — for listed entities (+ firm-decided); discuss significant matters with reviewer; **do not date report until EQCR complete**.
- **Differences of opinion** — resolve per firm policy; **report not dated until resolved**.
- **Monitoring** — consider firm's monitoring results/deficiencies and effect on this engagement.
- **Documentation** — ethics issues + resolution; independence conclusion; acceptance/continuance conclusions; nature/scope/conclusions of consultations.

### SQC 1 — Quality Control for Firms
Applies to firms performing audits, reviews of historical financial info, other assurance & related services. Objective: system of QC giving **reasonable assurance** that (a) firm/personnel comply with standards + legal/regulatory requirements, and (b) reports issued are appropriate. Reasonable, **not absolute** (mirrors SA 200 inherent limitations).
**Six elements:**
1. **Leadership responsibilities** (tone at the top) — commercial considerations must **not override** quality of work.
2. **Ethical requirements** (integrity, objectivity, professional competence, confidentiality, professional behaviour) incl. **independence** — obtain **written independence confirmation at least annually** from relevant personnel; address **familiarity threats** from long association via safeguards incl. **rotation**.
3. **Acceptance & continuance** of client relationships and specific engagements.
4. **Human resources** — recruitment, competence, career development, capabilities, performance evaluation, assignment of teams.
5. **Engagement performance** — houses documentation, supervision, review, consultation, EQCR, differences of opinion, **assembly (≤60 days)**, **retention (≥7 years)**, and custody attributes: **confidentiality, safe custody, integrity, accessibility, retrievability**.
6. **Monitoring** — ongoing evaluation of the system incl. **cyclical inspection of at least one completed engagement per engagement partner**. Deficiencies **evaluated** (isolated vs systemic/repetitive/significant) and **communicated** to relevant partners with remedial recommendations. Includes **complaints and allegations** channel.
- Firm must also **document the system itself** (policies/procedures + evidence of operation of each element).

### EQCR
- **What:** objective evaluation, by a reviewer **not part of the engagement team**, of significant judgements and conclusions in formulating the report. Reviewer = partner, other firm person, suitably qualified external person, or team, with sufficient experience/authority and required objectivity.
- **When mandatory:** all audits of **listed entities**; plus other engagements meeting firm's criteria (high public interest, unusual risk/nature/circumstances).
- **Reviewer evaluates:** significant judgements; conclusions (esp. appropriateness of report); significant matters discussed with partner; review of FS and proposed report; selected working papers on significant judgements; conclusion on independence.
- **Objectivity safeguards:** not selected by engagement partner in a way that compromises objectivity; does not participate in the engagement during the period under review; does **not make decisions** for the team; partner may consult reviewer but consultation must **not compromise** eligibility.
- **Timing (key control):** engagement partner **must not date the report until EQCR is complete**.
- EQCR is a check on, **not a replacement for**, the partner's own review; partner remains responsible for the opinion.

## Exam traps & must-remember
- **60 days (assembly) vs 7 years (retention)** — never confuse. Both run from the **report date**.
- Count everything from the **report date**, not year-end or destruction date.
- **Assembly = administrative** (collate/sort/discard superseded drafts). New procedures in the 60-day window are NOT assembly — trigger the after-report-date rule.
- Benchmark exact wording: **"experienced auditor, having no previous connection with the audit"** + all three limbs (nature/timing/extent + results + significant matters/conclusions/judgements). "Any reader" loses marks.
- Experienced auditor is **skilled, not ignorant** — over-documentation is a fault.
- **Oral explanation** can clarify, never substitute for documentation.
- "Document everything" is **FALSE** — only significant matters/judgements; superseded drafts need not be retained.
- **EQCR not for every audit** — only listed + firm-defined high-risk.
- EQCR timing rule **absolute** — report date cannot precede EQCR completion for a listed entity.
- **EQCR does not shift responsibility** — reviewer evaluates; partner remains responsible.
- **Consulting the intended reviewer** heavily on a significant judgement **disqualifies** them (recreates self-review bias).
- **SA 220 (engagement) vs SQC 1 (firm)** — firm policies (recruitment, monitoring, tone at top, retention policy) = SQC 1; this engagement/partner/team = SA 220.
- **Ownership** — working papers belong to the **auditor**, not the client, even though client pays.
- **Departure from an SA** allowed in exceptional circumstances *if documented* (how alternative met aim + reasons); undocumented departure is the sin. Conditional requirement whose condition is absent = inapplicable, not a departure.
- **Monitoring frequency:** at least one completed engagement per engagement partner, cyclical.
- **A monitoring deficiency ≠ a wrong report** — evaluate isolated vs systemic first.
- SQC 1 gives **reasonable, not absolute** assurance — an occasional deficient engagement doesn't prove system failure.
- Independence confirmations **at least annually** — not "whenever convenient".

## One-line recall
- SA 230: record procedures + evidence + conclusions so an **experienced auditor with no previous connection** understands nature/timing/extent, results, and significant matters/judgements.
- SA 220: **engagement partner responsible** for quality — leadership, independence conclusion, competent team, supervision/review, consultation (agreed + implemented), EQCR, resolve differences before dating.
- SQC 1: six elements — **Leadership, Ethics/Independence, Acceptance & Continuance, HR, Engagement Performance, Monitoring** — reasonable assurance across all engagements.
- Numbers from **report date**: assemble ≤ **60 days** (admin only); retain ≥ **7 years** (confidentiality, safe custody, integrity, accessibility, retrievability); annual independence confirmations; monitoring ≥ 1 completed engagement per partner.
- EQCR: independent reviewer (not on team) for **listed** + high-risk; report **not dated until EQCR complete**; reviewer evaluates, partner decides.
- Working papers are the **auditor's property**; post-report changes documented in the open, never silently rewritten.
