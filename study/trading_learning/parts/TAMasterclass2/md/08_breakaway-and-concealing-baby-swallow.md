# Breakaway & Concealing Baby Swallow

If the Tri-Star and Unique Three River are the shy introverts of the candlestick world, the **Breakaway** and the **Concealing Baby Swallow** are its exotic five- and four-candle specimens — patterns most traders have read about but few have knowingly traded. They belong to a family of *multi-candle continuation-and-reversal* signals that reward patience: you cannot see them forming until the whole sequence is nearly complete. That is precisely why they are worth learning. They force you to think in *sequences* — how a trend accelerates, exhausts, and turns — rather than reacting to a single bar.

Both are relatively rare on Indian charts, and both are easy to mislabel. This chapter defines each with precision, unpacks the crowd psychology, gives Indian worked examples in rupees, provides exact trade rules, adds option-chain confluence, and — as always in this book — is candid about how often they disappoint. The honest headline: treat these as *pattern-recognition education and confluence enhancers*, not as high-frequency bread-and-butter setups.

## The Breakaway — what it is and why it works

The **Breakaway** is a **five-candle** pattern that signals the *end of an accelerating move* — a reversal after a trend has gapped away and then run too far, too fast. There is a bullish version (at a bottom) and a bearish version (at a top).

### Bullish Breakaway (bottom reversal)

Anatomy, left to right:

- **Candle 1:** a long **bearish** candle, deep in a downtrend. The trend is strong and obvious.
- **Candle 2:** another **bearish** candle that **gaps down** away from candle 1. This gap is the "breakaway" — price accelerates lower, a sign of climactic, emotional selling.
- **Candle 3:** a small candle (either colour) continuing lower or drifting.
- **Candle 4:** another small **bearish** candle making a lower low. Candles 2-4 are a cluster stepping down, but with *shrinking momentum* — the body sizes and daily ranges contract even as price nudges to new lows.
- **Candle 5:** a long **bullish** candle that rallies strongly and closes **inside the gap** created between candles 1 and 2 — i.e., it retraces the whole accelerated leg but does *not* fully close the original gap.

Why it works: the pattern captures the full arc of a **blow-off**. The gap on candle 2 is the crowd panicking. Candles 3-4 show the selling losing steam — new lows, but feeble ones, on drying momentum. Candle 5 is the snap-back: buyers overwhelm the exhausted sellers and reclaim the entire accelerated move in a single session. The unfilled original gap (between 1 and 2) is deliberate — it marks the zone the reversal is aiming to fill next, and its survival tells you the first strong push has begun but isn't over.

### Bearish Breakaway (top reversal)

The mirror image: long bullish candle 1; gap-**up** bullish candle 2 (the breakaway gap, greed accelerating); small candles 3-4 grinding to marginal new highs on fading momentum; then a long **bearish** candle 5 that plunges back into the gap between candles 1 and 2. It marks the end of a buying climax.

The key discriminator versus a plain reversal: the Breakaway *requires the acceleration gap* and *requires the final candle to retrace deep into that gap zone without fully closing the first gap.* That is what distinguishes a Breakaway from a random three-black-crows-then-bounce.

## The Concealing Baby Swallow — what it is and why it works

The **Concealing Baby Swallow** is one of the rarest named candlestick patterns — a **four-candle bullish reversal** that appears at the bottom of a downtrend. Its poetic name refers to the way the later candles "swallow" and "conceal" the earlier ones. Its anatomy is demanding:

- **Candle 1:** a **bearish Marubozu** — a long down candle with essentially *no shadows* (open at the high, close at the low). Relentless selling, no buyer pushback at all.
- **Candle 2:** another **bearish Marubozu**, opening at or near candle 1's close and continuing straight down. Two black marubozus back-to-back: maximum, uninterrupted bearishness.
- **Candle 3:** a bearish candle that **gaps down** at the open (below candle 2's close) but then trades *up into candle 2's body*, leaving a **long upper shadow**. Despite closing lower, the session saw buyers rip price up intraday for the first time — a crack in the selling.
- **Candle 4:** a **large bearish candle** that opens *above* candle 3's high and closes *below* candle 3's low — a big black candle that completely **engulfs (conceals) candle 3**.

Here is the counter-intuitive part that trips everyone up: **the fourth candle is bearish, yet the pattern is bullish.** How? Read the flow. After two remorseless marubozus, candle 3 shows buyers finally fighting back intraday (the long upper shadow inside candle 2). Candle 4 then *gaps up* to open — meaning the market opened well above the prior close, buyers stepped in at the bell — before sellers dragged it down to engulf candle 3. The *gap-up open on candle 4* and the intraday buying on candle 3 reveal that demand is entering even though the closes are still red. It is a bottoming pattern hiding inside a sequence of bearish candles — capitulation reaching its final, disorderly stage. Confirmation is essential: you need a bullish follow-through candle after candle 4 before trusting it.

Both patterns share a theme with the previous chapter: **reversals as processes**. The Breakaway shows momentum *decaying* across a gapped run; the Concealing Baby Swallow shows demand *sneaking in* under a wall of bearish candles. Neither is obvious in real time, which is exactly why they are worth training your eye on.

## Mechanics and settings

No indicators — pure price action — but codifying the conditions keeps your reading honest.

**Bullish Breakaway checklist:**

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Prior trend | Clear, established downtrend |
| Candle 1 | Long bearish body |
| Candle 2 | Bearish; **gaps down** from C1 (the acceleration gap) |
| Candles 3-4 | Small bodies stepping to lower lows on **contracting** range/momentum |
| Candle 5 | Long bullish candle closing **inside the C1-C2 gap**, not fully filling it |
| Confirmation | Next candle closes above C5's high |

**Concealing Baby Swallow checklist:**

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Prior trend | Established downtrend |
| Candle 1 | Bearish Marubozu (no/negligible shadows) |
| Candle 2 | Bearish Marubozu, continuing lower |
| Candle 3 | Gaps down, trades up into C2's body → **long upper shadow**, closes bearish |
| Candle 4 | Opens above C3's high, closes below C3's low → **engulfs C3** |
| Confirmation | A following **bullish** candle (must-have) |

**Screening note.** Both are hard to encode cleanly in Chartink because of the gap and marubozu conditions and the multi-candle sequence. In practice you screen loosely — e.g., "downtrend + gap-down cluster + a large bullish reversal candle" for the Breakaway — and confirm the exact anatomy by eye. Do not expect a reliable automated scanner to hand you textbook specimens; these are eyeball patterns.

## Worked India example — bullish Breakaway (reconstruction)

*Approximate reconstruction; verify on charts before trading.*

Consider **Reliance Industries** sliding from ~₹1,320 to ~₹1,210 over three weeks on sector weakness.

- **Candle 1:** long red day, ₹1,238 → ₹1,218. Trend firmly down.
- **Candle 2:** poor global cues; gaps down to open ₹1,209 (below C1's ₹1,218 close — **acceleration gap**), closes ₹1,201. Panic selling.
- **Candle 3:** small red, ₹1,202 → ₹1,197. Momentum fading.
- **Candle 4:** small red, marginal new low ₹1,193, closes ₹1,196. New low, but a feeble one — the range has shrunk from C1's ₹20 to ~₹5.
- **Candle 5:** buyers arrive; a long green candle ₹1,197 → ₹1,214, closing **inside the C1-C2 gap** (₹1,209-₹1,218) but not above ₹1,218. The accelerated leg is reclaimed in one day.

That is a bullish Breakaway around ₹1,193-1,214.

**The trade.** Wait for confirmation: a candle closing above C5's high (₹1,214).

- **Entry:** buy on close above ₹1,214, say ₹1,215.
- **Stop:** below the pattern low ₹1,193 → place at ₹1,190. Risk ~₹25.
- **Target 1:** the top of the original gap / prior support ₹1,238 (~₹23, ~0.9R).
- **Target 2:** the swing origin ₹1,265-1,275 (~₹55, ~2.2R).

Trail under higher swing lows once ₹1,238 is cleared. Note the modest R:R to target 1 — Breakaways often need target 2 to justify the wide stop, so either size for target 2 or use a call spread to improve the payoff.

## Worked India example — Concealing Baby Swallow (reconstruction)

Take **Bank Nifty** in a sharp two-week drop from ~49,800 to ~48,000. *(Reconstruction — confirm on charts.)*

- **Candle 1:** bearish marubozu, 48,900 → 48,300, opens at high, closes at low, negligible shadows. Relentless.
- **Candle 2:** bearish marubozu, 48,290 → 47,720. Two black marubozus — total control by bears.
- **Candle 3:** gaps down to open 47,650, sellers press to 47,500, but buyers rip it back up to 48,050 intraday before it closes 47,780 — a long **upper shadow** poking into candle 2's body. First real buying.
- **Candle 4:** opens 48,120 (a **gap up**, above C3's 48,050 high), sellers fade it to close 47,400 — a big red candle **engulfing** candle 3.

Despite three-and-a-half bearish-looking candles, the gap-up open on candle 4 plus the intraday buying on candle 3 mark a Concealing Baby Swallow bottom near 47,400-48,050.

**The trade.** Confirmation is mandatory: wait for a bullish candle after candle 4.

- **Entry:** the next session opens 47,500 and rallies; buy on a break above candle 4's midpoint or C3's high (48,050), say 48,100.
- **Stop:** below the pattern low (candle 4 / candle 3 low) 47,400 → place at 47,300. Risk ~800 points — wide, so trade via index options (a call debit spread) to cap rupee risk.
- **Target 1:** 48,700 (prior support, ~600 pts).
- **Target 2:** 49,300-49,500 (gap-fill / swing origin, ~1,300 pts).

## How to trade them — shared framework

1. **Confirm the prior trend.** Both are reversals; without a real trend they are meaningless.
2. **Wait for the confirmation candle — non-negotiable.** The Concealing Baby Swallow *ends on a bearish candle*; entering before follow-through is reckless. The Breakaway ends on a bullish candle but still deserves a close above its high.
3. **Stop below the pattern low** (bullish versions). If price revisits it, the sequence has failed — exit, no averaging.
4. **Respect the wide stops.** Multi-candle patterns span several sessions and often carry large entry-to-stop distances. Use options or reduced size so one failure is ≤1R.
5. **Targets by structure.** Original gap tops, prior supports, swing origins. Because R:R to the first target is frequently thin, plan to hold a runner to the second target or use spreads.

## Confluence — including option-chain reads

**Location.** A bullish Breakaway or Concealing Baby Swallow that completes *at* a major support — 200-DMA, a round index level (Bank Nifty 47,000/48,000), a prior demand zone — is far more trustworthy than one in open air.

**Momentum divergence.** Look for **bullish RSI/MACD divergence**: price grinds to the pattern low (candle 4 of the Breakaway, candle 2-4 lows of the Swallow) while the oscillator makes a higher low. Confirms the "momentum decaying" thesis.

**Volume.** For the Breakaway, ideal volume *expands* on the acceleration gap (candle 2), *contracts* through the feeble candles 3-4, then *surges* on the reversal candle 5. For the Concealing Baby Swallow, a **climactic volume spike** on the marubozus followed by heavy two-way volume on candles 3-4 signals capitulation.

**Option chain (indices / F&O names).** Around these bottoms:

- **Put writers returning at the low.** OI building at the nearest support Put strike (e.g., Bank Nifty 47,000 PE) as the pattern completes = smart money marking a floor.
- **PCR turning up from a fearful extreme** as candle 5 / the confirmation candle prints.
- **India VIX spiking on the capitulation candle and dropping on confirmation** — fear draining out is textbook at exhaustion bottoms.
- **Put IV crush** after the flush — demand for downside protection fading.

For the bearish Breakaway at a top, invert: Call-writing building at resistance, PCR rolling over from greed, VIX ticking up off complacency.

## Pitfalls

**1. Forcing rare patterns.** These are genuinely uncommon. If you "see" a Concealing Baby Swallow weekly, your criteria are too loose. The marubozus, the gap-down-then-upper-shadow on candle 3, and the engulfing candle 4 are strict — honour them.

**2. Missing the gaps.** Both patterns hinge on gaps (the acceleration gap in the Breakaway; the gap-down on candle 3 and gap-up on candle 4 of the Swallow). On Indian single stocks intraday, clean gaps are scarce, so these live mostly on **daily charts** of gap-prone instruments.

**3. Fearing the bearish close.** The Concealing Baby Swallow's fourth candle is *red*, and beginners refuse to see a bullish signal in a big red candle. The bullishness is in the *gap-up open* and the intraday buying beneath — plus the mandatory confirmation. Read flow, not just colour.

**4. Skipping confirmation.** The single most common way to lose on both patterns. Wait for the follow-through candle every time.

**5. Thin R:R.** The wide stops mean the first target often barely covers 1R. Plan for the second target or use spreads; don't take these for a quick scalp.

**6. Wrong regime.** In a violent one-way liquidation (macro shock), exhaustion patterns get steamrolled. They work best in ordinary mean-reverting conditions, not in a crash still in free-fall.

**7. No large-sample edge.** Because they're rare, there is little statistical comfort. Use them as *confluence enhancers* on a location-based thesis, never as standalone triggers.

## Interview-ready summary

- **Breakaway** = **five-candle** reversal ending an *accelerating* move. Bullish version: long red C1, **gap-down** C2 (acceleration gap), small feeble C3-C4 to new lows on fading momentum, long green **C5 closing inside the C1-C2 gap** (without filling it). Bearish version is the mirror at a top. Signals a blow-off exhausting.
- **Concealing Baby Swallow** = **four-candle** bullish bottom: two **bearish marubozus**, then a bearish C3 that **gaps down but trades up into C2 (long upper shadow)**, then a large bearish C4 that **engulfs C3 after gapping up at the open.** Bullish *despite* red candles — demand shows in the gap-up open and intraday buying. **Confirmation candle is mandatory.**
- Both need a **prior trend**, a **confirmation candle**, and a **stop below the pattern low**; both carry **wide stops** (use options/spreads).
- **Confluence** — support/200-DMA, momentum divergence, capitulation volume, and Put-writing/PCR/VIX turning at the low — sharply raises reliability.
- Honest stance: **rare, gap-dependent, no standalone statistical edge.** Master them to read *sequences* of momentum decay and hidden accumulation, and deploy them to reinforce a location thesis — not as naked signals.
