# Swing Systems: Pullbacks, Breakouts & Bases

## Why this matters

Swing trading — holding 3 to 15 days — is the most *capital-and-life-friendly* style for most Indians: 20–30 minutes of chart work after 15:30, low cost drag, and setups that don't require staring at a screen. You already know patterns and indicators. The pro gap this closes is **structure and patience**: entering on confirmation instead of anticipation, placing stops under real structure instead of an arbitrary percentage, and sizing so an overnight gap can't wreck you. Retail swing traders lose by buying every dip (catching falling knives), chasing extended breakouts, and holding through earnings hoping. Swing trading rewards those who wait for the market to *prove* the setup and then get out of the way.

Three core swing setups, all traded **with the higher-timeframe trend** (daily chart, in the direction of the 50-DMA slope):

**1. Trend pullback.** In an established uptrend (higher highs/lows, price above rising 20/50-DMA), price pulls back to support — a moving average, a prior breakout level, or a Fibonacci retracement (38.2–61.8%) — then shows a **reversal confirmation** (bullish engulfing, hammer, or a close back above a short MA). Enter on the confirmation candle's close or a break of its high. You're buying a temporary discount in an ongoing trend, not calling a bottom.

**2. Breakout from a base.** Price consolidates in a tight range/rectangle for days-to-weeks (a "base"), volatility contracts, volume dries up — then breaks out on **expanding volume**. The base is accumulation; the breakout is demand overwhelming supply. Enter on the breakout *close* above resistance, ideally with volume ≥1.5× the 20-day average. Tight bases (small % range) give the cleanest, lowest-risk entries.

**3. Flag / consolidation continuation.** After a strong up-move (the "pole"), price drifts sideways or gently down in a small flag for a few days on declining volume, then resumes. Enter on the break of the flag's upper boundary. Measured target = pole height added to breakout point.

**Stops & targets:**
- **Stop under structure:** below the pullback swing low, below the base, or below the flag low — the level that, if broken, means the setup failed. Never a flat "8% stop" unrelated to the chart.
- **Target by measured move** (base depth or flag pole projected) **or ATR multiple** (e.g., 2–3× the 14-day ATR from entry). Use a **trailing stop** (below each new higher low, or under the 10/20-DMA) to ride extended winners.

**India-specific swing realities:**

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| Overnight/gap risk | full exposure to news, US/GIFT Nifty, sector events — size for it |
| Earnings | **never hold a full swing position through results** unless that's the thesis; gaps are brutal |
| Cost | delivery STT 0.1% buy + 0.1% sell (01-Apr-2026) + stamp + brokerage + GST — low per-trade drag over 8 days |
| Settlement | equity **T+1**; shares in demat (NSDL/CDSL) before you can sell |
| Tax | delivery = **STCG** (held ≤12 months) at applicable rate; report as capital gains, or as business income if that's your pattern — verify FY2026-27 treatment |
| MTF | Margin Trading Facility available for delivery if you want leverage — but interest cost + gap risk compounds |

## Worked example: a base-breakout swing over several days

Capital ₹5,00,000; risk **1.5% = ₹7,500/trade.** Stock trading in delivery (illustrative numbers).

A quality large-cap has based between **₹1,180–₹1,220** for 3 weeks; 50-DMA rising beneath at ₹1,150; volume contracting.

**Day 0 (entry trigger):** Daily candle **closes ₹1,232**, clearing the ₹1,220 base top, volume **1.8× the 20-day average** — valid breakout. **Buy 400 shares at ₹1,233** (T+1: shares in demat next day).
- **Stop:** below the base / breakout level, buffered — **₹1,196** (₹37 risk/share). Rupee risk = 37 × 400 = ₹14,800 → too big. **Cut size to 200 shares:** risk 37 × 200 = **₹7,400 ≈ 1.5%.** ✓ (Position sizing bows to the stop, always.)
- **Target 1:** measured move = base depth ₹40 → **₹1,272**; **Target 2:** ATR-based / trail.

**Day 2:** gaps to ₹1,255 on sector strength. Hold.
**Day 4:** touches **₹1,274.** Book **100 shares** (+₹4,100 gross), trail stop on the rest to **₹1,233 (breakeven).** Trade is now risk-free.
**Day 7:** rides to ₹1,298; trailing stop under the last higher-low at **₹1,270.**
**Day 9:** minor pullback hits ₹1,270 → exit remaining 100 (+₹3,700 gross). **Total gross ≈ ₹7,800.**

Costs (delivery): STT 0.1% each side on ~₹2.5 L turnover ≈ ₹250 buy + ₹255 sell, plus stamp (0.015% buy), brokerage/GST → all-in ≈ **₹650–750.** **Net ≈ ₹7,050 over 9 days**, risking ₹7,400 — a clean ~1:1-plus on risk with the runner covering costs many times over. Note the low cost drag vs the intraday chapters: **9% here vs 15%+ scalping.**

Had it instead closed back inside the base on Day 0 (a **failed breakout**), the stop at ₹1,196 caps the loss at ₹7,400 — planned, survivable.

## How pros do it / common mistakes

**Pros:**
- Trade **with the daily trend**, only the cleanest 1–2 setups a week — not every stock in the F&O list.
- Enter on **confirmation + volume**, never on anticipation of a breakout that hasn't happened.
- Stop under **structure**, size from the stop, and **check the earnings calendar** before entry — flat into results unless that's the trade.
- Scale out and **trail** to turn a few big winners into the month (swing P&L is fat-tailed — a handful of runners carry the year).

**Retail mistakes / red flags:**
- **Catching falling knives:** buying a downtrend pullback with no confirmation ("it's cheap now").
- Chasing an **extended** breakout 8% above the base — stop is now miles away, R:R ruined.
- Buying breakouts on **weak volume** (traps that fail).
- **Holding through earnings** and eating a 12% gap-down.
- Arbitrary % stops unrelated to the chart → stopped on normal noise or too wide.
- Turning a losing swing into a "long-term investment" — the swing version of style-drift.

## Checklist / drill

**Before entry:**
- [ ] Daily trend aligned (price above rising 20/50-DMA for longs)?
- [ ] Named setup: pullback / base-breakout / flag?
- [ ] **Confirmation** printed (reversal candle or breakout *close*), not anticipated?
- [ ] Volume confirms (breakout ≥1.5× avg; pullback drying up)?
- [ ] Stop under **structure**; rupee risk ≤1.5–2% of capital?
- [ ] Position size derived **from the stop**?
- [ ] **No earnings** before intended exit (checked the calendar)?
- [ ] Target (measured move / ATR) and trailing plan written?

**Drill:** Scan the daily charts of the Nifty 50 each weekend. Find **three** stocks in a tight base or clean pullback. Mark the exact trigger, stop, and target on each. Don't trade — just track for the next two weeks whether the breakout confirmed with volume or failed. After a month you'll recognise a *real* base-breakout on sight and stop chasing the fakes that empty retail accounts.
