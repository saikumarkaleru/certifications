# The Pre-Market Routine & Watchlist

## Why this matters — the pro vs retail gap this closes

The retail trader opens Kite at 9:14, sees Nifty is "up", buys whatever moved yesterday, and is stopped out by 9:35. The professional walked into 9:15 already knowing three things the amateur doesn't: (1) *why* the market is likely to open where it will, (2) a short list of 5-8 instruments with pre-drawn levels, and (3) the single rupee figure that ends his trading day if he loses it. The gap this chapter closes is **preparation as an edge**. Your options book taught you Greeks; your TA guide taught you patterns. Neither matters if you enter the day blind, oversized, and reacting. India's cash session is only 6 hours 15 minutes (9:15-15:30), and the first 15-45 minutes carry most of the day's directional information and most of the day's traps. A repeatable 30-minute morning routine — done *before* 9:15 — is the cheapest performance upgrade available to you, and unlike a strategy it cannot be arbitraged away.

## The essentials — the India pre-market stack

*(Rules and mechanics current as of July 2026 — verify on NSE/broker/SEBI, rules change.)*

**Session clock (IST):**

| Segment | Window |
|---|---|
| NSE pre-open (equity) | 09:00-09:15 (order collection 09:00-09:08, matching/price discovery 09:08-09:12) |
| Normal equity/F&O market | 09:15-15:30 |
| Closing session | 15:40-16:00 |
| GIFT Nifty (NSE IX, GIFT City) | ~06:30-15:40 and ~16:35-02:45 (nearly 21 hrs) |
| MCX (crude, gold, base metals) | 09:00-23:30 (23:55 on DST) |

**Global cues to read (in order of impact on the Nifty open):**

1. **GIFT Nifty** (replaced SGX Nifty in 2023) — the single best real-time proxy for the Nifty open. If GIFT Nifty trades ~100 pts above the previous Nifty futures close, expect a gap-up open of roughly that size. Read the *premium/discount to spot*, not just the number.
2. **US close (prior night):** S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow. Nasdaq matters most for Indian IT (TCS, Infosys, HCLTech).
3. **US futures + Asian markets live (this morning):** Nikkei, Hang Seng, Kospi, and S&P futures — these move GIFT Nifty in real time.
4. **Brent crude / WTI:** rising crude hurts OMCs (BPCL/HPCL/IOC), paints, aviation (IndiGo), tyres; helps ONGC/Oil India.
5. **USDINR:** a weaker rupee (say 87.20 → 87.60) is a tailwind for IT/pharma exporters, a headwind for importers.
6. **India VIX:** below ~12 = complacent/range day likely; above ~18 = expect wide ranges, cut size.
7. **FII/DII provisional flows** (previous day, ~17:30 NSE) and **FII index-futures long/short ratio** — persistent FII selling caps rallies.

**Event/results calendar to check every morning:** quarterly results (peak in Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct), RBI MPC dates, US Fed (FOMC) nights, US CPI/NFP, India CPI/IIP, F&O expiry (Nifty weekly Thursday, Bank Nifty monthly last Thursday — *verify current expiry days on NSE, they have changed repeatedly*), and index rebalancing. Never carry a fresh position into a result you didn't know was coming.

**Gap classification (open vs previous close):** flat < 0.3%, moderate gap 0.3-0.8%, large gap > 0.8%. Large gaps into empty space tend to *continue*; gaps back into the prior day's range tend to *fill*.

## Worked example — a Monday morning routine, 08:45-09:15

Assume Friday's Nifty spot closed **24,180**; Nifty futures **24,205**. It's 08:45 IST Monday.

- **GIFT Nifty** is at **24,320**, i.e. ~+115 over Friday futures. Signal: **gap-up ≈ +0.55%** (moderate-to-large).
- **US Friday:** Nasdaq +1.1%. **Asia now:** Nikkei +0.8%. **Brent** $79 (flat). **USDINR** 87.45 (rupee slightly weak → IT tailwind). **India VIX** 11.8 (low).
- **Calendar:** Reliance reports Q1 results *today after market*. No RBI/Fed. Thursday is weekly expiry.
- **Read:** Broad-based gap-up led by IT, low VIX = likely trend-up-then-grind day, but Reliance is an event-risk landmine into the close.

**Watchlist built (5 names + levels):**

| Instrument | Bias | Key level | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nifty (index) | Long above open | 24,320 open / 24,180 Fri close | Long only above 24,320 hold; invalidation 24,180 |
| Infosys | Long | 1,585 (Fri high) | Buy break-retest of 1,585, SL 1,566 |
| TCS | Long | 3,410 | Momentum continuation |
| Reliance | **AVOID intraday** | — | Result today; no fresh position |
| HDFC Bank | Neutral/laggard | 1,690 | Fade into 1,710, or short below 1,682 |

**Max risk for the day:** Capital ₹5,00,000. Daily stop = **1% = ₹5,000**. Per-trade risk = 0.4% = ₹2,000. On Infosys, entry 1,586, SL 1,566 = ₹20/share risk → max **100 shares** (₹2,000 ÷ ₹20). That single arithmetic step — *risk-first position sizing* — is what most retail traders never do. If two trades hit stops (−₹4,000), one more losing trade ends the day. That rule alone prevents the revenge-trading blowup.

## How pros do it / common mistakes

**How pros do it:** They separate *analysis* (done before 9:15) from *execution* (done after). They never trade the pre-open matching prints as if they're real liquidity. They wait out the first 5-15 minutes ("the 9:15 fakeout") on gap days rather than chasing the opening tick. They pre-commit levels in writing, so the decision is "did price reach my level?" not "do I feel like it?". They log the day's plan and grade themselves against it at 15:30.

**Classic retail errors & red flags:**
- Chasing the gap-up at 9:15:03 with no level and no stop.
- Confusing GIFT Nifty's *absolute* value with its premium — reading direction wrong.
- Trading into an event they didn't check (a result, RBI, US CPI night) and blaming "manipulation".
- No daily max-loss, so a −₹5,000 morning becomes a −₹40,000 afternoon via averaging down.
- A 40-name watchlist = a 0-name watchlist. You cannot watch 40 charts; you will trade the loudest, not the best.
- Ignoring costs: on intraday equity, STT is 0.025% on the sell side (from 01-Apr-2026) plus brokerage + exchange txn + 18% GST + stamp duty — verify the exact stack on your broker's contract note.

## Checklist / drill

**The 30-minute pre-market checklist (run 08:45-09:15 daily):**

1. [ ] GIFT Nifty level *and* premium/discount → expected gap %.
2. [ ] US close + Asia-live + S&P futures direction.
3. [ ] Brent, USDINR, India VIX noted.
4. [ ] FII/DII provisional (prev day) + FII futures long/short.
5. [ ] Events today: results / RBI / Fed / US data / expiry day?
6. [ ] Classify the likely open: flat / moderate gap / large gap; continuation vs fill.
7. [ ] Build watchlist: **max 5-8** names, each with bias + one key level + invalidation.
8. [ ] Write **daily max loss** (₹) and **per-trade risk** (₹); compute share/lot size from SL distance.
9. [ ] Decide what you will *not* trade today (event landmines).
10. [ ] Screenshot/save the plan. Grade against it at 15:30.

**Drill (2 weeks):** Every morning, write your gap prediction from GIFT Nifty at 09:00, then record the actual 09:15 open. Track your error in points. Within 10 sessions you'll internalize how premium translates to the open — a skill no indicator gives you.
