# Reading Market Regime: Trend, Range or Volatile

## Why this matters — the pro vs retail gap this closes

The single most expensive retail mistake is running the wrong strategy for the day's *regime*. A trend-following breakout system is a money-printer in a trending market and a paper-shredder in a choppy one — same rules, opposite outcomes, and the only variable that changed was the market's character. Retail traders don't even measure regime; they apply one style (usually breakout buying) in all conditions and then blame "the system" when 60% of days chop them to death.

The gap this closes: pros first ask *"what kind of market is this?"* and only then decide *how* to trade — or whether to trade at all. Nifty and Bank Nifty spend a large fraction of days in range/chop, where the highest-value skill is **doing nothing** or switching to mean-reversion. Reading regime tells you which of your edges is live today.

## The essentials — measuring regime in Indian markets

There are three regimes: **Trending** (directional, follow-through), **Ranging** (sideways, mean-reverting), and **Volatile/News-driven** (large two-way swings, gaps, low predictability).

**1. ADX (trend strength, not direction).**
- **ADX < 20:** no trend → **range regime.** Fade extremes, avoid breakouts.
- **ADX 20-25 and rising:** trend emerging.
- **ADX > 25:** **trending regime.** Breakouts and pullback-continuation work; mean-reversion is dangerous.
- ADX says *how strong*, +DI/−DI say *which way*.

**2. Moving-average slope & alignment.**
- 20/50-DMA **rising and price above** = uptrend; falling and below = downtrend.
- **Flat, intertwined MAs with price crossing back and forth** = range. On the Nifty daily, tightly coiled flat MAs are your "sit on your hands" signal.

**3. India VIX (the volatility regime gauge — this is the Indian trader's dashboard).**
- **VIX < 12-13:** calm, complacent — favours range/trend-continuation, tight ranges, theta.
- **VIX 13-18:** normal.
- **VIX > 18-20:** elevated — **volatile regime.** Expect large intraday swings, gaps, whippy stops; cut size.
- **VIX > 25-30:** fear/event mode (elections, budget, global shock). Directional bets are lotteries; size down hard.
- Rising VIX into an event (Budget, RBI policy, US Fed, election results) warns of a volatile regime *before* it hits.

**4. Market breadth (is the move broad or narrow?).**
- **Advance-Decline ratio** on NSE, and **% of Nifty 500 above their 50-DMA.** Broad participation (many stocks advancing) confirms a healthy trend; a Nifty up-move on narrow breadth (2-3 heavyweights dragging it) is fragile.
- Divergence — index new high but breadth deteriorating — flags an ending trend / coming volatility.

**5. Index bias for the day (the morning read).** Before 9:15, combine: overnight SGX/GIFT Nifty cue, US close, India VIX level and change, and yesterday's structure. Form a one-line bias: *"Gap-up open, VIX 11 and falling, ADX 28 up — trend-up day, buy pullbacks in leaders."* vs *"Flat open, VIX 14, ADX 16, MAs flat — range day, fade the extremes of yesterday's range, small size."*

**Regime → style map:**

| Regime | Signals | Trade this | Avoid this |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trending** | ADX > 25, MAs sloped & aligned, broad breadth, VIX calm | Breakouts, pullback-continuation, positional adds | Fading/mean-reversion |
| **Ranging** | ADX < 20, flat/tangled MAs, VIX low | Fade range extremes, buy support/sell resistance, reduce frequency | Breakout systems (fakeouts) |
| **Volatile** | VIX > 18-20, gaps, breadth divergence, event day | Small size, wider stops, wait for post-event clarity | Big directional bets, tight stops |

## Worked example — a Nifty regime decision

**Pre-market, a Wednesday.** You want to trade Nifty/Bank Nifty index bias and 2-3 stocks.

**Data:**
- GIFT Nifty points to a **flat-to-slightly-down** open.
- **India VIX = 21.5, up from 16** two days ago (an RBI policy is Friday).
- **Nifty daily ADX = 17** and drifting down; 20- and 50-DMA are **flat and intertwined** around 24,000; Nifty has oscillated 23,800-24,200 for two weeks.
- **Breadth:** yesterday's advance-decline was roughly even.

**Read:** ADX < 20 + flat MAs = **range**; but **VIX 21.5 and rising into an event = volatile overlay.** So: *range-bound but jumpy* — the worst environment for breakout trend systems and the best for getting chopped.

**Decision:**
- **Do NOT** take Nifty breakout trades above 24,200 or below 23,800 — high fakeout risk with rising VIX.
- If anything, **fade the extremes with small size**: e.g., near 23,820 support look for a reversal long targeting mid-range 24,000, stop 23,760. Half your normal size because VIX is elevated.
- **Reduce stock exposure**; skip high-beta names (PSU banks, metals) that will whip.
- **Plan to stand aside Thursday-Friday** around the RBI event; re-assess regime *after* the policy, when ADX/VIX resolve.

Net result: on a day retail loses money buying a false breakout, the regime read keeps you small or flat. *Not trading is a position.*

## How pros do it / common mistakes

**Pros:**
- **Check regime first, strategy second, every single morning.**
- **Cut size as VIX rises** — position size is inversely scaled to volatility.
- **Stand aside around known events** (Budget, RBI, Fed, election counting days, monthly expiry chop) unless the setup is specifically an event play.
- **Respect breadth** — they trust a trend only when many stocks confirm it.

**Retail errors / red flags:**
- Running the same breakout system on every day regardless of ADX/VIX.
- Buying breakouts on a low-ADX range day → serial fakeouts.
- Ignoring India VIX entirely, then getting stopped out by "random" spikes that were just an elevated-VIX regime.
- Full size into Budget day or RBI policy and getting gapped through the stop.
- Believing a Nifty new high made by 3 heavyweights while 300 stocks fall.

## Checklist / drill

**Morning regime-decision checklist (before 9:15):**
- [ ] India VIX level and 2-day change noted (calm / normal / elevated / fear).
- [ ] Nifty daily ADX read (< 20 range, > 25 trend) and +DI/−DI direction.
- [ ] 20/50-DMA slope & alignment (sloped = trend, flat/tangled = range).
- [ ] Breadth check (advance-decline, % above 50-DMA) — broad or narrow?
- [ ] Event calendar checked (RBI, Budget, Fed, expiry, election counts, big results).
- [ ] Overnight cue (GIFT Nifty, US close) → gap expectation.
- [ ] One-line index bias written: regime + what to trade + what to avoid + size adjustment.

**Drill:** For 15 trading days, log each morning: VIX, Nifty ADX, MA state, breadth, and your one-line regime call — then at close, mark whether the day was trend/range/volatile and whether your call was right. Within two weeks you'll predict the regime better than most retail traders ever do, and you'll see how often the correct answer was "range — trade small or not at all."
