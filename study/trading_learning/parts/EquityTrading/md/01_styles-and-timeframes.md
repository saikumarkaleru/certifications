# Trading Styles & Timeframes: Choosing Yours

## Why this matters

Retail traders lose money not because they can't read a chart — you already can — but because they trade a style that doesn't fit their capital, their clock, and their nervous system. A ₹50,000 account trying to scalp Bank Nifty is dead in a month; a working professional trying to intraday-trade with one eye on the screen and one on a Zoom call is a donor. The pro's first edge is *self-honesty about which game they can actually play well.* This chapter is about picking one game and staying in it. Style-drift — jumping timeframes mid-trade — is the single most common way a disciplined plan turns into a blown account.

## The essentials

Indian markets run **09:15–15:30 IST** (pre-open 09:00–09:15; F&O and equity same session). Your "style" is really a bundle of holding period, screen-time, capital, and cost tolerance. Here is the honest map (rules as of July 2026 — verify on NSE/broker, they change):

| Style | Holding | Screen time/day | Trades/mo | Min. realistic capital | Overnight risk | Cost sensitivity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scalping | seconds–minutes | 3–6 hrs, glued | 200–1000+ | ₹5–10 L (F&O) | none | brutal — churn kills you |
| Intraday | minutes–hours, sq-off by 15:30 | 2–4 hrs active | 20–60 | ₹1–3 L | none | high |
| BTST/ATST | 1–2 days | 30 min/day | 8–20 | ₹1 L+ | full gap risk | medium |
| Swing | 3–15 days | 20–30 min/day | 6–15 | ₹1 L+ | full gap risk | low |
| Positional | weeks–months | a few hrs/wk | 2–8 | ₹2 L+ | full + event | very low |

Key India-specific facts that shape the choice:

- **Costs scale with frequency.** Intraday equity STT is 0.025% on the sell side (from 01-Apr-2026); delivery is 0.1% buy *and* sell. Add brokerage, exchange txn, SEBI fee, stamp duty, and **18% GST on brokerage+txn**. A scalper crossing the spread 40 times a day is fighting a heavy cost drag; a swing trader holding 8 days barely feels it.
- **Overnight = gap risk.** BTST/swing/positional trades are exposed to news, US markets, and SGX Nifty/GIFT Nifty moves. Delivery settles **T+1** (T+0 optional beta on select stocks). You cannot sell what hasn't settled for intraday-style flips beyond the rules.
- **Tax follows style.** Intraday equity = **speculative business income**; F&O = **non-speculative business income**; delivery held and sold = **STCG/LTCG**. Speculative losses carry forward 4 years, non-speculative 8. Your style literally changes your ITR-3 treatment (FY2026-27).
- **Leverage is capped.** Peak-margin is fully enforced; no intraday leverage beyond broker limits. Scalping/intraday do NOT give you a magic multiplier anymore — that era ended.

## Worked example: the cost drag by style

Take a ₹2,00,000 account, ~₹1,00,000 deployed per position.

**Scalper** aiming for 0.15% net per trade, 15 trades/day, 20 days = 300 trades/month. Round-trip cost on a ₹1 L intraday equity position is roughly ₹30–45 (brokerage ₹20 flat both legs + STT ₹25 on sell of ₹1L @0.025% + txn + GST + stamp). Call it ₹40/trade × 300 = **₹12,000/month of pure cost** — 6% of the account gone before a single rupee of profit. To break even you must be right *and* big enough to overcome it. This is why 90%+ of scalpers bleed.

**Swing trader**, same account, 10 trades/month, holding 6–8 days. Delivery round-trip cost on ₹1 L is higher per trade (~₹250, mostly STT 0.1%+0.1% and stamp) but only 10 trades = **₹2,500/month**, 1.25% drag. A single clean 4% swing (₹4,000) covers 1.5 trades' worth of costs. The math *permits* profitability.

Same skill, same charts — the timeframe alone decides whether costs are survivable.

## How pros do it / common mistakes

**How pros do it:**
- They pick **one primary style** and one **backup** (e.g., intraday primary, swing backup for trending days), and they *never* let a losing intraday trade become a "positional investment." That conversion — moving the stop to "let's hold it" — is style-drift, and it's how a ₹3,000 intraday loss becomes a ₹40,000 overnight disaster.
- They match style to their **actual calendar**. If you have a day job, intraday is a trap; you'll take rushed entries and miss exits. Swing/positional is built for you — set-and-alert.
- They size for the **worst normal day**, not the best. A scalper's edge is thin and needs volume + tight spreads (Nifty, Bank Nifty, top-50 stocks) — not illiquid mid-caps where the spread eats the edge.

**Classic retail errors & red flags:**
- **Timeframe hopping** — entering intraday, then holding overnight when it goes against you. #1 account killer.
- Trading a style your capital can't support (scalping a small account into oblivion via costs).
- Believing "more trades = more money." Frequency multiplies *costs and mistakes*, not edge.
- Choosing based on YouTube dopamine (scalping looks exciting) rather than fit.
- Ignoring the tax/audit implications until March.

## Checklist / drill

**Self-assessment — answer honestly, score yourself:**

1. **Screen time:** Can I watch live charts undistracted for 2+ hours in the 09:15–11:00 window? (No → rule out scalping/intraday.)
2. **Capital:** Is my risk capital ≥ ₹2 L and can I lose 20% of it without lifestyle impact? (No → swing/positional, small size.)
3. **Temperament:** Do rapid-fire decisions energize or panic me? Can I click a stop-loss without hesitation?
4. **Cost tolerance:** Have I calculated my all-in round-trip cost on my typical position, in rupees? (If not, do it before your next trade.)
5. **Overnight nerves:** Can I sleep holding a position through a possible 2% gap-down? (No → intraday only.)
6. **Calendar:** Does my job/life actually permit mid-day exits?

**Drill:** For the next 20 trading days, write your intended style on a card and tape it to your monitor. Log every trade against it. At month-end, count how many trades *drifted* (intraday held overnight, swing exited in an hour out of boredom). If drift > 15%, you don't have a style yet — you have impulses. Fix that before adding capital.
