## Suggested study order

1. **Universal core first** (asked in nearly every finance interview): #1 Accounting → #2 Corporate Finance → #8 Economics.
2. **Then your target cluster:**
   - Markets/research (ER, IB, IR): #3 Valuation → #4 Modeling → #5 Equity.
   - Credit/risk (Rating, Credit, Risk, Treasury): #6 Credit & Fixed Income → #7 Derivatives/Risk.
   - Ops/advisory (Ops, Fund Acct, KYC, Wealth): #9 Ops/Compliance/Wealth → #5 Equity.
3. Finish each guide by drilling its Q&A section out loud, then revisit the `trap` callouts the day before an interview.

_Regulatory figures/rates are kept conceptual ("typically/around") — verify current specific numbers (repo rate, TER caps, thresholds) before an interview, as these change._
