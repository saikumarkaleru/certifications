# Business Development / Sales Resumes — Saikumar Kaleru

Three tailored BD/sales resume variants for a career pivot from finance/tech into sales.
Editable sources in `source/*.html`; rendered PDFs alongside.

> **Honesty note:** These resumes claim **no prior sales experience**. They reframe genuine
> transferable strengths (analytical rigor, client-ready communication, financial-product domain
> knowledge, tech fluency) and present self-directed BD upskilling. NISM **Investment Adviser
> (Series X-A)** is shown as *pursuing* — pass it to strengthen the financial-services variant.

## The 3 variants — when to use each

| Resume | Best for | Lead angle |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Business_Development_Financial_Services** | Wealth/RM, AMC & broking sales, BFSI BD, financial-product sales | MBA Finance + NISM + D.E. Shaw markets exposure → sells financial products credibly. **Your strongest fit.** |
| **Fintech_SaaS_Sales** | SDR/BDR & inside sales at fintechs / B2B SaaS | CS background + finance domain → sells technical/API products; outbound + CRM + SaaS metrics |
| **Business_Development_Executive_General** | Generalist BDE / inside sales, any industry | Business acumen, market research, communication, work ethic — widest net |

## Tips
- Tailor the **role-tag** and **summary** to each job's exact title before applying.
- Keep it to one page (current length).
- If you build the BD portfolio projects (see `../README.md`), swap the "Projects & Initiatives"
  bullets to cite those concrete projects — that's the single biggest credibility boost for a
  no-experience sales applicant.
- Once you clear NISM Series X-A, add its registration number and remove "pursuing".
