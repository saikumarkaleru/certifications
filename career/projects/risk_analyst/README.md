# Risk Analyst (Market & Credit) — Projects

Two core risk projects, kept simple and fully explainable, each with a
plain-English **study guide** for interview prep.

| Project | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| [`1_value_at_risk`](1_value_at_risk) | Portfolio 1-day 95%/99% VaR by historical and parametric methods, plus Expected Shortfall, in % and $. |
| [`2_credit_risk_scoring`](2_credit_risk_scoring) | Altman Z-score (five ratios) classifying companies into safe / grey / distress zones. |

Each is one readable `main.py` with Excel/chart output, plus `STUDY_GUIDE.md`
(pitch, historical-vs-parametric VaR, Q&A). Run: `python main.py`.
