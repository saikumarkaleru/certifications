# Business Development / Sales — Curated Learning Resources

The best **free-first** resources to learn BD/sales from scratch, plus a paced study plan.
Pair these with the 5 study guides in this folder (`01`–`05`). Prioritise the **free** items first —
they're more than enough to become job-ready.

---

## 📚 Books (read in this order)

| Book | Author | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| **To Sell Is Human** | Daniel Pink | Best mindset primer — sales is influence, not sleaze. Start here. |
| **SPIN Selling** | Neil Rackham | The research-backed questioning method (Situation/Problem/Implication/Need-payoff). Core skill. |
| **Fanatical Prospecting** | Jeb Blount | The bible of filling your pipeline — cold calling, email, cadences. |
| **New Sales. Simplified.** | Mike Weinberg | Simple, practical outbound playbook for new reps. |
| **The Challenger Sale** | Dixon & Adamson | Teach-tailor-take-control; how top B2B reps actually win. |
| **Never Split the Difference** | Chris Voss | Negotiation via tactical empathy (ex-FBI). For the closing/negotiation guide. |
| **Predictable Revenue** | Aaron Ross | The SDR/AE model that built Salesforce's outbound engine (SaaS). |

*Access:* many are on Kindle/Audible; check your local library / Blinkist-style summaries for a fast first pass.

  "Relationship Manager", "Associate – Sales/Partnerships".

## 🗓️ 4–6 week self-study plan

**Week 1 — Foundations.** Read *To Sell Is Human*; study Guide 01 (Fundamentals); start HubSpot *Sales* cert.
**Week 2 — Prospecting.** Guide 02; read *Fanatical Prospecting* (skim); write your own cold email + call script; finish HubSpot *Inbound Sales*.
**Week 3 — Deal cycle.** Guide 03; *SPIN Selling*; practice discovery questions & objection responses out loud; start Salesforce Trailhead.
**Week 4 — Metrics & tools.** Guide 04; build a mock pipeline in HubSpot free CRM; learn CAC/LTV/ARR cold (your quant edge); one negotiation course.
**Week 5 — Your edge.** Guide 05 (Financial-Services & Fintech Sales); prepare 2 pitches (a wealth/MF pitch, a fintech B2B pitch); map target employers.
**Week 6 — Interview & apply.** Rehearse "why sales after MBA Finance", "sell me this pen/mutual fund/payment gateway"; polish the right resume variant; start applying and doing mock calls.

> Consistency beats intensity: 60–90 focused minutes/day + speaking answers **out loud** will make you
> job-ready faster than passive reading.
