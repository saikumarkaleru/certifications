interview-prep guides. Editable sources in `source/*.html`; rendered PDFs alongside.
See **[RESOURCES.md](RESOURCES.md)** for books, free courses, YouTube, tools, and a 4–6 week plan.

## How to use
- Read guides **in order** (1 → 5); each has concepts, frameworks, scripts, worked examples,
  a Q&A bank, and "common trap" callouts.
- Guide **05** is your differentiator — it maps your finance + NISM background to sales roles you
  can realistically win.
- Combine with the paced plan and free certs in **RESOURCES.md** (HubSpot Academy + Salesforce
  Trailhead give you résumé-worthy certificates).
- Practice answers **out loud** — sales is a spoken skill.

_India/fintech context throughout (SEBI/NISM, INR, target employers). Specific tool prices/limits
change — verify current details before relying on them._
